This method focuses on recognizing that each student learns at their own pace and possesses unique strengths and areas for improvement. Rather than a one-size-fits-all curriculum, personalized learning employs flexible pacing, varied instructional methods, and customized content. The goal is to provide students with agency over their learning journey, offering them choices in how they engage with material and demonstrate their understanding.

For instance, a student struggling with fractions might receive additional practice problems and visual aids, while another student who has mastered the concept might be offered more challenging applications or a related advanced topic. This approach is commonly employed in educational settings, from K-12 classrooms to higher education and professional development programs.
