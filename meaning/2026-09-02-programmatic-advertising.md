This method replaces traditional manual processes of ad negotiation and placement with technology-driven automation. It leverages data to identify target audiences and bid for ad impressions in real-time, often within milliseconds. Instead of human buyers and sellers making direct deals, software determines which ads appear where, based on predefined parameters such as audience demographics, location, and browsing history.

The process frequently involves real-time bidding (RTB) within an ad exchange, where advertisers compete instantaneously for an ad impression as a user loads a webpage or application. This allows for highly targeted ad delivery, ensuring that advertisements are shown to the most relevant users at the optimal moment. For example, a sports brand might use programmatic advertising to automatically display its new running shoe ad to users who have recently searched for athletic footwear or visited fitness-related websites.

This approach is widely adopted across the digital marketing and advertising technology industries, particularly for display, video, mobile, and native advertising campaigns, enhancing efficiency and precision in media buying.
