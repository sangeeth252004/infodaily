This process involves building a model that identifies patterns and relationships within existing datasets. By analyzing these patterns, the model can then be applied to new, unseen data to estimate what is likely to happen. The core idea is to learn from the past to make informed guesses about the future.

For instance, a company might use predictive modeling to forecast which customers are most likely to stop using their service.

This approach is widely applied in fields such as finance for credit risk assessment, marketing for customer segmentation and campaign targeting, healthcare for disease outbreak prediction, and in retail for demand forecasting.
