Pixel density, commonly measured in pixels per inch (PPI), quantifies how many pixels are packed into each inch of a display's surface. A higher pixel density means more pixels occupy the same physical space, which translates to a finer and more detailed image. This increased concentration of pixels makes individual picture elements less discernible to the human eye, resulting in smoother lines, sharper text, and more defined graphics.

For example, a smartphone with a higher pixel density typically displays text and images with greater clarity. This metric is crucial for the visual quality of various digital displays, including computer monitors, televisions, tablets, and virtual reality headsets. It significantly influences the perceived sharpness and realism of content, especially when screens are viewed at close distances.
