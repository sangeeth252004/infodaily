This involves integrating digital solutions into all areas of a business, from customer interactions and operational processes to business models and organizational culture. The goal is to improve efficiency, enhance customer experience, foster innovation, and enable new revenue streams. It typically extends beyond simply digitizing existing processes, often requiring a re-evaluation and redesign of core strategies and systems.

For instance, a retail company might implement e-commerce platforms, data analytics for personalized marketing, and automated inventory management to streamline operations and better serve customers. This strategic shift is commonly discussed within business management, technology strategy, and organizational change management contexts, particularly in industries undergoing rapid technological advancement or seeking competitive advantage.
