A data pipeline automates the flow of information, ensuring that data is collected, cleaned, processed, and delivered efficiently. It consists of distinct stages, each performing a specific task, such as extracting data from a source, converting it into a usable format, and loading it into a target system like a database or data warehouse. This systematic approach allows for consistent and reliable handling of large volumes of information.

For instance, a company might use a data pipeline to gather sales figures from various online stores, combine them with customer demographics, and then analyze the consolidated data to identify purchasing trends.

This concept is widely employed in fields such as business intelligence, machine learning, and big data analytics, where efficient data management is crucial for generating insights and making informed decisions.
