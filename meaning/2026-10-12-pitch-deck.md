A pitch deck is a visual aid designed to communicate key information about a venture to a specific audience, typically investors, partners, or potential clients. It usually comprises a series of slides that sequentially present the core aspects of the business.

These presentations often include an executive summary, a problem statement, a proposed solution, details about the market opportunity, the business model, the team, financial projections, and a request for investment or collaboration. The goal is to generate interest and persuade the audience to take a desired action.

*Example usage:* The startup shared its pitch deck with venture capitalists, hoping to secure seed funding.

This term is frequently encountered in the realms of entrepreneurship, startup fundraising, business development, and venture capital.
