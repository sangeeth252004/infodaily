This approach relies heavily on the resources and efforts of the founders themselves. Instead of seeking loans or investment from outside sources, individuals or teams utilize their own savings, sweat equity, and resourcefulness to get their venture off the ground. This often involves being frugal, prioritizing essential tasks, and leveraging existing skills and networks.

For example, a software developer might build and launch their application using their personal computer and free online marketing tools before seeking any outside capital.

Bootstrapping is commonly employed by startups and small businesses, particularly in technology, creative industries, and service-based sectors, where initial capital requirements can be relatively low.
