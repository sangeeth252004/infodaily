This bias occurs when the data used to train an algorithm reflects existing societal prejudices, or when the algorithm's design inadvertently favors certain groups. The algorithm learns patterns from the data, and if those patterns are discriminatory, the algorithm will perpetuate or even amplify that discrimination in its outputs. This can manifest in various ways, from skewed recommendations to unfair loan application approvals.

For instance, if a facial recognition system is trained primarily on images of people with lighter skin tones, it may perform poorly and misidentify individuals with darker skin tones.

This term is commonly used in discussions concerning artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and the ethical implications of technology.
