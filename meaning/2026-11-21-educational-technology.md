This field involves the study and ethical practice of facilitating learning and improving performance through the creation, utilization, and management of appropriate technological processes and resources. It integrates various digital and non-digital tools, instructional design principles, and methodologies to address educational challenges and improve learning outcomes. The primary goal is to optimize learning experiences, make education more accessible, and personalize instruction for diverse learners.

For example, a university might leverage educational technology by deploying a learning management system for course delivery, online assignments, and student collaboration. This concept is commonly discussed in academic research, curriculum development, teacher training programs, and professional development settings across K-12 education, higher education, and corporate learning environments.
