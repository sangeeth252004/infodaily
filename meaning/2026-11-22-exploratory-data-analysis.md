EDA involves using statistical graphics and other data visualization techniques to explore data, uncover underlying patterns, detect anomalies, test hypotheses, and check assumptions with the aid of summary statistics and graphical representations. Its primary purpose is to gain a foundational understanding of the data's structure, relationships between variables, and distributions before formal modeling or hypothesis testing. This initial investigation helps data professionals derive insights, identify potential data quality issues, and guide subsequent steps like feature engineering or model selection.

For example, a marketing team might conduct EDA on customer demographic data to visualize age group distributions and identify regional spending patterns.

This approach is fundamentally employed across disciplines such as data science, machine learning, business intelligence, and academic research, serving as an essential first step in most data-driven projects.
