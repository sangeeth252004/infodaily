Glasgow, Scotland – India secured its first medal at the Commonwealth Games today as para powerlifter Jhandu Kumar earned a bronze in the Men's Lightweight category. His achievement marked a significant milestone for the Indian contingent on the second day of competition in Glasgow, which otherwise saw a mixture of strong performances and early exits across various sports.

Kumar's medal was won following a determined performance in the para powerlifting event. Competing against a strong international field, he successfully lifted 135 kilograms, placing him third and earning India its first podium finish of the Games. This initial medal provided a boost to the Indian team, which is participating with a large contingent across numerous disciplines.

Day two of the Games presented a varied landscape for Indian athletes. The nation’s badminton mixed team advanced through their initial rounds, demonstrating strong form and securing their place in subsequent stages of the competition. Similarly, the women's hockey team registered a decisive victory against a lower-ranked opponent, indicating a positive start to their campaign.

However, not all results were favorable. Several Indian boxers faced tough competition in their preliminary bouts, leading to a number of early eliminations from the tournament. In swimming, Indian participants competed in various heats but were unable to secure qualification for medal contention, highlighting the high level of global competition in aquatic events. Artistic gymnastics also saw Indian athletes deliver promising performances, yet they fell short of securing medals on the apparatus finals.

Key results for India on Day 2 included:
*   **Para Powerlifting:** Jhandu Kumar – Bronze medal (Men's Lightweight, 135kg lift).
*   **Badminton:** Mixed Team advanced to the next round.
*   **Hockey:** Women's team secured a win against their opponents.
*   **Boxing:** Mixed results in preliminary rounds, with some athletes progressing and others eliminated.
*   **Swimming:** No advancements to medal finals.
*   **Gymnastics:** Strong performances but no medals secured.

Looking ahead, Indian athletes are preparing for further challenges on Day 3 of the Commonwealth Games. The focus remains on capitalizing on initial successes while aiming for improved performances in disciplines where results were mixed. Competition is set to intensify as more medal events approach, with India hoping to add to its medal tally in the coming days.
