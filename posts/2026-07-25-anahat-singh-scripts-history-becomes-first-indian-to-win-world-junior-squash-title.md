Anahat Singh, a 16-year-old squash player from India, has made history by becoming the first Indian to win the World Junior Squash Championship in the Girls' U-19 category. The landmark victory occurred in Gold Coast, Australia, where Singh defeated England's Sophie Spicer in the final. This achievement marks a significant milestone for Indian squash and concludes a 13-year period of dominance by Egyptian athletes in this particular tournament.

The final match, held on February 13, 2024, saw Singh deliver a commanding performance against her English opponent. Her triumph highlights a period of rising talent within Indian junior squash and positions her as a prominent figure to watch in the sport. The win underscores her consistent progress and ability to perform under international pressure, bringing a world title to India for the first time in this junior category.

Singh's path to the championship involved navigating a challenging draw, demonstrating her strategic play and athletic endurance. Her victory over Spicer, a formidable competitor, was secured with a final scoreline of 3-1, with individual game scores reflecting a hard-fought contest. This success follows a series of notable performances by Singh on the international junior circuit, indicating a sustained upward trajectory in her burgeoning career.

Key details of Anahat Singh's historic win include:
*   **Event:** World Junior Squash Championship (Girls' U-19)
*   **Location:** Gold Coast, Australia
*   **Date of Final:** February 13, 2024
*   **Opponent:** Sophie Spicer (England)
*   **Final Score:** Anahat Singh defeated Sophie Spicer 3-1 (11-8, 8-11, 11-7, 11-5)
*   **Historical Significance:** First Indian to win the World Junior Squash title in any category.
*   **Impact:** Ended Egypt's 13-year winning streak in the Girls' U-19 championship.
*   **Age:** 16 years old at the time of victory.

Prior to this world title, Anahat Singh had already garnered international attention. In 2022, she became the youngest Indian athlete to represent the country at the Commonwealth Games in Birmingham, competing in the women's singles and mixed doubles events. She also secured the Girls' U-15 British Junior Open title in 2023, showcasing her capabilities across different age groups. These previous achievements provided a strong foundation for her recent success on the global junior stage.

Anahat Singh's historic win is expected to significantly boost the profile of squash in India and inspire a new generation of athletes.
