Nationalist Congress Party (Sharadchandra Pawar) chief Sharad Pawar recently addressed public speculation regarding the circumstances surrounding Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan's withdrawal of candidature from the Sambalpur Lok Sabha constituency. Pawar disclosed details of a meeting he held with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, suggesting a connection to Pradhan's subsequent decision.

Pawar's statements came amidst a backdrop of political discussions, where he highlighted an interaction he had with the Prime Minister on March 14. During this meeting, Pawar stated that he raised various issues concerning sugar cooperative factories in Maharashtra. He noted that Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan, who at the time held a portfolio relevant to cooperatives, subsequently withdrew his candidacy.

*   **Meeting Date:** March 14
*   **Attendees:** Sharad Pawar and Prime Minister Narendra Modi
*   **Discussion Focus:** Concerns related to Maharashtra's sugar cooperative factories
*   **Related Event:** Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan's withdrawal of his candidature from the Sambalpur Lok Sabha seat shortly after the meeting.

Pawar, as quoted by media, stated, "I personally met PM Modi in Delhi. I raised issues related to sugar cooperative factories which were facing problems in Maharashtra. Dharmendra Pradhan was the minister for cooperatives and it was reported that he resigned." This statement refers to Pradhan's decision to withdraw from the Sambalpur Lok Sabha contest. Following this, Pradhan subsequently filed his nomination from the Deogarh Lok Sabha seat in Odisha.

The NCP(SP) chief further elaborated on the political developments that followed Pradhan's initial withdrawal. He noted that after the initial decision, supporters of Pradhan raised slogans, advocating for his re-election from the Sambalpur constituency. Despite this, Pradhan was ultimately nominated for a different electoral region, contesting from Deogarh. This sequence of events, as described by Pawar, adds a layer of political intrigue to the pre-election period.
