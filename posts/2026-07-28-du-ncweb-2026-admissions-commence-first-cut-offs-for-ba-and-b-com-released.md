Delhi University's Non-Collegiate Women's Education Board (NCWEB) has officially initiated its admission process for the academic year 2026. The first cut-off list for Bachelor of Arts (BA) and Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) programs was released on July 28, marking the start of merit-based admissions for eligible female candidates. Applicants can now begin the process of securing seats across NCWEB's various teaching centres.

The Non-Collegiate Women's Education Board plays a significant role in providing access to higher education for women who are unable to enroll in regular college programs. NCWEB students attend classes predominantly on weekends or during academic breaks, allowing them to pursue full-time degree courses while managing other commitments. This model aims to expand educational opportunities, particularly for those residing in the National Capital Region.

The recently published first cut-off list details the minimum percentage marks required for admission to specific BA and B.Com courses at various NCWEB colleges. Candidates who meet the stipulated cut-off criteria for their desired program and college are eligible to apply. Admissions are strictly based on academic merit, determined by the marks secured in their qualifying examinations.

Eligible applicants are required to access the official Delhi University admission portal to complete their online application. Following the submission of the application and required documents, candidates will proceed to a physical document verification stage at their chosen NCWEB teaching centre. This verification step is crucial to confirm the authenticity of academic records and other submitted information.

Key details for the first admission round are as follows:
*   **Application Period:** Eligible candidates must complete their online application and college selection between July 28 and July 30.
*   **Verification and Approval:** Colleges will verify documents and approve admissions from July 28 to July 31.
*   **Fee Payment:** The deadline for fee payment for candidates approved under the first cut-off is August 1.
*   **Required Documents:** Applicants must typically submit their Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, provisional certificates, character certificates, caste certificates (if applicable), income certificates (for EWS category), recent passport-sized photographs, and signature.
*   **Program Offerings:** NCWEB offers BA and B.Com programs, with various subject combinations available under the BA curriculum depending on the teaching centre.

Following the conclusion of admissions under the first cut-off list, Delhi University's NCWEB is expected to release subsequent cut-off lists if seats remain vacant across its programs and colleges. Applicants are advised to regularly monitor the official NCWEB website for further announcements, additional cut-off releases, and any updates regarding the admission schedule for the 2026 academic session.
