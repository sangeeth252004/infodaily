*   **Technical Resistance and Support:** Technical analysis points to strong resistance at the upper end of the projected range, with robust buying interest typically emerging at the lower support levels. These technical barriers are expected to contain price movements.

This outlook suggests that investors may see reduced directional volatility in gold prices, potentially favoring strategies focused on trading within established ranges or maintaining long-term holdings without significant near-term appreciation. A range-bound market also implies that for gold to break out of this pattern, a significant shift in core economic indicators, central bank rhetoric, or geopolitical events would be necessary.
