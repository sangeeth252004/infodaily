A man in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, reportedly died by suicide after allegedly killing his wife in their rented accommodation late Tuesday night. Anuj Awasthi, 28, was found hanging, and his wife, Neha, 25, was found dead on their bed. Police have launched an investigation into the incident, which occurred in the Keshav Puram area under the Kalyanpur police station limits.

The bodies were discovered by family members who became concerned after the couple did not respond to calls. Upon gaining entry to the residence, they found Neha's body on the bed with a towel wrapped around her neck and sindoor on her forehead. Anuj's body was found hanging from the ceiling fan. Authorities were immediately notified, and police personnel arrived at the scene to initiate preliminary investigations and secure the area.

Initial police findings suggest that Anuj Awasthi first allegedly killed Neha before taking his own life. A four-page suicide note, reportedly written by Anuj, was recovered from the scene. The note was addressed to his parents, in-laws, and wife, offering apologies for his actions. In the note, Anuj reportedly expressed profound distress over the non-acceptance of their marriage by their respective families, which he cited as a significant source of his turmoil.

*   **Marital Background:** Anuj and Neha were first cousins who had married approximately six months prior against the explicit wishes of their families. This familial opposition to their union was a prominent theme discussed in Anuj's suicide note.
*   **Motive Stated in Note:** Anuj's suicide note reportedly explained his decision to kill Neha, stating that his deep love for her prevented him from imagining her with anyone else after his death. He expressed a desire to take her with him. The note also contained requests for their last rites to be performed together by their families.
*   **Occupations:** At the time of the incident, Anuj Awasthi was employed as a security guard, while Neha was a homemaker.
*   **Police Investigation:** Superintendent of Police (West) Vibhav Srivastava confirmed the recovery of the suicide note and stated that a case has been registered. The bodies have been sent for post-mortem examinations to ascertain the exact cause of death for both individuals. Preliminary observations on Neha's body indicated no external injuries, suggesting strangulation as a possible cause, pending forensic confirmation.
*   **Family Statements:** Relatives, including Anuj's uncle and Neha's father, confirmed the couple's marriage against family wishes and the ongoing issues regarding its acceptance within the family. Their statements corroborated the details found in the suicide note concerning the family's disapproval.

The Kalyanpur police continue their comprehensive investigation into the sequence of events leading to the deaths. Forensic teams have collected evidence from the scene, and further details are expected to emerge following the completion of the post-mortem reports. The case remains under active investigation to confirm all facts surrounding the tragic incident.
