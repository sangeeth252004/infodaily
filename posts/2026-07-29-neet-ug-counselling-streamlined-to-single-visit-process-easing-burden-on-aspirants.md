**NEW DELHI** – Medical aspirants seeking admission through the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test-Undergraduate (NEET-UG) will now only be required to make a single physical visit for document verification during the counselling process. This significant policy alteration, aimed at streamlining the admission procedure and alleviating logistical challenges for students and their families, was recently announced by the official counselling authorities responsible for NEET-UG admissions. The move is effective for the upcoming counselling sessions, impacting thousands of students nationwide.

The new directive marks a departure from the previous system, which often necessitated multiple physical visits for document verification across various rounds of counselling, proving to be both time-consuming and financially burdensome for candidates, particularly those travelling from remote areas. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), under the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) of the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, is the primary body overseeing the All India Quota (AIQ) counselling for NEET-UG. While specific details on the exact implementation mechanism are awaited in subsequent notifications, the core principle is to centralize and reduce physical interactions.

The shift is expected to enhance efficiency and accessibility within the medical admission ecosystem. By reducing the number of mandatory in-person verifications, authorities aim to de-stress the process for students and parents, enabling them to complete the crucial stages of counselling with greater ease. This measure aligns with broader efforts to leverage technology and improve administrative processes in higher education admissions.

Key aspects and anticipated benefits of the single-visit counselling system include:

*   **Reduced Travel and Costs:** Eliminates the need for repeated long-distance travel, directly translating into significant cost savings on transportation and accommodation for families.
*   **Time Efficiency:** Frees up valuable time for students who would otherwise spend days travelling and waiting for verification processes, allowing them to focus on academic preparedness or other commitments.
*   **Streamlined Logistics:** Simplifies the overall counselling schedule and reduces the administrative load on verification centres, potentially leading to a faster and more organized admission cycle.
*   **Enhanced Accessibility:** Particularly beneficial for candidates from economically disadvantaged backgrounds or those residing in remote regions, making the counselling process more equitable.
*   **Focus on Digital Platforms:** Implies a greater reliance on online platforms for initial registration, choice filling, provisional allotment, and other stages, with the physical visit reserved for final document checks.

The implementation details, including the exact stage at which the single physical verification will occur (e.g., after final allotment or at designated nodal centres), will be communicated through official information bulletins released by the counselling authorities. Candidates are advised to monitor the official websites of the MCC and state counselling bodies for the precise guidelines, schedules, and any necessary preparatory steps for the upcoming counselling rounds. This policy adjustment represents a notable effort to modernize and humanize the competitive medical admission process in India.
