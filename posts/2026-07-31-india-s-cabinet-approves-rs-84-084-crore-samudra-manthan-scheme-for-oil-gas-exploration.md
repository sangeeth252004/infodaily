The Indian Cabinet has formally approved the 'Samudra Manthan' scheme, committing an outlay of Rs 84,084 crore towards significantly enhancing domestic oil and gas exploration and production. This substantial financial commitment, announced recently, aims to accelerate discovery and extraction efforts across the nation's extensive sedimentary basins, contributing directly to India's long-term energy security objectives.

This initiative underscores the government's strategic imperative to reduce India's substantial reliance on imported crude oil and natural gas. As the world's third-largest energy consumer, India imports approximately 85% of its crude oil requirements and nearly half of its natural gas needs. The 'Samudra Manthan' scheme is designed to bolster indigenous hydrocarbon reserves, thereby aiming to mitigate the impact of volatile global energy prices on the national economy and ensure a stable, affordable energy supply for its rapidly growing industrial and consumer base.

The 'Samudra Manthan' program is poised to intensify exploration activities across both onshore and offshore regions. It is expected to involve advanced geological and geophysical surveys, including 2D and 3D seismic data acquisition, followed by extensive exploratory drilling campaigns in identified high-potential blocks. The scheme will focus on unlocking new hydrocarbon provinces and increasing the output from existing fields through enhanced oil recovery (EOR) and gas recovery techniques, utilizing cutting-edge technology and best international practices.

*   **Key Aspects of the 'Samudra Manthan' Scheme:**
    *   **Financial Commitment:** A total of Rs 84,084 crore sanctioned by the Indian Cabinet.
    *   **Core Objective:** To significantly boost domestic production of crude oil and natural gas.
    *   **Strategic Goal:** Enhance India's energy self-reliance and reduce import bills.
    *   **Operational Scope:** Includes geological and geophysical surveys, 2D and 3D seismic data acquisition, and exploratory drilling across diverse sedimentary basins.
    *   **Technological Focus:** Deployment of advanced exploration and production technologies to maximize resource recovery.
    *   **Economic Impact:** Anticipated to generate substantial employment opportunities, foster technological advancements, and attract further investment into India's upstream oil and gas sector.

The implementation of the 'Samudra Manthan' scheme is anticipated to unfold in phases, beginning with the identification and assessment of prospective exploration blocks. Following detailed planning and transparent bidding processes for exploration and production contracts, initial activities are expected to focus on high-potential areas previously identified through preliminary geological studies. The long-term success of this ambitious program is considered crucial for securing India's energy future and supporting its economic growth trajectory in the coming decades.
