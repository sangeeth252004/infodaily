A passenger ferry caught fire off the coast of Madura Island, Indonesia, in the early hours of Monday, October 28, 2024, resulting in five confirmed fatalities and 41 individuals reported missing. The incident has prompted a large-scale search and rescue operation by Indonesian authorities.

The vessel, identified as the MV Merdeka Raya, was reportedly carrying 166 passengers and crew on a routine journey from Surabaya, East Java, to Sumenep, Madura. According to a preliminary statement from the ferry operator, PT Nusantara Maritim, the fire originated in the engine room shortly after midnight local time and rapidly spread through sections of the ship. A distress signal was broadcast before communication with the vessel was lost.

Indonesia's National Search and Rescue Agency (Basarnas) immediately launched a comprehensive operation following the alert. Multiple search vessels, including patrol boats from the Indonesian Navy and local marine police, along with a surveillance aircraft, were deployed to the location approximately 15 nautical miles off Madura's northern coast. Strong currents and limited visibility in the initial hours of the operation posed significant challenges for rescue teams.

As of Monday afternoon, Basarnas officials confirmed that approximately 120 individuals had been rescued from the burning vessel or the surrounding waters. Rescued passengers were transported to nearby ports for medical attention, with several treated for smoke inhalation and minor injuries. The five confirmed fatalities were recovered from the water or within the ship's wreckage. The search for the 41 missing individuals remains ongoing, with operations expected to continue through the night and into the following days.

Indonesia, an archipelago nation, relies heavily on ferry services for inter-island transport of both people and goods. While essential for connectivity, the country has a history of maritime accidents, often attributed to overcrowding, aging vessels, and inadequate safety standards. This latest incident underscores persistent concerns regarding maritime safety in the region.

Key details confirmed by authorities include:

*   **Confirmed Fatalities:** 5
*   **Missing Individuals:** 41
*   **Rescued Individuals:** Approximately 120
*   **Vessel:** MV Merdeka Raya, a passenger and cargo ferry
*   **Incident Location:** Approximately 15 nautical miles off Madura Island, East Java province
*   **Agencies Involved:** Basarnas, Indonesian Navy, local marine police, port authorities

Basarnas has stated that search and rescue efforts will continue without interruption as long as there is a possibility of finding survivors. A formal investigation into the cause of the fire is anticipated to be launched by the National Transportation Safety Committee (KNKT) once the immediate rescue phase concludes. The findings of this investigation will be crucial in determining the exact circumstances of the disaster and informing potential improvements to maritime safety regulations.
