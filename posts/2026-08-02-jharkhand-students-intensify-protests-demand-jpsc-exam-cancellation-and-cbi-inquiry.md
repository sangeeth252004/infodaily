Thousands of students across Jharkhand have intensified their protests, demanding the immediate cancellation of the recent Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) Combined Civil Service Main Examination and advocating for a Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) inquiry into alleged irregularities. The demonstrations, primarily concentrated in the state capital Ranchi, have seen significant participation from aggrieved aspirants over recent days, echoing long-standing concerns regarding transparency in the state's public recruitment processes.

The ongoing unrest stems from widespread allegations of discrepancies and procedural lapses surrounding the conduct and evaluation of the JPSC examination. This examination is a critical gateway for recruitment to various administrative and civil service posts within the state government. Students claim that the integrity of the examination process has been severely compromised, directly impacting the career prospects of thousands who appeared for the highly competitive assessment.

Protesters have highlighted several specific points of concern, including alleged anomalies in the preliminary examination results, questionable evaluation methods for main examination answer sheets, and a perceived lack of clarity and transparency throughout the entire selection process. These accusations have led to a significant erosion of trust in the JPSC's ability to conduct fair and unbiased examinations. Student organizations have pointed to patterns they describe as inconsistent, with several individuals allegedly receiving disproportionately high marks or being selected despite apparent discrepancies.

The core demands from the various student groups are unequivocal:
*   **Immediate cancellation** of the results for the JPSC Combined Civil Service Main Examination.
*   **A thorough and impartial investigation** to be conducted by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) to probe all allegations of malpractices and procedural violations.
*   **Enhanced transparency** in all future JPSC examination processes, including detailed score breakdowns and clear selection criteria.

Various student bodies, including the Jharkhand Chhatra Morcha and other unaffiliated student groups, have organized rallies, sit-ins, and public appeals to press their demands. Several local political leaders have also expressed solidarity with the protesting students, further amplifying calls for state intervention and a credible inquiry.

The JPSC examination holds immense importance for graduates aspiring to government jobs in Jharkhand, making any allegations of misconduct a highly sensitive issue with broad implications for public confidence and the state's administrative framework. Past instances of similar allegations in other state-level examinations have also contributed to the current climate of distrust among aspirants. The students maintain that a CBI investigation is essential to ensure an unbiased probe, given their perceived lack of confidence in internal or state-level inquiries.

As of the current reporting, state authorities, including the JPSC, have not issued a definitive public statement directly addressing the demands for examination cancellation or the initiation of a CBI inquiry. The protesting students have collectively vowed to continue their agitation until their grievances are addressed and concrete steps are taken to ensure justice and transparency. The situation remains under close observation by stakeholders as the administration deliberates on a response to the escalating demands.
