PUNE, Maharashtra – An IT professional, his wife, and their son were discovered dead at their residence in the Narhe area of Pune on Thursday, May 16, 2024. Local authorities confirmed the recovery of a note from the scene, which reportedly cited job loss and significant financial stress as factors contributing to the tragic incident.

The incident came to light after neighbors grew concerned when the family had not been seen for several days. Following checks by local residents, the police were alerted and subsequently arrived at the residence. Upon forced entry, officers discovered the bodies of the three family members. The initial police assessment points towards an apparent suicide.

Investigators from the Sinhagad Road police station initiated proceedings, registering an accidental death report (ADR) as part of their standard procedure. Preliminary findings suggest that the family had been grappling with severe financial difficulties.

Key details emerging from the ongoing investigation include:
*   **Discovery:** Neighbors reported an unusual silence from the flat, leading to a police welfare check.
*   **Location:** The family resided in the Narhe area, within the jurisdiction of the Sinhagad Road police station.
*   **Note Content:** The recovered note reportedly detailed an IT professional's recent job loss and the subsequent financial strain. It also mentioned the wife's previous job loss and concerns over unpaid EMIs and outstanding loans. This information aligns with the police's statement regarding the primary focus of their inquiry.
*   **Police Action:** An accidental death report has been filed, and further investigation is underway to corroborate the details mentioned in the note and ascertain all circumstances surrounding the deaths.

Authorities have stated that a comprehensive investigation will be conducted to fully understand the sequence of events and verify the claims made in the note. The bodies have been sent for post-mortem examination, and forensic teams have processed the scene for evidence. The police are also examining the family's financial records and speaking with relatives and acquaintances to gather more information. No further official statements regarding ongoing developments have been released at this time.
