A missile strike on a recreational beach in Sevastopol, Crimea, on Sunday, June 23, resulted in the deaths of seven individuals, including three children, and left 40 others injured. Russian authorities stated that fragments from an intercepted Ukrainian missile fell onto the crowded Black Sea coastline, causing the casualties.

The incident occurred as beachgoers were present during daylight hours. Local governor Mikhail Razvozhayev reported the immediate aftermath, noting the rapid deployment of emergency services. According to the Russian Defense Ministry, Ukrainian forces launched five ATACMS (Army Tactical Missile System) missiles, supplied by the United States, targeting civilian infrastructure in Sevastopol, a port city in Crimea, which Russia annexed from Ukraine in 2014. Four of the missiles were reportedly intercepted by Russian air defenses, but the warhead of a fifth missile detonated after being hit, scattering fragments across the beach area.

*   **Victims:** Seven confirmed fatalities, comprising four adults and three children.
*   **Injuries:** Approximately 40 individuals sustained injuries, ranging from shrapnel wounds to trauma.
*   **Location:** Uyutny beach and park area in Sevastopol, a popular recreational spot.
*   **Weapon System:** Russian authorities identified the weapon as U.S.-supplied ATACMS missiles.

The Russian Defense Ministry condemned the attack, characterizing it as a "terrorist" act and asserting that the U.S. bears direct responsibility for the strike due to the supply of the missiles and alleged involvement in targeting intelligence. The Ministry stated, "All flight assignments for ATACMS operational-tactical missiles are entered by American specialists based on their own satellite reconnaissance data." No immediate comment was issued by Ukrainian officials regarding the attack, nor by the United States concerning Russia's allegations.

Governor Razvozhayev announced a state of emergency in Sevastopol following the attack and declared June 24 a day of mourning. Medical teams provided immediate assistance to the injured, with some victims requiring hospitalization. Preparations were made for specialized medical evacuations for those with severe injuries. The incident prompted swift condemnation from Moscow, with the Russian Foreign Ministry vowing a response. The full implications of the strike, including potential retaliatory actions, remain under observation.
