Indian cricketer Ajinkya Rahane has recently shared his perspective on the transformative period in Indian cricket following Mahendra Singh Dhoni's decision to step down from captaincy. Rahane, a prominent figure in the Test squad for many years, highlighted the roles played by Virat Kohli as captain and Ravi Shastri as head coach in shaping the team's approach and mental toughness during their tenure. His remarks provide an insider's view into the evolution of the national side.

Rahane, known for his calm demeanor and tactical awareness, suggested that Dhoni's departure from the captaincy in white-ball cricket in 2017 and subsequently from Test leadership in late 2014, paved the way for a new philosophy. According to Rahane, this transition allowed Kohli and Shastri to instill a more aggressive and resilient mindset within the team. He emphasized that their combined leadership focused on fostering a team capable of competing fiercely in all conditions globally, a significant shift that garnered attention from cricket pundits and fans alike.

The period under Kohli and Shastri saw India achieve considerable success, particularly in Test cricket, including their historic maiden Test series victory in Australia in 2018-19. Rahane, who served as vice-captain during much of this era, observed that the duo prioritized developing a "tougher" Indian side. This toughness, as per Rahane's statement, was not merely about on-field aggression but also about developing a strong mental fortitude and an unwavering belief in achieving victory away from home. Their strategy involved challenging players to push their limits and fostering a culture of high performance and uncompromising fitness standards.

Key aspects highlighted by Ajinkya Rahane regarding the Kohli-Shastri era include:
*   **Mental Fortitude:** A deliberate focus on building players' mental strength to overcome adversity in high-pressure situations.
*   **Aggressive Approach:** Promotion of an attacking brand of cricket, especially in Test matches, aiming for wins rather than draws.
*   **Fitness Standards:** An increased emphasis on physical fitness, elevating benchmarks for all squad members.
*   **Team Unity:** Cultivating a strong dressing room environment where players supported each other and fought as a collective unit.

Rahane's comments underscore the lasting impact of this leadership phase on Indian cricket's identity. His reflections come at a time when the team continues to build on the foundations laid during these transitions, with a new generation of players integrating into the squad. The insights from a senior player like Rahane offer a valuable historical account of how Indian cricket evolved through distinct leadership styles to become a formidable force on the international stage. As the sport continues to progress, such historical perspectives contribute to understanding the trajectory and strategic shifts within national teams.
