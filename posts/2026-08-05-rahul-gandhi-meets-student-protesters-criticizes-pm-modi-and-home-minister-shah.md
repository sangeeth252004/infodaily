New Delhi – Congress leader Rahul Gandhi recently met with student protesters, offering his strong support and asserting that there was "no need to apologise" for their demonstrations. During the interaction, Mr. Gandhi delivered sharp criticism towards Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Union Home Minister Amit Shah, specifically addressing allegations of police brutality against students involved in the protests.

Mr. Gandhi’s remarks signal a clear message of solidarity from a prominent opposition figure towards the student community. He conveyed robust support for the students' fundamental right to protest, emphasizing that their dissent was a legitimate exercise of democratic freedom and required no apology. This direct engagement aims to underscore the Indian National Congress party's stance on student activism and the perceived overreach in government responses to public gatherings.

The criticism directed at Prime Minister Modi and Home Minister Shah primarily focused on the perceived heavy-handedness in managing the student demonstrations. Mr. Gandhi publicly accused the ruling leadership of employing excessive force against students, raising significant concerns over the erosion of democratic rights and freedom of expression in the country. His statements frame the government's actions as an attempt to suppress legitimate protest through coercive means.

Student protests have a long-standing history in India, often serving as a significant catalyst for political and social change. The recent wave of demonstrations, though the specific triggers were not detailed in the interaction, has drawn national attention, prompting widespread debate over civil liberties and the limits of state power. Mr. Gandhi's intervention is widely interpreted as a strategic move to galvanize opposition against the incumbent Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) government and to amplify the voices of the protesting students on a national platform. Such high-profile political endorsements often lead to increased scrutiny of government policies and police responses to public unrest.

**Key Details from the Interaction:**
*   **Leader Involved:** Rahul Gandhi, a key leader of the Indian National Congress party and a prominent voice in the opposition.
*   **Core Message to Students:** "No need to apologise" for their participation in protest actions, validating their dissent.
*   **Targets of Criticism:** Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Union Home Minister Amit Shah, both senior leaders of the ruling BJP.
*   **Basis of Criticism:** Allegations of police brutality and the use of excessive force against peaceful student protesters.
*   **Context:** The meeting occurred amidst ongoing student demonstrations across various locations, highlighting broader issues of democratic participation.

The interaction between Mr. Gandhi and the student protesters is expected to further intensify the political discourse surrounding democratic rights, civil liberties, and government accountability in India. As student activism continues to play a vital role in national debates, the opposition's vocal support could influence future strategies for both the demonstrators seeking redress and the government's approach to managing public dissent. Political observers anticipate further developments as various parties continue to engage with the issues and concerns raised by the student community.
