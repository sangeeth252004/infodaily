Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP), a Mumbai-based civil rights organization, has announced the launch of a nationwide public awareness initiative titled "'Kya Bolti Public'" (What Does the Public Say?). The campaign, led by activist Teesta Setalvad, is slated to commence in September and aims to function as a "pressure group" to counter misinformation and safeguard constitutional values across India.

The initiative comes ahead of general elections and is designed to address the increasing prevalence of hate speech and divisive narratives within the public sphere. According to statements from CJP, the campaign's core objective is to engage citizens at the grassroots level, fostering a more informed and constitutionally aware populace. Setalvad emphasized that CJP will maintain a non-partisan stance, focusing solely on issues pertinent to the nation's democratic fabric rather than endorsing any specific political entity.

The 'Kya Bolti Public' campaign will primarily concentrate on educating and mobilizing communities to identify and challenge the spread of misinformation and communal polarization. CJP intends to facilitate discussions and provide resources that empower individuals to critically evaluate information and advocate for principles enshrined in the Indian Constitution. This includes promoting secularism, equality, and justice, which the organization states are crucial for maintaining social cohesion.

CJP's history of advocacy in human rights and constitutional law provides the backdrop for this latest endeavor. The organization has previously been involved in various efforts to promote justice and combat communal violence. The upcoming campaign represents a strategic effort to broaden its impact, moving from specific legal and advocacy interventions to a more widespread public engagement model. It seeks to create a collective voice that can influence public discourse and hold institutions accountable to constitutional mandates.

The campaign's planned rollout in September signifies a timely intervention, strategically placed to influence public opinion and participation as the country moves closer to significant electoral processes. CJP plans to outline further details regarding specific activities, outreach programs, and engagement methodologies as the launch date approaches. The effectiveness of this nationwide initiative will be observed as it unfolds, particularly in its capacity to generate civic participation and reinforce the commitment to democratic principles across various regions of India.
