The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has announced an extension to the application deadline for the Karnataka Land Surveyor recruitment, part of the 2026 hiring cycle. Candidates now have until August 20 to submit their applications. This decision follows recent modifications to the eligibility criteria for the positions, as confirmed by official KEA notifications.

The extension aims to provide all eligible candidates, particularly those who newly qualify under the revised criteria, sufficient time to complete their application process. The recruitment drive seeks to fill numerous Land Surveyor positions across Karnataka, crucial roles for the state's land record management and infrastructure development. The KEA is the primary state agency responsible for conducting competitive examinations and facilitating recruitment for various government departments.

Originally, the application period was set to close earlier, but the KEA's review and subsequent adjustment of eligibility requirements prompted the need for a revised timeline. While the specific details of the eligibility changes were not elaborated upon in the initial extension announcement, such revisions often pertain to educational qualifications, age limits, or specific certification requirements, potentially broadening the pool of eligible applicants. This adjustment reflects an effort to ensure wider participation and accommodate a diverse range of qualified individuals for these vital public service roles.

Key details regarding the application process and recruitment include:

*   **New Application Deadline:** August 20
*   **Recruiting Authority:** Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)
*   **Recruitment Cycle:** Land Surveyor 2026
*   **Official Website for Applications:** kea.kar.nic.in
*   **Reason for Extension:** Modifications to eligibility criteria
*   **Application Method:** Online submission through the KEA portal

Aspiring candidates are advised to visit the official KEA website, kea.kar.nic.in, to review the updated eligibility criteria, access the application portal, and stay informed about any further announcements. The online application process typically requires candidates to register, fill in personal and educational details, upload necessary documents, and pay the prescribed application fee.

Following the closure of the application window on August 20, the KEA is expected to proceed with the next stages of the recruitment process. This will likely involve the screening of applications, announcement of examination dates, and subsequent phases of selection. Further updates regarding examination schedules and subsequent selection procedures will be communicated by the KEA through its official channels.
