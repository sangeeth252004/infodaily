The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has announced a revised schedule for Round 2 of the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) Super Speciality (SS) 2024 counselling. The update, issued recently, officially withdraws an earlier notice dated August 3, 2024. This significant change directly affects thousands of candidates seeking admission to DM/MCh and DrNB Super Speciality courses for the academic year 2024-25. All aspirants are directed to the MCC's official website, mcc.nic.in, for the comprehensive revised schedule and further official announcements.

The decision by the MCC to withdraw its previous notice and subsequently issue a new timeline for Round 2 necessitates immediate attention from all registered candidates. While specific reasons for the withdrawal of the August 3 notice were not detailed, such revisions in the counselling schedule are not uncommon. They are typically implemented to accommodate administrative adjustments, address technical considerations, or incorporate updated directives from regulatory bodies to ensure a fair and streamlined admission process. The NEET SS counselling represents a critical juncture for medical postgraduates across India, determining their entry into highly competitive super specialisation programs at various prestigious medical institutions.

The MCC plays a pivotal role in facilitating admissions to Super Speciality courses by conducting counselling for the 50% All India Quota seats. Additionally, it manages the counselling for all seats in Deemed Universities, Central Universities, Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) colleges, and Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS) institutions. Given the highly competitive nature of these programs and the limited number of seats available, meticulous adherence to the updated dates is paramount. Candidates are responsible for ensuring they meet all deadlines for registration, choice filling, and final reporting to their allotted colleges to avoid forfeiture of their admission opportunities.

The counselling process is designed to ensure transparency and equity in the allocation of super speciality seats. Each round involves several stages, beginning with registration and fee payment, followed by the crucial choice filling and locking phase where candidates select their preferred courses and institutions. The subsequent seat allotment process is computer-generated based on merit and preferences, culminating in the publication of results and the final step of reporting to the assigned colleges. This structured approach aims to provide clarity and fairness throughout the admissions cycle.

The revised schedule for NEET SS 2024 Round 2 counselling outlines the following critical dates for participating candidates:

*   **Registration and Payment:** This phase commenced on September 25, 2024, and is set to conclude on September 27, 2024, by 3:00 PM (as per server time).
*   **Choice Filling and Locking:** Candidates are able to fill and lock their preferred choices from September 25, 2024, until September 27, 2024, up to 11:55 PM (as per server time).
*   **Processing of Seat Allotment:** The technical processing for seat allocation is scheduled for September 28, 2024.
*   **Publication of Result:** The results for Round 2 seat allotment will be officially released on September 29, 2024.
*   **Reporting to Allotted Institutions:** Candidates who are successfully allotted seats must report to their respective institutions between September 30, 2024, and October 5, 2024.

Candidates are strongly advised to refer exclusively to the official notifications and information published on the MCC website, mcc.nic.in. Relying solely on unofficial sources or social media for critical updates could lead to misinformation and potential issues in the counselling process.

With the new Round 2 schedule now formally announced, candidates are urged to meticulously complete their registration, choice filling, and subsequent steps within the stipulated revised dates. The MCC's swift communication of these procedural changes underscores its commitment to maintaining transparency and facilitating an organised and efficient counselling process for admissions into Super Speciality medical programs for the 2024-25 academic session. Any further updates or modifications, if necessary, are expected to be published solely on the official MCC portal, advising candidates to check regularly.
