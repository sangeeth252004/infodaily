Tarun Tejpal, former editor-in-chief of Tehelka magazine, is set to challenge his recent conviction by the Bombay High Court (Goa Bench) in a 2013 rape case before the Supreme Court of India. The decision to appeal was announced by Tejpal's legal team following the High Court's ruling, which overturned his earlier acquittal by a trial court.

The High Court's judgment on Thursday, June 13, 2024, found Tejpal guilty of sexually assaulting a former junior colleague in November 2013. This conviction reverses the verdict delivered by a Goa sessions court in May 2021, which had acquitted him of all charges. The case has drawn significant public and media attention due to Tejpal's prominent position in Indian journalism and the sensitive nature of the allegations.

The incident central to the case occurred during an event hosted by Tehelka in a five-star hotel in Goa. The former colleague accused Tejpal of sexual assault inside an elevator on two separate occasions during the event. Following an internal complaint and subsequent police investigation, Tejpal was arrested in November 2013. The Goa Police had filed a charge sheet against him in February 2014, detailing charges under various sections of the Indian Penal Code.

*   **Trial Court Acquittal:** On May 21, 2021, the Mapusa sessions court in Goa acquitted Tejpal, citing a lack of corroborative evidence and inconsistencies in the victim's testimony.
*   **State's Appeal:** The State of Goa subsequently filed an appeal against the acquittal in the Bombay High Court at Goa, arguing that the trial court had erred in its judgment and interpretation of evidence.
*   **High Court Conviction:** After extensive hearings, the High Court delivered its verdict, convicting Tejpal. The detailed judgment from the High Court is anticipated to elaborate on the grounds for overturning the lower court's decision.

Tejpal's legal representatives confirmed their intent to file an appeal with the Supreme Court, indicating that they plan to challenge the High Court's conviction. The legal process will now move to the apex court, where the arguments from both the defence and the prosecution will be heard.

This development marks a significant turn in the protracted legal battle that has spanned over a decade. The Supreme Court's proceedings will be the next critical phase in determining the final outcome of the case. The specific date for the appeal to be heard by the Supreme Court is yet to be announced.
