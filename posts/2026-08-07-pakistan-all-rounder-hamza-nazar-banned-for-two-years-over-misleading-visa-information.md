Islamabad, Pakistan – The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) announced on May 21, 2024, a two-year ban for national all-rounder Hamza Nazar. The disciplinary action was taken after Nazar was found to have provided misleading information during his visa application process for an upcoming international series against New Zealand. The ban prohibits him from participating in any form of cricket, both domestic and international, for the stipulated period.

The PCB initiated an investigation into the matter after discrepancies were identified in the documents submitted by Nazar. The cricketer was reportedly seeking to travel with the national squad for the series but failed to meet specific visa requirements. Further inquiries revealed that Nazar had allegedly misrepresented details concerning his father's previous travel history, a critical component of the visa assessment process for certain international destinations. This misrepresentation led to the formal charge and subsequent ban by the governing body.

The ban underscores the PCB's stringent stance on maintaining integrity and adherence to ethical standards among its players. Misleading information, particularly concerning official travel documentation, is considered a serious breach of player conduct regulations. Such actions not only compromise individual player eligibility but also reflect negatively on the national team and the broader cricketing institution. The PCB's code of conduct mandates transparency and honesty from all contracted players, particularly when dealing with official procedures and representations.

*   **Player Involved:** Hamza Nazar, a promising all-rounder for Pakistan.
*   **Duration of Ban:** Two years, effective immediately from the date of the announcement.
*   **Reason for Ban:** Providing misleading information in a visa application.
*   **Imposing Authority:** Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB).
*   **Impact:** Ineligibility to participate in any PCB-sanctioned cricket activities, including national teams, domestic tournaments, and franchise leagues.

This disciplinary measure sends a clear message regarding the consequences of violating established protocols. The PCB has historically emphasized discipline and professionalism within its ranks, and this ruling reaffirms its commitment to upholding these values. Players are regularly educated on the importance of following procedures and the potential repercussions of non-compliance, particularly concerning international travel and official representation.

Hamza Nazar will be ineligible to return to competitive cricket until May 2026. This period will significantly impact his career trajectory and future opportunities within Pakistan cricket. The PCB did not release further details regarding potential avenues for appeal or specific sections of the code of conduct violated, beyond stating that misleading information was the primary basis for the two-year suspension.
