Prime Minister Narendra Modi recently held a breakfast meeting with 45 Members of Parliament (MPs) from the National Democratic Alliance (NDA), where he advised them to "avoid the Lutyens web" and overcome "anxiety from inside the House." The meeting, a part of the Prime Minister's ongoing engagement with NDA parliamentarians, took place in New Delhi, emphasizing a focus on grassroots connection and public service over perceived insular political circles.

During the interaction, Prime Minister Modi reportedly stressed the importance of remaining connected with constituents and focusing on development work rather than getting entangled in the perceived complexities and power dynamics often associated with the political establishment in the capital. The phrase "Lutyens web" is commonly understood in Indian political discourse to metaphorically refer to the powerful, often interconnected, bureaucratic, political, and media elite centered in New Delhi's traditional administrative heart, designed by British architect Edwin Lutyens. The advice underscores a long-standing theme in the Prime Minister's public messaging: the need for political representatives to be accessible, responsive, and grounded in the realities of ordinary citizens.

The Prime Minister's guidance is seen as a directive for MPs to prioritize their work in their constituencies and uphold a standard of public service. This counsel is consistent with the government's broader narrative of good governance, transparency, and a direct approach to public welfare schemes. It also serves as a reminder for parliamentarians to maintain an independent perspective and resist pressures that might arise within the parliamentary or political environment.

Key aspects of the Prime Minister's interaction include:
*   **Target Audience:** 45 NDA Members of Parliament, indicating a selective engagement, possibly aimed at newer or specific groups of MPs for mentoring.
*   **Core Message:** Emphasis on "avoiding the Lutyens web," signaling a caution against becoming detached from the electorate or overly influenced by New Delhi's political culture.
*   **Underlying Principle:** Encouraging MPs to combat "anxiety from inside the House," which could imply navigating political pressures, media scrutiny, or internal party dynamics by staying true to public service.
*   **Meeting Format:** A breakfast meeting, often chosen for informal yet direct communication, allowing for a more personal exchange of ideas and guidance.

The advice delivered by Prime Minister Modi is expected to reinforce the NDA's commitment to a governance model focused on public outreach and accountability. For the 45 MPs present, the message serves as a clear directive to prioritize constituent engagement and legislative duties with a refreshed perspective, aiming to bridge any perceived gap between the political establishment and the general populace. This engagement signifies the party leadership's ongoing efforts to shape the conduct and priorities of its elected representatives.
