Arunachal Pradesh Chief Minister Pema Khandu recently rejected claims of Chinese encroachment into Indian territory, asserting that the Indian Army is effectively safeguarding the region's borders. During a public address, Mr. Khandu affirmed that not "an inch of land" has been lost to China, directly countering narratives regarding incursions. His statement reaffirms India's long-held sovereignty over Arunachal Pradesh, a state that China claims as part of its territory, referring to it as 'South Tibet'.

The Chief Minister's remarks come amidst ongoing territorial disputes between India and China along the Line of Actual Control (LAC). Arunachal Pradesh, which shares a nearly 1,126-kilometer border with China, has been a frequent point of contention in bilateral relations. India consistently maintains that Arunachal Pradesh is an integral and inalienable part of the nation, a stance reiterated by Mr. Khandu.

Mr. Khandu highlighted the significant advancements in border infrastructure undertaken by the Indian government, including the construction of new roads, bridges, and tunnels. These development projects, he noted, have substantially improved connectivity to remote areas, enhancing the Indian Army's operational capabilities, patrolling frequency, and surveillance across the challenging mountainous terrain. He emphasized that these improvements enable Indian forces to maintain a robust presence and respond effectively to any perceived threats.

The India-China border dispute has a complex history, marked by the 1962 Sino-Indian War and subsequent military standoffs, most recently in eastern Ladakh in 2020. China has on multiple occasions attempted to assert its claims over Arunachal Pradesh, including the recent renaming of places within the state, which India has consistently rejected as absurd and invalid. India's Ministry of External Affairs has previously stated that assigning invented names to places does not alter the fact that Arunachal Pradesh is an integral part of India.

Chief Minister Khandu's declaration aligns with the broader Indian government strategy of firm diplomatic engagement coupled with a strengthened defensive posture along the northern borders. The government continues to advocate for a peaceful resolution of border issues through established diplomatic channels while simultaneously investing in robust border security and infrastructure development. The statement underscores India's unwavering commitment to protecting its territorial integrity and national interests in the face of persistent claims.
