A captain of an Indian commercial flight that encountered severe turbulence during a recent journey from Phuket, Thailand, to Delhi, India, is reportedly under investigation following allegations of failing a mandatory drug test. The incident involving the alleged dope test failure surfaced on May 15, 2024, drawing immediate attention from aviation authorities and raising questions about pilot fitness and safety protocols.

The flight, operated by a major Indian airline, designated as Flight XYZ, experienced significant air turbulence on May 2, 2024. Approximately 10 passengers and crew members sustained minor injuries, including cuts and bruises, as the aircraft encountered an unexpected and severe weather phenomenon. Following a safe landing in Delhi, the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA), India's primary aviation regulatory body, initiated a comprehensive inquiry into the turbulence event. The revelation of the captain's alleged failed dope test introduces a new dimension to this ongoing investigation.

According to initial reports, the drug test in question was either part of a routine screening process or conducted in the aftermath of the turbulence incident. Under DGCA regulations, all flight crew members are subject to mandatory drug and alcohol tests, both randomly and following any significant operational incidents. A confirmed failure of such a test can lead to immediate grounding, suspension of license, and a detailed inquiry, potentially resulting in termination and further disciplinary actions. The specific substance detected in the alleged failed test has not been officially disclosed.

The captain has reportedly been removed from active flying duties pending the outcome of the investigation. This move aligns with standard aviation safety protocols designed to ensure that all flight personnel meet the stringent fitness-to-fly standards. The DGCA's focus will include determining the timeline of the test, the validity of the results, and any potential implications for the safety of the flight in question. The airline has not issued a detailed public statement on the matter, citing an ongoing internal investigation.

*   **DGCA Mandates:** The DGCA established strict regulations for drug and alcohol testing for all flight crew members in 2022, making India one of the few countries with such comprehensive mandatory testing protocols for airline personnel.
*   **Testing Protocol:** These protocols include mandatory pre-flight breath analyser tests, random post-flight tests, and tests following any incident or accident involving aircraft.
*   **Consequences of Failure:** A confirmed positive test result typically leads to a mandatory three-year grounding period for the first offense, during which the individual must undergo de-addiction and rehabilitation programs approved by the DGCA. Subsequent offenses can lead to permanent license cancellation.

The investigation by the DGCA and the airline is ongoing. Outcomes are expected to clarify the circumstances surrounding both the turbulence incident and the alleged dope test failure. This incident underscores the continuous scrutiny on aviation safety and crew fitness, with authorities reiterating their commitment to maintaining the highest standards for passenger and crew well-being in air travel. Further updates will be provided as the investigation progresses and official findings are released.
