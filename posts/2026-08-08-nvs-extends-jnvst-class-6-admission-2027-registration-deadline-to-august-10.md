The Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) has announced an extension for the registration deadline for the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027, for admissions into Class 6. Prospective students and their guardians now have until August 10 to complete their applications for the upcoming academic year. The extension aims to provide additional time for eligible candidates to apply for these prestigious residential schools across India.

JNVST is the entrance examination for admission to Class 6 in the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs), which are co-educational residential schools affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). These institutions provide free education, boarding, and lodging to talented children, primarily from rural areas, with the objective of providing them with a quality education comparable to the best residential schools in a common curriculum. The extension of the application window is a significant update for families seeking to enroll their children.

To be eligible for JNVST Class 6 admissions for the 2027 academic session, candidates must meet specific criteria. Applicants must have been born between May 1, 2015, and April 30, 2017 (inclusive). Furthermore, candidates must be currently studying in Class 5 during the academic session 2026-27 at a government, government-aided, or other recognized school within the same district where they are seeking admission to a Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya. The application process is conducted online through the official NVS website.

Key details for applicants are as follows:
*   **Extended Registration Deadline:** August 10.
*   **Official Application Portal:** navodaya.gov.in.
*   **Eligibility Criteria (Age):** Candidates must have been born between May 1, 2015, and April 30, 2017 (inclusive).
*   **Eligibility Criteria (Education):** Must be studying in Class 5 during the academic session 2026-27 in a government/government-aided/recognized school of the same district.
*   **Required Documents for Upload:**
    *   Candidate's photograph.
    *   Signature of the candidate.
    *   Signature of the parent/guardian.
    *   Study certificate, duly signed by the headmaster of the current school, confirming the candidate's details and eligibility.

Following the closure of the registration window on August 10, the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti will proceed with the next stages of the admission process. This typically involves the scheduling and conduct of the JNVST examination, which assesses candidates on mental ability, arithmetic, and language. Successful candidates from the selection test will then undergo document verification before final admission to the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic year commencing in 2027. This rigorous selection process ensures that deserving students are provided access to quality education.
