Bhubaneswar, Odisha – The Student Academic Management System (SAMS) Odisha has officially released the Round 1 seat allotment results for Postgraduate (PG) admissions for the academic session 2026-27. Aspiring candidates who applied for various postgraduate programs across state universities and colleges in Odisha can now check their allotment status by visiting the official SAMS Odisha website, samsodisha.gov.in. The release marks a critical phase in the admissions cycle, dictating the initial placement of thousands of students into their chosen academic disciplines.

The announcement, made earlier today, provides a direct link for candidates to download their individual allotment letters. To access their results, applicants are required to log in using their existing application number and password. This first phase of seat allotment is based on the candidates' academic merit, preferences submitted during the application process, and the availability of seats in various PG courses. The system aims to ensure a transparent and streamlined admission procedure for higher education in the state.

Following the release of the Round 1 results, students are now tasked with completing a series of mandatory steps to confirm their admission. This includes choosing to accept the allotted seat, opting for a 'freeze' or 'float' option, and proceeding with the institutional reporting and document verification process.

Key steps for candidates after checking Round 1 allotment:

*   **Online Acceptance of Allotment:** Candidates must log in to the SAMS portal and explicitly accept the seat allotted to them. They will have options to 'freeze' the seat (confirming their acceptance and not wishing to participate in subsequent rounds) or 'float'/'slide' (accepting the seat but indicating a desire to be considered for a higher preferred option in future rounds, if available).
*   **Payment of Admission Fees:** A non-refundable admission fee, as specified by SAMS Odisha, must be paid online within the stipulated deadline to secure the allotted seat. This payment is crucial for validating the seat acceptance.
*   **Reporting to Allotted Institution:** Successful candidates are required to report physically to their respective allotted colleges or universities with all necessary original documents for verification. This step is mandatory, and failure to report within the designated timeframe will lead to the forfeiture of the allotted seat.
*   **Document Verification:** During institutional reporting, officials will verify all academic certificates, mark sheets, caste certificates (if applicable), domicile certificates, and other supporting documents against the information provided in the application form. Discrepancies may lead to cancellation of admission.

Specific deadlines for online acceptance, fee payment, and institutional reporting are published on the SAMS Odisha portal. Candidates are strongly advised to refer to these exact dates and adhere to them strictly to avoid any issues with their admission process. The SAMS Odisha portal also provides detailed guidelines and frequently asked questions (FAQs) to assist students through each stage.

The SAMS Odisha system facilitates admissions to postgraduate courses across all public universities and government degree colleges in the state, covering a wide array of disciplines including Arts, Science, Commerce, and professional courses. The timely release of these results is integral to maintaining the academic calendar for the upcoming 2026-27 session. Candidates who were not allotted a seat in Round 1, or those who opted for the 'float' option, will have the opportunity to participate in subsequent rounds of seat allotment, the schedule for which will be announced by SAMS Odisha in due course. Regular monitoring of the official SAMS website is recommended for all applicants for further updates.
