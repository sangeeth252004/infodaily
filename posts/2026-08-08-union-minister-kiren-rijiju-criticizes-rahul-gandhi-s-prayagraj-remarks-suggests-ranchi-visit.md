On Friday, May 24, 2024, Union Minister Kiren Rijiju publicly criticized Congress leader Rahul Gandhi's recent statements delivered during a public rally in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. Mr. Rijiju's remarks included the specific suggestion that Mr. Gandhi "should have gone to Ranchi," connecting the Congress leader's address to ongoing political developments and alliances.

Mr. Gandhi, accompanied by Congress General Secretary Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, had addressed a gathering in Prayagraj, focusing his speech on issues such as the alleged threats to the Constitution, the state of democracy in India, and the demand for a nationwide caste census. His address included criticisms directed at the central government regarding what he described as attempts to undermine democratic institutions and the welfare of various communities. The event was part of the broader campaign efforts for the ongoing Lok Sabha general elections.

Responding to Mr. Gandhi's address, Union Minister Rijiju took to social media to articulate his criticism. He stated, "The way he spoke in Prayagraj, he should have gone to Ranchi." Mr. Rijiju’s comment directly referenced the political situation in Jharkhand, where the Congress is in alliance with the Jharkhand Mukti Morcha (JMM). The context for this suggestion stems from the recent arrest of former Jharkhand Chief Minister Hemant Soren, a JMM leader, by the Enforcement Directorate (ED) in connection with an alleged land fraud case.

Mr. Rijiju’s jab implies a perceived inconsistency in the Congress party's stance, particularly concerning the use of central agencies and the arrests of opposition leaders. While Mr. Gandhi often speaks about safeguarding democracy and constitutional principles, Mr. Rijiju's comment suggests that the Congress should also examine the circumstances surrounding its allies. This exchange underscores the heightened political rhetoric dominating the campaign trail as parties vie for public support ahead of the final phases of the Lok Sabha elections.

The verbal exchange between the prominent leaders highlights the ongoing confrontational narrative between the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and the opposition INDIA bloc. Such statements are common during election periods, serving to underscore party differences and challenge the narratives presented by opposing political figures.

The incident is part of a series of criticisms and counter-criticisms that have characterized the current election cycle. As the Lok Sabha elections progress, political observers anticipate continued exchanges between leaders, further shaping public discourse on governance, democracy, and electoral choices.
