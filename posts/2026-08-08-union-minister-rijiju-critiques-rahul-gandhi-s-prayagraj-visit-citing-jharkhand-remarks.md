Union Minister Kiren Rijiju on Tuesday, May 21, 2024, criticized Congress leader Rahul Gandhi's recent visit to Prayagraj, stating Gandhi "should have gone to Ranchi." The comment, made in New Delhi, immediately entered the public discourse, highlighting ongoing political exchanges concerning caste representation and Prime Minister Narendra Modi's Other Backward Class (OBC) status. Rijiju's remark directly referenced Gandhi's previous statements regarding the Prime Minister's caste lineage, setting a contentious tone in the lead-up to upcoming electoral cycles.

Rahul Gandhi had visited Prayagraj on February 14, 2024, where he performed a puja at the Sangam, a sacred confluence of rivers. His visit also included a joint press conference with Samajwadi Party president Akhilesh Yadav, a move interpreted as a display of opposition unity under the INDIA bloc. This visit occurred amidst Gandhi's sustained campaign advocating for a nationwide caste census and questioning the legitimacy of Prime Minister Modi's OBC classification. Gandhi has consistently maintained that the Prime Minister was not born into an OBC family, alleging that the Modh Ghanchi community, to which Prime Minister Modi belongs, was included in the Central List of OBCs by the BJP government in Gujarat around 2000-2001. These assertions form a core part of the Congress party's strategy to highlight perceived disparities in representation among various social groups.

Responding to Gandhi's actions and ongoing statements, Minister Rijiju's pointed "should have gone to Ranchi" remark specifically alluded to the location where Gandhi had made some of his critical statements about Prime Minister Modi's caste. Gandhi had delivered speeches in Jharkhand, including during a rally in Ranchi, articulating his position on the Prime Minister's caste identity and the broader imperative for a comprehensive caste-based enumeration. Rijiju's comment, relayed through a media interaction, suggested that Gandhi's focus should have remained on substantiating his previous claims with evidence rather than engaging in what the BJP perceives as symbolic or diversionary political events.

The exchange between Minister Rijiju and Rahul Gandhi underscores the intensifying political rhetoric prevalent in India, particularly on issues of social justice and caste politics, which are expected to be pivotal themes in the upcoming 2024 general elections. The Congress party, under Gandhi's leadership, has consistently championed the cause of a national caste census, arguing it is essential for the equitable distribution of resources and opportunities based on population demographics. The party's slogan, "Jitni abadi, utna haq" (proportionate representation based on population), aims to mobilize support from OBC, Dalit, and tribal communities across the nation.

Conversely, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has rigorously defended Prime Minister Modi's OBC identity, emphasizing his background as a "chaiwala" (tea seller) from a humble family. BJP leaders have frequently accused the opposition of attempting to create societal divisions along caste lines for political gain. The ruling party has consistently highlighted various government initiatives aimed at the welfare and empowerment of OBC communities as evidence of its commitment to social justice.

As political parties intensify their campaigns ahead of crucial elections, such verbal sparring on issues of caste, social justice, and leadership is anticipated to become more frequent. The differing approaches to socio-economic data and identity politics are expected to remain central to the national political discourse in the coming months, shaping electoral narratives and voter sentiment.
