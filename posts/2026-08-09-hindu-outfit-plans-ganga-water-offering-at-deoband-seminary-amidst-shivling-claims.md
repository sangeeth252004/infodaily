A Hindu organization has announced plans to conduct a Ganga water offering at the prominent Islamic seminary of Darul Uloom Deoband in Uttar Pradesh on August 11. The planned event stems from the organization's assertion that a 'Shivling,' a revered symbol in Hinduism associated with Lord Shiva, exists beneath the seminary's premises. This development introduces a new focus on religious claims at an established site of Islamic education.

The proposed offering, known as 'Jalabhishek,' involves the ritual pouring of sacred water, typically sourced from the Ganga river, over a Shivling. The Hindu outfit has scheduled this observance for Saturday, August 11, at the Deoband location. Their public statement underscores a belief in the presence of an ancient Hindu relic on the grounds currently occupied by the seminary. Details regarding the specific evidence supporting this claim or the exact methodology for the proposed offering have not been widely disseminated by the organizing group in their initial announcement.

Darul Uloom Deoband holds significant stature as one of the largest and most influential Islamic seminaries globally, particularly within Sunni Islam. Established in 1866, it has been a crucial center for the revival of Islamic religious sciences and a hub for the Deobandi movement. Its campus in Saharanpur district, Uttar Pradesh, draws students and scholars from various parts of the world, making it a pivotal institution in the landscape of religious education and thought. The seminary has not yet issued an official response to the Hindu organization's claim or the planned event.

The announcement of a religious observance based on a claim about a historical structure underneath an existing religious institution reflects a broader trend seen in India, where such assertions frequently lead to public discourse and sometimes legal or administrative interventions. These situations often involve diverse interpretations of historical narratives and archaeological findings. The current claim at Deoband adds to a series of similar contentions regarding religious sites across the country, highlighting the ongoing sensitivities surrounding such matters.

*   **Event:** Proposed Ganga water offering (Jalabhishek)
*   **Date:** Saturday, August 11
*   **Location:** Premises of Darul Uloom Deoband, Deoband, Uttar Pradesh
*   **Basis for Event:** Claim by a Hindu organization regarding the existence of a 'Shivling' beneath the seminary grounds
*   **Institution Affected:** Darul Uloom Deoband, a globally recognized Islamic seminary
