Eighteen individuals sustained injuries, with four reported to be in critical condition, following an incident of firing by unidentified assailants on Sunday morning, May 12, along the disputed Assam-Arunachal Pradesh border. The incident occurred in the Gabhoru Para area of Lakhimpur district, Assam, marking a significant security development in the sensitive interstate boundary region.

The victims, all identified as residents of Gogamukh in Assam's Lakhimpur district, were reportedly caught in the crossfire. Authorities have not yet identified the perpetrators or established a definitive motive behind the attack. This incident underscores the persistent tensions associated with the long-standing and often volatile border dispute between Assam and Arunachal Pradesh, which has experienced intermittent clashes and confrontations for decades. The exact circumstances leading to the firing remain under active investigation by law enforcement agencies.

Following the incident, local police and emergency services were promptly dispatched to the scene. The injured were initially transported to a hospital in Gogamukh for immediate medical attention. Four individuals, whose injuries were deemed critical by medical professionals, were subsequently transferred to the Lakhimpur Medical College & Hospital for specialized treatment and ongoing monitoring. Their conditions are being closely observed.

Assam Director General of Police (DGP) GP Singh confirmed the incident via a social media post, stating that his office had been informed and an investigation had been launched. Assam Police personnel have initiated a comprehensive probe, which includes gathering forensic evidence, interviewing witnesses, and reviewing intelligence to identify those responsible for the violence. The affected area remains under heightened security surveillance as authorities work to restore calm and prevent any further escalation of unrest.
