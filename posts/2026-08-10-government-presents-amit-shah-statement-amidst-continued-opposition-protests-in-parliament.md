He reportedly assured members that the government was taking the incident with utmost seriousness.
*   **Opposition's Demands:** Opposition leaders articulated that Mr. Shah's statement was insufficient and did not fully address their concerns regarding the implications for national security and parliamentary sanctity. They highlighted the need for a higher level of political accountability and a structured debate on the issue.
*   **Parliamentary Impact:** The ongoing impasse resulted in multiple adjournments of both the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha, impeding legislative business. Several bills listed for consideration and passage faced delays due to the persistent disruptions.
*   **Calls for Discussion:** Opposition members repeatedly attempted to move adjournment motions and notices for discussions, which were largely disallowed by the presiding officers, further fueling the protests.

The government, through its ministers, indicated that a discussion could be held once the investigation advanced further and parliamentary order was restored. However, the opposition maintained that the immediate urgency of the matter required an instant and comprehensive parliamentary debate. This sustained confrontation underscores the deepening political divide within the Indian Parliament on issues of national security and governmental accountability.

As the parliamentary session progresses, the continued refusal of the opposition to relent suggests that the deadlock is likely to persist. The government faces the challenge of advancing its legislative agenda amidst these disruptions, while the opposition aims to keep pressure on the Prime Minister for a direct response, signaling further contentious debates and potential adjournments in the coming days.
