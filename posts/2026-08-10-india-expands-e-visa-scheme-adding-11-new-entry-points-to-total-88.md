The Government of India has expanded its electronic visa (e-visa) scheme, incorporating 11 additional ports of entry across the country. This latest expansion increases the total number of designated entry points for e-visa holders to 88. The initiative is designed to streamline the entry process and enhance accessibility for international travelers visiting India for tourism, business, medical, or conference purposes.

The updated list of entry points now encompasses a broader range of airports, seaports, and land border crossings, facilitating smoother arrivals for a greater number of foreign nationals. The e-visa facility allows eligible international travelers to apply for a visa online from their home country, receive an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA), and enter India without the need for a physical visa stamp prior to arrival.

Key details of the expansion and the e-visa scheme include:

*   **Total Entry Points:** The inclusion of 11 new ports brings the national total to 88 designated entry points for e-visa holders.
*   **Types of Ports:** The expanded list includes a mix of major international airports, select seaports, and various land immigration checkpoints.
*   **Visa Categories:** The e-visa scheme covers multiple categories, including e-Tourist Visa, e-Business Visa, e-Medical Visa, e-Conference Visa, and e-Medical Attendant Visa.
*   **Application Process:** The entire application process is conducted online, providing convenience and reducing processing times for applicants.

This strategic expansion by the Bureau of Immigration and the Ministry of Home Affairs underscores India's commitment to boosting its tourism sector and facilitating international engagement. By increasing the number of accessible entry points, the government aims to encourage more foreign visitors, including those who may wish to enter India through less conventional or regional routes. The convenience offered by the e-visa scheme contributes to an improved travel experience, potentially leading to an increase in inbound travel figures for both leisure and professional purposes.

The expansion is anticipated to particularly benefit travelers arriving via cruises or those crossing land borders from neighboring countries where specific checkpoints are now enabled for e-visa processing. This broadened reach not only supports the growth of travel and tourism but also aids in strengthening economic and cultural ties with numerous nations.

Going forward, the Indian government is expected to continue monitoring the effectiveness of the e-visa scheme and may consider further adjustments or expansions based on evolving international travel trends and infrastructure development. This ongoing commitment aims to solidify India's position as a preferred global destination, offering efficient and accessible entry options for visitors worldwide.
