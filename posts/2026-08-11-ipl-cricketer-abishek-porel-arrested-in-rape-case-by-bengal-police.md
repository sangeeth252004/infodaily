Kolkata, West Bengal – Indian Premier League (IPL) cricketer Abishek Porel, a wicketkeeper-batsman for the Delhi Capitals, was arrested by Bengal Police on Monday, May 13, 2024, in connection with a case involving allegations of rape and cheating. The 21-year-old athlete was apprehended from a hotel in Kolkata following an investigation into a complaint filed in the Purba Medinipur district of West Bengal.

The arrest stems from a case registered under Sections 376 (rape) and 420 (cheating) of the Indian Penal Code (IPC). Following his apprehension, Porel was produced before a local court in Purba Medinipur, which subsequently remanded him to four days of police custody. The legal proceedings are now underway as authorities continue their investigation into the allegations.

The complaint was lodged by a 22-year-old woman who alleged that Porel had promised marriage to her but subsequently refused to honor the commitment. She further claimed that the alleged incidents occurred over several months, leading to the filing of the formal complaint with the police. Investigators tracked Porel to a hotel in Kolkata before taking him into custody.

Abishek Porel is a prominent figure in the Indian domestic cricket circuit and has made his mark in the IPL.
