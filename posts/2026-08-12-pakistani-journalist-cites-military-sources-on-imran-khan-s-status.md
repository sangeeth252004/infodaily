The phrase "no longer with us" can be interpreted in multiple ways, a point of clarification emphasized in subsequent media reporting. In journalistic discourse, this expression can signify that an individual is no longer accessible, has been relocated, or is otherwise out of public sight, rather than an explicit declaration of death. This nuance is critical given the context of Khan's legal challenges and previous arrest. The claim, made by a prominent journalist citing authoritative sources, underscores the prevailing climate of political uncertainty in Pakistan, particularly surrounding high-profile figures.

This follows the arrest of Imran Khan on May 9, 2023, from the Islamabad High Court premises on corruption charges, which subsequently triggered widespread protests and a severe crackdown on his party, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI).
