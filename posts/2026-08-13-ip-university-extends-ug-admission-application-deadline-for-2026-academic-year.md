Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), commonly known as IP University, has announced an extension for the application deadline for its undergraduate (UG) programs for the academic year 2026. Prospective students now have until August 18 to submit their applications. This extension specifically applies to admissions processed through the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) scores.

The decision to extend the deadline provides additional time for a broader pool of applicants to complete their submissions for various UG courses offered by the university. GGSIPU utilizes CUET (UG) scores as a primary criterion for admission into many of its undergraduate programs, aligning with the national directive for standardized university entrance examinations.

CUET, introduced by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, is a nationwide entrance examination conducted for admission to all undergraduate programs in Central Universities, and numerous State and private universities across the country. Its implementation aims to streamline the admission process and ensure a uniform evaluation standard for students aspiring to pursue higher education. GGSIPU is one of the prominent state universities in Delhi that has adopted CUET scores for its admission process, alongside its own counseling procedures.

Students seeking admission to GGSIPU's undergraduate programs for the 2026 academic year are required to have appeared for the respective CUET (UG) examination. The university will subsequently prepare merit lists based on valid CUET scores, followed by an online counseling process for seat allocation. The extended window is crucial for candidates who may have encountered delays in obtaining necessary documents or completing their application formalities.

GGSIPU, established in 1998 by the Government of NCT of Delhi, is a state university recognized for its wide array of professional and technical education programs. It offers undergraduate degrees across disciplines such as engineering, technology, management, law, medicine, and other allied health sciences through its affiliated colleges and schools. The university plays a significant role in providing higher education opportunities in the Delhi region and beyond.

Applicants are strongly advised to utilize this extended period to finalize their applications well before the new deadline of August 18. It is imperative for all candidates to regularly check the official GGSIPU website for detailed information regarding eligibility criteria, application guidelines, counseling schedules, and any further updates pertaining to the 2026 academic year admissions. Timely submission of applications and adherence to university guidelines are essential for consideration.
