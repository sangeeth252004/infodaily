QUETTA, PAKISTAN – Balochistan Finance Minister Mir Shoaib Nausherwani survived an armed attack on his convoy on Thursday, May 23, 2024, while traveling through the Rakhshan area of Balochistan province. One security guard sustained injuries during the incident, which involved an exchange of gunfire with unknown assailants.

The assault occurred as Minister Nausherwani's convoy was en route from Gwadar to Kharan. According to initial reports, armed individuals opened fire on the vehicles. Security personnel accompanying the minister swiftly retaliated, leading to a brief but intense firefight. Minister Nausherwani emerged from the attack unharmed, but one of his guards required medical attention for injuries sustained during the confrontation.

Shortly after the incident, the Balochistan Liberation Army (BLA), a prominent separatist militant group, claimed responsibility for the attack. A spokesperson for the BLA, Jeeyand Baloch, issued a statement via the group's official account on the social media platform 'X' (formerly Twitter), asserting their involvement.

Balochistan, Pakistan's largest province by area, is rich in natural resources but has long been afflicted by an insurgency driven by separatist and nationalist groups. These organizations, including the BLA, have been active since the early 2000s, frequently targeting government officials, security forces, and infrastructure projects. The region is a crucial component of the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC), a multi-billion-dollar infrastructure development initiative, which has also made it a focal point for attacks aimed at disrupting foreign investment and state authority.

The attack on Minister Nausherwani's convoy underscores the persistent security challenges facing Pakistan in its southwestern province. The incident is part of a broader pattern of violence in Balochistan, which has seen increased targeting of security personnel and, at times, foreign nationals involved in development projects, particularly those related to CPEC in areas such as Gwadar. These actions aim to destabilize the region and challenge the writ of the state.

Authorities have condemned the attack and confirmed that an investigation has been launched to identify and apprehend those responsible. Security measures for public officials in high-risk areas of Balochistan are expected to be reviewed. The injured security guard is currently receiving treatment at a local medical facility, and his condition is being monitored. The government has reiterated its resolve to counter militant activities in the province and ensure the safety of its citizens and officials.
