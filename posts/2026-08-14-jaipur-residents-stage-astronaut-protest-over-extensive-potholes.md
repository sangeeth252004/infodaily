Over 100 residents in Jaipur, Rajasthan, staged a distinctive protest in early November 2023, with participants donning astronaut suits to highlight the severe and long-standing issue of cratered roads. The demonstration, which took place in areas like Jagatpura and Pratap Nagar, aimed to draw immediate attention to hazardous road conditions and demand accountability from local civic authorities.

The protest involved individuals dressed in full astronaut attire walking on and around large, waterlogged potholes, simulating lunar exploration. Participants planted miniature flags within the road craters and performed symbolic "moonwalks," accompanied by placards expressing their frustration and demanding urgent repairs. The visual spectacle sought to metaphorically compare the neglected urban roads to an extraterrestrial landscape, emphasizing the extreme state of disrepair.

Residents participating in the protest articulated a range of safety concerns stemming from the damaged infrastructure. They reported that potholes, some exceeding 1.5 to 2 feet in depth, have become a major cause of daily accidents, resulting in injuries and significant vehicle damage. Beyond the immediate dangers, the poor road conditions lead to chronic issues such as waterlogging during monsoon seasons and excessive dust pollution, impacting public health and daily commutes. Areas specifically highlighted during the protest included critical stretches such as the Sitapura Industrial Area road, Mahal Road, Akshaya Patra Road, Haldi Ghati Road, and Goner Road.

*   **Participants:** Over 100 residents.
*   **Locations:** Primarily Jagatpura and Pratap Nagar areas of Jaipur, targeting roads like Sitapura Industrial Area road, Mahal Road, Akshaya Patra Road, Haldi Ghati Road, and Goner Road.
*   **Duration of Issue:** Residents claim the road conditions have been neglected for over five years.
*   **Protest Method:** Dressing as astronauts, walking on potholes, planting flags, performing "moonwalks," and displaying protest placards.
*   **Demands:** Immediate repair of damaged roads and accountability from the Jaipur Development Authority (JDA) and Jaipur Municipal Corporation (JMC).

Organizers of the demonstration stated that numerous representations and complaints regarding the deteriorating road infrastructure had been submitted to the Jaipur Development Authority (JDA) and the Jaipur Municipal Corporation (JMC) over several years. Despite these persistent efforts, residents reported a lack of concrete action from the authorities to address the critical safety and mobility challenges posed by the potholes.

As of the immediate aftermath of the protest, no official statement or commitment to a specific repair timeline has been publicly issued by the civic bodies. Residents involved in the demonstration indicate their willingness to pursue further forms of peaceful protest if their demands for comprehensive road repairs and maintenance are not met promptly. The protest underscores ongoing public pressure on urban planning and infrastructure management in rapidly developing cities.
