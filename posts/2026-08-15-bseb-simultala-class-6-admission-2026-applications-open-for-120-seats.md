The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has officially opened the application window for admission to Class 6 at Simultala Awasiya Vidyalaya (SAV), Jamui, for the academic session commencing in 2026. This announcement makes available a total of 120 seats, allocated equally with 60 seats designated for boys and 60 for girls. Interested candidates and their guardians are advised to review the detailed eligibility criteria, application procedures, and fee structure available on the BSEB's official website.

Simultala Awasiya Vidyalaya, often referred to as a "school of excellence," is a residential institution established by the Government of Bihar. It is recognized for its rigorous academic curriculum and a highly competitive admission process, drawing applications from across the state. The admission process for Class 6 is designed to identify promising students through a multi-stage selection procedure, ensuring entry for those demonstrating strong academic potential.

The application period initiates the journey for aspiring students to secure a place at this esteemed institution. It is imperative for applicants to adhere to all specified guidelines and deadlines to ensure their candidacy is considered. The detailed prospectus outlines the necessary documents, photograph specifications, and other prerequisites for a complete application submission.

Key details regarding the admission process for the 2026 academic session include:

*   **Total Seats:** 120
*   **Seat Distribution:** 60 for boys, 60 for girls
*   **Application Status:** Applications are currently open.
*   **Eligibility:** Specific age limits and academic qualifications are mandated. Typically, candidates must be within a certain age bracket and have completed Class 5 from a recognized school. Exact age cut-off dates will be specified in the official notification.
*   **Application Procedure:** Submissions are primarily conducted online through the designated portal on the BSEB official website.
*   **Selection Process:** Admission is typically based on a two-tier entrance examination: a Preliminary Test followed by a Main Examination. Successful candidates from the Main Examination may then undergo an interview and medical examination.
*   **Application Fee:** A non-refundable application fee is required, the specific amount of which will be detailed in the official admission prospectus. Fee waivers or concessions, if any, will also be outlined for eligible categories.

Prospective students and their parents are strongly encouraged to visit the official Bihar School Examination Board website to access the comprehensive admission notification. This will provide precise dates for application submission, examination schedules, detailed eligibility requirements, and the complete fee structure. Timely access to this information is crucial for ensuring a smooth application process for entry into Simultala Awasiya Vidyalaya in 2026. Further updates regarding the admission cycle will also be published on the same official platform.
