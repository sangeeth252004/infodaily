The Indian National Congress has issued a strong rebuke against Prime Minister Narendra Modi's recent "dimaagi Naxals" (intellectual Naxals) remark, asserting that the statement is indicative of the Prime Minister's desperation. Congress leaders have publicly criticized the comment, framing it as an attempt to polarize public discourse and deflect from critical issues ahead of upcoming elections.

Prime Minister Modi made the controversial remark during a public address, reportedly targeting critics and those he perceives as intellectually aligned with Naxalite ideology without direct involvement in armed insurgency. The term "urban Naxals" or "dimaagi Naxals" has been previously used by the Prime Minister and his party to criticize certain intellectuals, activists, and civil society groups, alleging their role in supporting or instigating anti-establishment sentiments. The context of the Prime Minister’s latest statement suggested it was aimed at opposition voices and those scrutinizing government policies.

The Congress party's response was swift and emphatic. Several prominent leaders across the party spectrum condemned the remark, articulating their objections through official statements and media interactions. They argued that such terminology serves to stifle dissent and demonize critics, rather than engage in constructive political debate.
