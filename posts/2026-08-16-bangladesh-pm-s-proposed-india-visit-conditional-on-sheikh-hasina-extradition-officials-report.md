A proposed visit by the Prime Minister of Bangladesh to India is contingent upon the extradition of former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina, officials familiar with the ongoing diplomatic discussions have stated. The condition, reportedly put forth by Bangladesh, introduces a significant new dynamic into the long-standing bilateral relationship between the two South Asian nations.

This development follows persistent reports of legal issues and political developments concerning Sheikh Hasina within Bangladesh. While specific charges necessitating the extradition request were not detailed by the officials, her return is now being presented as a prerequisite for high-level talks. The exact timeline for the proposed visit remains unconfirmed, awaiting resolution on this key demand.

The demand for Sheikh Hasina's extradition underscores a complex domestic political landscape in Bangladesh and places a diplomatic challenge before India. India and Bangladesh share extensive historical, cultural, and economic ties, with cooperation spanning trade, connectivity, and security. Such a condition, however, could test the resilience of these established relations and diplomatic protocols.

*   **Key Figures:** The current Prime Minister of Bangladesh and former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina are central to this diplomatic juncture.
*   **Bilateral Importance:** India is a crucial partner for Bangladesh, and high-level visits are integral for fostering mutual interests and regional stability.
*   **Legal Framework:** Extradition requests are typically governed by bilateral treaties and international law, requiring a legal process that considers judicial standards and due process. India and Bangladesh have an existing extradition treaty.

The implications of this condition extend beyond the immediate scheduling of a prime ministerial visit. It could influence future diplomatic engagements, strategic dialogues, and regional cooperation initiatives. India has historically maintained a policy of non-interference in the domestic affairs of its neighbors while also upholding its legal obligations and international commitments. The potential complexities of an extradition request involving a former head of government would require careful consideration of legal precedents and diplomatic sensitivities.

Officials did not provide a specific timeframe for India's response to the extradition condition. The situation is expected to necessitate careful deliberation within India's Ministry of External Affairs and other relevant governmental bodies. Further diplomatic exchanges are anticipated as both nations navigate this significant development, with the outcome potentially shaping the trajectory of India-Bangladesh relations in the coming months.
