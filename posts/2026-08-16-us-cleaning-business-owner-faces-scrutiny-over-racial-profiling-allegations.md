A cleaning business owner in the United States and his wife are currently under widespread scrutiny following the recent circulation of a video online. The footage, which quickly gained traction across social media platforms, shows the owner making statements about not counting "Indian" workers, sparking significant outrage and allegations of racial profiling and discriminatory employment practices.

The incident came to light after a video, reportedly filmed by a customer inside a Starbucks establishment, captured an exchange involving the cleaning business owner and his wife. In the video, the owner is heard stating, "They were Indian. We didn't count them," when discussing payment for cleaning services. His wife is also heard reinforcing this sentiment by adding, "They don't count." This conversation, centered around the accounting of workers for payment, has led to a torrent of public criticism.

The content of the video has been widely interpreted online as indicative of potential racial discrimination and unfair labor practices. Critics argue that the statements directly reference the ethnicity of workers as a basis for not counting them in their payment calculations, raising serious questions about the business's operational ethics and adherence to fair employment laws.

Key details surrounding the allegations include:
*   **The Conversation:** The video reportedly shows the cleaning business owner explaining to the customer that he had paid the workers "$100" for their services. However, he stated he would only charge the customer "$100" because, as he put it, the "Indian" workers were not counted in his billing.
*   **Owner's Response:** Following the video's viral spread and the ensuing backlash, the cleaning business owner issued a statement. He claimed his comments were "taken out of context" and that his company maintains a "diverse workforce." He further asserted that his employees are "compensated well."
*   **Public Reaction:** The video generated an immediate and strong reaction across social media. Users have called for boycotts of the cleaning business and demanded investigations into its practices by relevant authorities, including the Department of Labor. The primary concerns articulated online revolve around potential wage exploitation and systematic discrimination based on ethnicity.
*   **Allegations:** The core allegations against the business owner include racial profiling, discrimination against a specific ethnic group, and potential wage theft, stemming directly from the implication that workers were not fully accounted for due to their race.

The widespread public reaction underscores ongoing concerns regarding equitable treatment in the workplace and the enforcement of non-discriminatory labor standards. As of the publication of this article, it remains unclear whether official investigations by labor authorities or other governmental bodies have been formally initiated in response to the public outcry and allegations. The situation continues to draw significant public attention, highlighting the demand for transparency and accountability in employment practices.
