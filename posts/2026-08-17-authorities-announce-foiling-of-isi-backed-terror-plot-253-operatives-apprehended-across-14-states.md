Indian security agencies have announced the successful foiling of a significant terror plot, leading to the apprehension of 253 operatives across 14 states. Authorities stated that the network was backed by Pakistan's Inter-Services Intelligence (ISI) and was allegedly planning disruptive activities targeting the nation's Independence Day celebrations. The comprehensive operation reportedly dismantled a widespread conspiracy, preventing potential acts of terrorism.

The announcement highlights a major achievement in national security efforts, underscoring the ongoing threat posed by state-sponsored terrorism. The scale of the operation, spanning nearly half of India's states and involving a large number of arrests, indicates a deeply entrenched and well-coordinated network. Officials emphasized that the timely intervention prevented threats to public safety and critical infrastructure that might have been targeted during high-profile national events.

Key details released by authorities include:

*   **Operative Count:** A total of 253 individuals identified as operatives of the ISI-backed ring were apprehended.
*   **Geographic Scope:** The arrests and investigative actions took place across 14 different states, demonstrating the pan-Indian reach of the terror network. While specific state names were not immediately released, the breadth of the operation signifies a national-level threat.
*   **Target:** The primary objective of the foiled plot was reportedly to disrupt India's Independence Day observances, which traditionally involve large public gatherings and high-security events.
*   **Foreign Linkage:** The explicit mention of ISI backing points to external support and direction for the terror activities, raising concerns about cross-border subversive efforts.
*   **Preventative Measures:** The arrests were the culmination of extensive intelligence gathering and coordinated law enforcement actions, designed to neutralize the threat before it could materialize.

The foiling of this plot is seen as a significant blow to terror outfits attempting to destabilize the region. Security analysts suggest that such extensive networks often involve recruitment, radicalization, logistics, and planning for various types of attacks. The scale of the arrests suggests that the network likely possessed capabilities for multiple types of operations.

Investigations into the apprehended operatives and the broader network are ongoing. Authorities are expected to continue their efforts to trace financial linkages, uncover handler identities, and identify any remaining sleeper cells or associated individuals. The focus remains on maintaining heightened vigilance and reinforcing security measures, particularly around key national events and vulnerable areas. Further details are anticipated as the investigative process progresses.
