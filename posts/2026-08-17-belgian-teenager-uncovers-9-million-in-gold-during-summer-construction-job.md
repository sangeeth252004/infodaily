The significant find, initially dismissed as a mere €1 coin, occurred beneath an old building slated for demolition. The construction company employing the student subsequently issued an official announcement confirming the details of the discovery.

The student, whose identity has not been publicly disclosed, was engaged in routine excavation and demolition activities when the metallic objects were first encountered. Reports from the company indicate that the initial assessment of the find was conservative, with the student believing he had stumbled upon a common euro coin. Further investigation at the site, prompted by the unusual nature of the items, revealed a substantial cache comprising numerous gold bars and an assortment of gold coins. The aggregated value of these items has been professionally assessed at an estimated €9 million, based on current market prices for gold.
