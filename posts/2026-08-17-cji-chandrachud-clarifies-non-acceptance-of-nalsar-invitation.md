The statement, made recently, dispelled prior speculation and reports suggesting his potential presence at an upcoming program at the prominent law institution. The Chief Justice's office emphasized that without an official acceptance, there was no question of his attendance.

The clarification addresses growing discussions surrounding the Chief Justice's potential engagement with NALSAR. Such an appearance by the country's top judicial figure at a leading national law university would typically be a significant event, drawing considerable attention from students, faculty, and the broader legal community. His non-attendance, now officially confirmed, concludes any anticipation built around the possibility.

The specifics of the invitation, including the exact nature of the event and its scheduled date, had not been widely detailed publicly prior to the CJI’s statement. However, the Chief Justice’s unequivocal denial of acceptance indicates that any discussions or proposals for his participation did not culminate in a formal commitment. This position underscores the meticulous protocol followed regarding the public engagements of high judicial office holders.

*   **Individual Concerned:** Chief Justice of India Dr. D.Y. Chandrachud.
*   **Institution Involved:** NALSAR University of Law, located in Hyderabad, Telangana.
*   **The Clarification:** CJI Chandrachud explicitly stated he "never accepted" the invitation, thereby confirming no plan to attend.
*   **Context:** The statement was made to address ongoing speculation or reports in various circles regarding his attendance at an unspecified event.
