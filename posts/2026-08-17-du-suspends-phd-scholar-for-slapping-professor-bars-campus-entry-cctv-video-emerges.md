Delhi University (DU) has taken decisive disciplinary action, suspending a PhD scholar and barring their entry onto the university campus. The suspension follows an alleged incident where the scholar reportedly slapped a professor. Reports indicate that closed-circuit television (CCTV) footage capturing the altercation has emerged, playing a significant role in the university's swift response.

The suspension order, issued recently, mandates an immediate prohibition for the scholar from accessing any university premises. This includes academic departments, libraries, hostels, and recreational areas. The decision underscores the university's firm stance against any form of physical assault or misconduct within its academic environment, particularly in interactions between students and faculty members.

The incident reportedly involved a physical altercation between the PhD scholar and a professor on the university campus. While specific details regarding the exact location or departmental affiliation of those involved have not been publicly detailed by the university, the gravity of the situation prompted immediate administrative intervention. The surfacing of CCTV video footage has provided critical evidence for the university administration to act, corroborating the allegations of physical assault. This digital evidence is understood to be a key factor in the rapid issuance of the disciplinary measures.

University officials have reiterated their commitment to maintaining a safe, respectful, and conducive learning and working environment for all students, faculty, and staff. Such incidents are considered serious breaches of conduct, violating the principles of academic decorum and mutual respect. The institution's regulations typically outline severe penalties for actions that threaten the physical safety or well-being of any member of the university community.

The suspension is understood to be an initial step, likely pending a more comprehensive internal inquiry into the matter. A formal investigation committee may be constituted to review all evidence, including the CCTV footage, and gather statements from involved parties and witnesses. Depending on the findings of this inquiry, further disciplinary actions, which could include expulsion, may be considered in line with the university's statutes and ordinances governing student conduct. The university is expected to uphold its internal processes to ensure a fair and thorough examination of the incident.
