The appointment signals a commitment to scrutinize law enforcement conduct during public demonstrations.

The committee's mandate is to thoroughly examine the circumstances surrounding the police intervention, assess the legality and proportionality of actions taken, and determine accountability for any potential misconduct. The "Parliament march," which involved various groups and individuals, drew attention due to its timing and the subsequent allegations of police missteps.

Details surrounding the formation of the inquiry committee indicate a directive for an impartial investigation into the events of September 17, 2023. On that day, Qazi Faez Isa formally assumed the office of the Chief Justice of Pakistan. Reports suggest that during the parallel "Parliament march" in India, police employed various measures to manage the demonstration, leading to subsequent allegations of excessive force, arbitrary detentions, or obstruction of peaceful assembly.
