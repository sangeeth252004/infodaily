NEW DELHI – India's Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) on Tuesday affirmed the robust nature and global credibility of the nation's electoral process and institutions. The statement from MEA Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal came in response to former U.S. President Donald Trump's recent commendation of India's Chief Election Commissioner (CEC), Gyanesh Kumar.

Speaking to the press in New Delhi, Jaiswal underscored that India's electoral framework has a well-established and globally recognized reputation for integrity and fairness. "Our electoral processes, our institutions, have credibility well established globally. We have a very strong track record and robust and time-tested processes," Jaiswal stated, emphasizing the democratic foundations of the country.

The MEA's comments follow reports of Donald Trump expressing appreciation for CEC Gyanesh Kumar, particularly in the context of India's recently concluded general elections. While specific details of Trump's praise were not elaborated upon by the MEA, the Indian government's response highlighted the systemic strength of its democratic mechanisms rather than focusing on an individual.

India, recognized as the world's largest democracy, successfully concluded its multi-phase general elections (Lok Sabha elections) on June 1, with results declared on June 4. The extensive electoral exercise involved approximately 968 million eligible voters across more than 1 million polling stations nationwide. The Election Commission of India (ECI), a constitutional body, is tasked with the supervision, direction, and control of elections, ensuring their free and fair conduct.

*   **Independent Body:** The ECI functions as an independent and neutral constitutional authority, safeguarding the integrity of the democratic process.
*   **Vast Scale:** The recent general elections were conducted over 44 days, involving millions of election officials and security personnel.
*   **International Observation:** India's electoral processes frequently draw international attention due to their unprecedented scale and complexity, often receiving commendation from global observers and democratic institutions.

The MEA's statement reaffirms India's consistent position regarding the independence and impartiality of its electoral body. It underscores the nation's confidence in its democratic framework, which has overseen numerous transitions of power since independence. The exchange also highlights the continued global scrutiny and interest in India's democratic practices, especially from international political figures.

Looking ahead, India's democratic institutions are expected to continue their functions as per constitutional mandates, with the Election Commission playing a pivotal role in maintaining electoral integrity. The government continues to uphold the established processes that have earned international recognition over decades.
