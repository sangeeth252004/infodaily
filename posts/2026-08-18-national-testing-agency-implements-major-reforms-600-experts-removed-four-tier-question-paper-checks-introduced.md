The National Testing Agency (NTA) has initiated a substantial reform of its examination procedures, including the removal of approximately 600 experts from its existing panel and the implementation of a new four-tier system for scrutinizing question papers. These measures follow recent allegations of paper leaks and irregularities in examinations conducted by the agency, prompting a comprehensive review of its operational protocols. The objective of these changes is to enhance the integrity, security, and fairness of national entrance and eligibility tests across India.

Under the new directives, the NTA has undertaken a complete re-evaluation of its entire panel of subject matter experts and paper setters. The previous pool, comprising specialists responsible for drafting and reviewing question papers, has been disbanded. The agency plans to onboard a new set of experts who will undergo rigorous vetting processes to ensure their credibility and competence. This widespread change aims to eliminate potential vulnerabilities associated with the previous system and introduce a fresh approach to content development.

Central to the NTA's reforms is the introduction of a multi-layered verification mechanism for question papers. This four-tier checking system is designed to identify and rectify errors, ensure syllabus alignment, maintain appropriate difficulty levels, and prevent any unauthorized access or leaks before the examinations. The move signifies a concerted effort to fortify the security infrastructure surrounding high-stakes tests.

Key components of the new question paper scrutiny system include:

*   **Tier 1: Subject Expert Panel:** This initial stage involves a group of subject matter experts responsible for drafting the question papers. Their primary role is to ensure accuracy, relevance, and adherence to the prescribed syllabus.
*   **Tier 2: Moderation Committee:** Following the initial draft, a moderation committee reviews the paper for balance, ambiguity, grammatical errors, and overall quality. This committee ensures that questions are clear, unambiguous, and fairly assess candidates' knowledge.
*   **Tier 3: High-Level Scrutiny Committee:** A more senior committee conducts a final, comprehensive review of the entire question paper. This tier focuses on security aspects, potential for misinterpretation, and ensuring that the paper is tamper-proof.
*   **Tier 4: Technology Expert Committee:** This committee specifically leverages technological tools, including artificial intelligence (AI), to flag any anomalies or potential patterns that might indicate compromise or previous exposure. This tier adds an advanced layer of digital security and intelligence to the process.

In addition to these structural changes, the NTA is enhancing security features at examination centers. These include mandatory two-level security audits for all centers and the deployment of new technological solutions to monitor and prevent irregularities. The agency is also planning to establish an independent high-level standing committee to offer continuous recommendations on improving its procedures.

These reforms by the NTA are intended to restore public confidence in the national examination system. The agency’s stated commitment is to ensure that future examinations are conducted transparently, securely, and with the highest degree of fairness for all aspirants. The implementation of these new protocols will be critical for upcoming examination cycles.
