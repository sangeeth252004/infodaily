Energy consumption at public electric vehicle (EV) charging stations across India has surged by 72% in the past year, according to recent data released by Statiq, a prominent EV charging network provider. This substantial increase underscores the steady growth in electric vehicle adoption and the expanding utilization of public charging infrastructure within the country.

The data, comparing the first quarter of fiscal year 2023 (Q1 FY23) with the first quarter of fiscal year 2024 (Q1 FY24), reveals a significant uptick in charging activity. This trend highlights a pivotal shift in consumer behavior and an increasing reliance on public charging solutions to support the burgeoning EV ecosystem. The rise in consumption volume at public chargers directly correlates with the overall expansion of the EV sector, which has reportedly seen over 50% year-on-year growth.

Key details from the data indicate:
*   **Total Energy Consumption:** Energy consumed at public chargers increased from 3.6 million kilowatt-hours (kWh) in Q1 FY23 to 6.2 million kWh in Q1 FY24.
*   **Network Expansion:** Statiq's charging network itself expanded by 33% during the same comparative period, demonstrating ongoing investment in infrastructure development.
*   **Utilization Rates:** The increased consumption suggests a higher utilization rate per charging point, indicating that existing and new infrastructure is being actively used by EV owners.
*   **Geographic Spread:** Growth in EV usage and public charging is not confined to metropolitan areas, with a notable rise observed in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, signaling wider market penetration.

This surge in public charging usage reflects several contributing factors. Government incentives aimed at promoting EV adoption, such as purchase subsidies and tax benefits, have played a role in accelerating the transition from internal combustion engine vehicles. Additionally, the introduction of a wider range of EV models across various segments, coupled with growing environmental awareness among consumers, has spurred demand. The continuous expansion and improved reliability of charging networks are also critical in alleviating "range anxiety," a common concern among potential EV buyers.

Looking ahead, the upward trajectory in public EV charging consumption is expected to continue. Industry stakeholders anticipate further investments in charging infrastructure, including the deployment of more fast chargers and the integration of smart charging technologies. These developments are crucial for supporting the projected growth of India's EV fleet, which remains a key component of the nation's broader sustainable transportation and decarbonization goals. The sustained increase in public charging usage will be a vital indicator of the EV market's maturity and its long-term viability.
