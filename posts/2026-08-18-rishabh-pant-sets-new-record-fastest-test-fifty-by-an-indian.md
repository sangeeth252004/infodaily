Bengaluru, India – Indian wicketkeeper-batsman Rishabh Pant etched his name into the annals of Test cricket history on March 13, 2022, by smashing the fastest half-century ever recorded by an Indian player in the longest format of the game. Pant achieved the milestone during the second Test match against Sri Lanka at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru, reaching his fifty off an astonishing 28 deliveries.

The left-handed batsman’s blistering innings played a pivotal role in India's dominant performance during the day-night Test. His aggressive approach not only captivated the audience but also put immense pressure on the Sri Lankan bowlers, shifting the momentum firmly in India's favor. Pant’s performance underscored his reputation as a dynamic and fearless cricketer capable of turning matches on their head with his explosive batting.

Pant's record-breaking knock surpassed the long-standing record held by legendary all-rounder Kapil Dev, who had scored a 30-ball fifty against Pakistan in Karachi back in 1982. The achievement solidified Pant's status as a generational talent, known for his ability to score rapidly and dictate the pace of an innings, regardless of the match situation. His fearless brand of cricket has frequently drawn comparisons to some of the game's most exciting players.

Key details surrounding Rishabh Pant's historic achievement include:
*   **Date:** March 13, 2022
*   **Opponent:** Sri Lanka
*   **Venue:** M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru (Day-Night Test)
*   **Balls Faced for Fifty:** 28 deliveries
*   **Previous Record Holder:** Kapil Dev (30 balls vs Pakistan, Karachi, 1982)
*   **Overall Match Impact:** Pant's quickfire 50 contributed significantly to India's total, helping them secure a comprehensive victory by 238 runs in the second Test, sealing a 2-0 series whitewash against Sri Lanka.

His innings of 50 runs, which included seven boundaries and two sixes, came at a strike rate of 178.57, highlighting the sheer dominance he exerted at the crease. Beyond this particular record, Pant has been lauded for his innovative strokeplay and his ability to score runs in crucial situations, often rescuing India from precarious positions. His career has also seen him achieve the rare feat of scoring Test centuries in both England and Australia as a wicketkeeper, further cementing his place among India's elite cricketers.

Following this record-breaking feat, Rishabh Pant continued to be a central figure in the Indian Test team, with his performances consistently demonstrating his unique skill set as a wicketkeeper-batsman. The record remains a testament to his attacking philosophy and his capacity to deliver impact performances under pressure, showcasing his ability to redefine traditional Test match batting.
