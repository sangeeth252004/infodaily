His renewed focus on noncitizen voting seeks to highlight a particular aspect of election eligibility and integrity, potentially influencing ongoing debates surrounding voter ID laws and electoral processes.

While isolated instances of ineligible individuals attempting to vote have occurred, analyses by non-partisan organizations and academic researchers have consistently indicated that noncitizen voting is rare and not widespread enough to influence election results. For example, a 2017 study by the Brennan Center for Justice characterized the rate of noncitizen voting as "infinitesimal."

Mr. Trump's continued emphasis on alleged election irregularities, particularly concerning noncitizen votes, remains a key component of his political platform as he campaigns for the 2024 presidency. Such statements aim to rally his support base and keep the issue of election integrity at the forefront of political discourse, influencing legislative discussions on voting procedures and voter eligibility nationwide.
