Indian cricketer Virat Kohli has completed eighteen years in professional cricket, a significant milestone acknowledged across the sport. Having made his first-class debut for Delhi in July 2006, this period highlights a career distinguished by consistent performance, record-breaking achievements, and significant leadership roles at both domestic and international levels. The ongoing duration of his career underscores his sustained presence and influence in global cricket.

Kohli’s journey began with his first-class debut at the age of 17, representing Delhi against Tamil Nadu. His international debut followed in August 2008, where he played his first One Day International (ODI) for India against Sri Lanka. This progression marked the beginning of a prolific international career that saw him become a central figure in the Indian team across all three formats of the game. His initial years were characterized by a rapid ascent, demonstrating a blend of aggressive batting and a strong temperament that would define his play.

Over the past eighteen years, Kohli has accumulated numerous records and accolades. He has captained India across Tests, ODIs, and Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is), leading the team to significant victories and achieving the number one ranking in Test cricket. His batting statistics place him among the sport's all-time leading run-scorers, particularly noted for his exceptional consistency in limited-overs cricket and his ability to perform under pressure. His pursuit and breaking of long-standing records, such as surpassing Sachin Tendulkar's tally of 49 ODI centuries, have been focal points of his career narrative.

Key career highlights and statistics include:

*   **International Centuries:** As of late 2023, Kohli holds the record for most centuries in One Day International cricket, surpassing 50 ODI hundreds. He also has significant century counts in Test matches and T20Is.
*   **Run Aggregates:** He is one of the few cricketers to have scored over 8,000 runs in Test cricket, over 13,000 runs in ODIs, and over 4,000 runs in T20 Internationals.
*   **ICC Awards:** Recipient of multiple ICC Player of the Year awards, including the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for ICC Player of the Decade (2010s). He has also won the ICC ODI Player of the Year award multiple times.
*   **Captaincy:** Led India in 68 Tests, 95 ODIs, and 50 T20Is. Under his captaincy, India achieved a historic Test series victory in Australia in 2018-19.
*   **Tournament Performance:** Holds the record for the most runs scored in a single edition of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup, achieved in 2014 and 2022. He was also the Player of the Tournament in the 2014 and 2016 T20 World Cups.
*   **Domestic Honours:** Captained India to victory in the 2008 ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup.

His sustained presence at the highest level of the sport continues to be a subject of observation, as he remains an active member of the Indian cricket team. Kohli's career progression demonstrates an athlete's ability to maintain peak performance and adapt to the evolving demands of international cricket over nearly two decades. His ongoing participation in future tournaments and series will continue to add to his extensive professional legacy.
