IndiGo, India's largest airline, experienced a significant technical issue with its official website and mobile application on Thursday, July 25, 2024. The disruption led to temporary inconveniences for customers attempting to make new flight bookings, modify existing reservations, and complete online check-ins. The airline acknowledged the incident through an official statement, confirming the technical difficulties.

The glitch reportedly began affecting users in the morning hours, rendering key online services inaccessible for a period. Passengers attempting to access IndiGo's digital platforms encountered error messages or slow loading times, preventing them from proceeding with essential travel preparations. This included difficulties in retrieving booking information, generating boarding passes, and conducting other self-service functions typically available online.

IndiGo promptly issued a statement confirming the system anomaly. The airline communicated that its Information Technology (IT) team was actively engaged in identifying the root cause and implementing a resolution plan to restore full functionality as quickly as possible. During the period of disruption, IndiGo advised affected passengers facing urgent travel requirements to consider arriving earlier at the airport for manual check-in processes, where staff could assist with necessary formalities.

The scope of the disruption affected a segment of IndiGo's extensive customer base. Reports of difficulties surfaced across various social media platforms, indicating a notable impact on passengers planning to travel on July 25 and those attempting to manage future bookings. IndiGo's immediate response focused on mitigating customer inconvenience and restoring operational stability.

By afternoon, many users reported that services were gradually being restored, indicating progress in the airline's efforts to resolve the technical problem. While a complete timeline for the full restoration of all services was not immediately detailed, the airline's focus remained on ensuring system stability and minimizing further passenger impact.
