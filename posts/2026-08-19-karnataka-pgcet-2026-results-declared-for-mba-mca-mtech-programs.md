Bengaluru, Karnataka – July 15, 2026 – The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) officially declared the results for the Post Graduate Common Entrance Test (PGCET) 2026 today, July 15, 2026. Aspirants seeking admission to Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Computer Applications (MCA), and Master of Technology (MTech) programs across various institutions in Karnataka can now access their scores and ranks through the KEA's designated online portals.

The PGCET 2026 serves as a crucial gateway for postgraduate admissions into government, aided, unaided, and private professional colleges across the state. The declaration of results marks a significant step for these candidates as they move closer to securing their preferred academic placements.

To check their results, candidates are advised to visit the official KEA websites, which include kea.kar.nic.in or cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea. Upon navigating to the results section, candidates will be required to enter their PGCET application number and date of birth to retrieve their statement of marks and rank card. The result displays the candidate's total score, individual subject scores, and their overall rank, which will be instrumental in the subsequent counselling rounds.

The KEA is expected to release the detailed schedule for the PGCET 2026 counselling process in the coming days. This multi-stage process will include:
*   **Document Verification:** Mandatory for all eligible candidates to confirm their academic qualifications and other necessary credentials.
*   **Option Entry:** Candidates will be required to choose their preferred courses and colleges based on their rank and availability.
*   **Seat Allotment:** Based on merit (rank), preferences, and reservation criteria.
*   **Reporting to Colleges:** Selected candidates must report to their allotted institutions within the stipulated timeframe to complete admission formalities.

The ranks obtained in PGCET 2026 are a critical factor determining a candidate's eligibility and prospects for admission into their desired postgraduate programs. The KEA will publish comprehensive guidelines and dates for each stage of the counselling, including eligibility criteria, fee structures, and the list of participating institutions and their seat matrix. Candidates are strongly advised to regularly monitor the KEA official website for the latest updates and announcements regarding the counselling process to ensure they do not miss any critical deadlines. Preparation for document verification with all original certificates and testimonials is recommended for all successful candidates.
