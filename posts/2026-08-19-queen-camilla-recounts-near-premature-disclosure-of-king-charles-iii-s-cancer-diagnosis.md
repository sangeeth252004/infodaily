Queen Camilla recently disclosed that she nearly revealed King Charles III's cancer diagnosis days before Buckingham Palace officially announced the King's condition to the public. The Queen's remarks were made during an appearance at the second annual Queen's Reading Room Literary Festival, held at Hampton Court Palace.

Speaking to attendees, Queen Camilla recounted the period preceding the February 5, 2024, announcement, stating she "nearly let the cat out of the bag" regarding the King's health. Her comment, delivered in a lighthearted manner, provided insight into the intense discretion and pressure surrounding royal health matters. The King's diagnosis followed a procedure for a benign prostate enlargement in January, during which a separate issue of concern was identified and subsequent diagnostic tests confirmed a form of cancer. Buckingham Palace confirmed at the time that it was not prostate cancer, without specifying the type.

The revelation underscores the delicate balance between royal privacy and public interest, particularly concerning the health of the reigning monarch. Following the official announcement, King Charles III began a schedule of regular treatments. During this period, he has largely postponed public-facing duties on medical advice, though he has continued with certain constitutional and state responsibilities. These include weekly private audiences with the Prime Minister and receiving official documents.

Key details regarding King Charles III's diagnosis and ongoing royal duties:

*   **February 5, 2024:** Buckingham Palace officially announced King Charles III's cancer diagnosis.
*   **January 2024:** King Charles underwent treatment for a benign prostate enlargement, during which a separate issue was identified, leading to the cancer diagnosis.
*   **Treatment Course:** The King commenced regular treatments immediately following the diagnosis.
*   **Public Engagements:** King Charles has suspended most public-facing engagements but continues to fulfill essential constitutional duties.
*   **Queen Camilla's Role:** Since the King's diagnosis, Queen Camilla has maintained a full schedule of engagements, often representing the monarchy at public events and providing visible support.

The Queen's comment highlights the human aspect behind the often-formal machinery of the monarchy. The period leading up to the official announcement would have been one of significant concern and careful management for the Royal Household. Public communication regarding the health of a monarch is typically handled with extreme caution, aimed at providing clarity while maintaining an appropriate level of privacy.

King Charles III publicly expressed his gratitude for the messages of support he received following his diagnosis, stating that they had been "the greatest comfort and encouragement." The King has also used his experience to raise awareness about cancer screening, encouraging men to get checked.

As King Charles III continues his treatment, Queen Camilla remains a prominent figure, fulfilling her royal duties and maintaining a visible presence for the monarchy. The Royal Household continues to provide updates on the King's health as deemed appropriate, emphasizing his positive outlook and continued dedication to his duties. The monarch's long-term health plan remains under the guidance of his medical team, with further information regarding his return to a full schedule of public engagements expected to be released as his treatment progresses.
