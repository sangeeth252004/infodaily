Former Supreme Court Justice R. Subhash Reddy has been appointed to head an independent inquiry into the actions taken during a recent march towards Parliament by a group identified as CJP. The appointment aims to ascertain the full facts and circumstances surrounding the incident, focusing on the conduct of all parties involved.

The decision to initiate an independent probe, led by a retired apex court judge, underscores the significance placed on transparency and accountability regarding public demonstrations and law enforcement responses. Justice Reddy's role will involve a thorough examination of the events that transpired during the CJP Parliament march, which drew public and media attention.

Such inquiries are typically established to provide an unbiased assessment of complex situations, especially those involving interactions between civil society groups and security forces. The appointment of a former Supreme Court Justice to lead this investigation is intended to ensure impartiality and to instill public confidence in the findings. Justice Reddy brings a wealth of judicial experience to the role, having served on the Supreme Court of India.

Key details surrounding the inquiry include:
*   **Head of Inquiry:** Justice R. Subhash Reddy, former judge of the Supreme Court of India.
*   **Subject of Probe:** Actions taken during the CJP Parliament march. This will likely encompass the conduct of law enforcement agencies, the actions of the protestors, and adherence to established protocols for public assemblies and demonstrations.
*   **Mandate:** To investigate and report on the factual matrix, circumstances, and any potential procedural or legal infringements.
*   **Objective:** To establish an objective record of events, which can then inform future policy, legal proceedings, or administrative actions.

The Parliament march, organized by CJP, was reportedly a significant gathering intended to bring specific issues to the attention of lawmakers and the public. The subsequent "actions" that are now subject to inquiry could refer to measures taken by authorities to manage, control, or disperse the march, or other specific incidents that occurred during the event. An inquiry of this nature often examines aspects such as the proportionality of force used, if any, the legality of detentions, and the overall management of the public assembly.

The findings of the inquiry are expected to be critical for understanding the dynamics of public protest in the capital and the operational procedures adopted by law enforcement. The report submitted by Justice Reddy will likely include detailed observations, evidence analysis, and recommendations aimed at preventing future recurrences of issues or improving the handling of similar events. While a specific timeline for the submission of the report has not been detailed, such inquiries typically operate within a defined period to ensure timely resolution and provide clarity to all stakeholders. This development is a crucial step towards ensuring accountability and upholding democratic principles related to freedom of assembly and expression.
