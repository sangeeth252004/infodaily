The FIFA Disciplinary Committee has announced a 10-match ban for Argentina midfielder Leandro Paredes, citing unsporting behavior during the intensely contested 2022 FIFA World Cup final against France. The suspension applies to all international matches and follows an investigation into player conduct during the high-stakes encounter, which took place on December 18, 2022, at Lusail Stadium in Qatar.

The decision addresses an incident during the final moments of regular time and extra time, which saw multiple players from both national teams involved in heated exchanges and confrontations. Paredes’ actions, particularly his involvement in a scuffle near the French bench, were highlighted as a contributing factor to the disciplinary ruling. The confrontation escalated after Paredes reportedly kicked the ball towards the French dugout, sparking a strong reaction from French players, including forward Randal Kolo Muani, and leading to a broader on-field altercation involving several players, such as Argentina's Nicolas Otamendi.

This significant penalty underscores FIFA's ongoing commitment to maintaining discipline and fair play within international football competitions. Such bans are intended to deter players from engaging in conduct deemed detrimental to the spirit of the game, especially in high-profile matches like a World Cup final. The ban means Paredes will be unavailable for a substantial portion of Argentina's upcoming international fixtures, including World Cup qualifiers, friendly matches, and potentially segments of future continental competitions, depending on the timing of those events.

Key details surrounding the FIFA Disciplinary Committee's decision include:
*   **Player Affected:** Leandro Paredes (Argentina national team).
*   **Duration of Ban:** 10 international matches.
*   **Reason:** Unsporting behavior.
*   **Event:** 2022 FIFA World Cup final, Argentina vs. France.
*   **Location:** Lusail Stadium, Qatar.
*   **Specific Actions Cited:** Involvement in a confrontation with French players, reportedly initiated by kicking the ball towards the French bench.
*   **Impact:** Renders Paredes ineligible for forthcoming FIFA-sanctioned international games.

The absence of Paredes, a central figure in Argentina's midfield during their triumphant World Cup campaign, will pose a challenge for national team coach Lionel Scaloni as he prepares for upcoming fixtures. The midfielder, who has consistently been a part of Argentina's squad, provides experience and defensive solidity. His unavailability will necessitate adjustments in squad selection and tactical approaches.

While the specific details of the full disciplinary report have not been extensively publicized beyond the ban's announcement, standard FIFA procedures allow for an appeals process. The Argentina Football Association (AFA) has the option to challenge the ruling, presenting their case to FIFA’s Appeal Committee within a specified timeframe. Should an an appeal be lodged, a review of the evidence and the Disciplinary Committee's findings would ensue.

The incident during the World Cup final was one of several moments of heightened tension during a match celebrated for its dramatic conclusion. FIFA has previously initiated disciplinary proceedings against other teams and federations for player and fan conduct during the tournament, signaling a broader effort to enforce regulations. This latest ruling on Paredes serves as a direct consequence of individual player actions that were deemed to have crossed disciplinary lines. The football world now awaits any potential appeal from the AFA and will observe how Argentina navigates its upcoming international schedule without one of its key players.
