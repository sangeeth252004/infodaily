Two individuals sustained non-life-threatening injuries following a stabbing incident at Gurdwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha in Edmonton, Alberta, on Saturday, June 29. The Edmonton Police Service (EPS) confirmed the arrest of a suspect, Kevin Johnson, 30, on Sunday, June 30, less than 24 hours after his release from federal prison.

The incident occurred during morning prayers at the Sikh house of worship located in the Mill Woods area of Edmonton. Police responded to reports of an assault, where officers found two injured victims. Both individuals received medical attention for their injuries.

According to police statements, Johnson had been released from prison on Friday, June 28, after completing a seven-year sentence for manslaughter. His swift re-arrest in connection with a new violent crime has prompted public discussion regarding the processes for monitoring and re-integrating individuals with serious criminal histories into the community.

*   **Victims:** A 66-year-old priest and a 62-year-old male congregant were identified as the injured parties. Both were transported to a local hospital and are reported to be in stable condition.
*   **Suspect:** Kevin Johnson, 30, was taken into custody on Sunday, June 30.
*   **Charges:** Johnson faces two counts of aggravated assault, one count of possession of a weapon, and one count of breach of a probation order.
*   **Incident Classification:** Authorities have stated that the incident is not currently being investigated as a hate crime.

The attack has generated significant concern within the local Sikh community and among the wider public. Community leaders have emphasized the importance of ensuring safety and security within places of worship and have called for a thorough review of support systems for recently released offenders. Statements from congregants expressed shock and distress over the violence occurring in a sacred space.

Johnson remains in police custody pending a court appearance. The Edmonton Police Service has indicated that its investigation into the circumstances surrounding the stabbing is ongoing. Further details are expected to emerge as the legal process advances.
