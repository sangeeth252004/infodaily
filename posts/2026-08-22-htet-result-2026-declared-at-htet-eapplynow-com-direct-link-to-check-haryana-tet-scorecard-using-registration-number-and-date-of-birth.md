The Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH) has officially declared the results for the Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) 2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination can now access their scorecards directly on the official website, htet.eapplynow.com. The results were published today, making individual performance accessible through a dedicated portal requiring the candidate's registration number and date of birth.

The HTET examination is a mandatory qualification for individuals aspiring to teach in government and private schools across the state of Haryana. It assesses a candidate's eligibility for three distinct levels of teaching positions: Primary Teacher (PRT) for classes 1-5 (Level 1), Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT) for classes 6-8 (Level 2), and Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) for classes 9-12 (Level 3). The 2026 examination cycle, conducted earlier this year, saw a substantial number of candidates participate across all three levels. Successful qualification in HTET is a prerequisite for candidates to apply for teaching vacancies released by various educational authorities in Haryana.

The declaration of the HTET 2026 results marks a crucial phase for thousands of aspiring teachers in Haryana. Qualified candidates will now possess a valid HTET certificate, which is typically valid for a specified period, enabling them to apply for forthcoming recruitment drives. The examination aims to ensure a standardized benchmark for teacher quality, contributing to the overall educational standards within the state. The rigorous testing process evaluates candidates on subjects including Child Development and Pedagogy, Language (Hindi and English), General Studies, Mathematics, Environmental Studies, and specific subject knowledge for TGT and PGT levels.

To check their HTET 2026 scorecard, candidates are advised to follow these steps:

*   **Visit the official website:** Navigate to htet.eapplynow.com.
*   **Locate the result link:** Click on the prominent link labeled "HTET Result 2026" or similar.
*   **Enter credentials:** Input the registration number and date of birth in the designated fields.
*   **Submit and view:** Click the "Submit" button to view the scorecard, which will display marks obtained in each section and the overall qualification status.
*   **Download and print:** Candidates should download and print their scorecard for future reference and verification during recruitment processes.

The HTET certificate is a vital document for those seeking employment in Haryana's educational sector. The Board of School Education Haryana oversees the conduct of the examination, ensuring transparency and fairness in the assessment process. With the results now declared, qualified candidates are poised to move forward with their career aspirations, awaiting further announcements regarding teacher recruitment campaigns by the state government and other educational institutions. The validity period for the HTET certificate will be specified on the scorecard or by official BSEH notifications, guiding candidates on its applicability for future job applications.
