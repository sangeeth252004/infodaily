The primary concern following the incident has been the status and welfare of the 24 individuals who were on board the vessel at the time of its submersion.

Upon receiving distress information or notification of the sinking, maritime search and rescue (SAR) operations were initiated. Naval and Coast Guard assets from regional jurisdictions, including India, are typically deployed to respond to such incidents in their broader area of responsibility.
