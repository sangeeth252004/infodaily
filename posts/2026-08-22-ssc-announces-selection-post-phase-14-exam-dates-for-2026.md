NEW DELHI – The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially released the examination schedule for the Selection Post Phase 14 exam 2026. The announcement, made on November 15, 2025, on the Commission's official website, outlines key dates for the application process and the Computer Based Examination, scheduled to take place in April and May 2026. This notification is critical for lakhs of aspirants seeking recruitment to various Group B and Group C positions across different ministries, departments, and organizations of the Government of India.

The Selection Post examination is a significant recruitment drive conducted annually by the SSC, targeting candidates with diverse educational qualifications ranging from Matriculation to Graduation and above. The Phase 14 iteration for 2026 will fill a wide array of vacancies, each with specific eligibility criteria tailored to the post's requirements. The timely release of the examination calendar provides candidates with adequate time to prepare for the rigorous selection process, which typically involves a Computer Based Examination followed by skill tests, physical endurance tests, or document verification, depending on the nature of the post.

The notification specifies that the online application window for the Selection Post Phase 14 exam 2026 will commence on December 1, 2025, and conclude on January 15, 2026. Candidates are advised to submit their applications well in advance to avoid last-minute technical difficulties. The Computer Based Examination is provisionally scheduled from April 25, 2026, to May 5, 2026. This period allows for the conduct of examinations across multiple shifts and days, accommodating the large number of applicants.

Key dates and information released by the SSC include:

*   **Online Application Submission Window:** December 1, 2025, to January 15, 2026
*   **Last Date for Online Fee Payment:** January 18, 2026
*   **Dates for Computer Based Examination:** April 25, 2026, to May 5, 2026
*   **Admit Card Availability:** Expected in the first week of April 2026
*   **Official Website for Updates:** Candidates must refer to ssc.nic.in for the detailed notification, syllabus, scheme of examination, and other crucial updates.

Candidates are strongly advised to meticulously review the official notification (Advertisement No. Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts) for specific details pertaining to educational qualifications, age limits, post codes, application procedures, and examination patterns for each category of posts. The Commission emphasizes that all eligibility conditions must be met by the specified cut-off dates. Any discrepancies found during the scrutiny of applications or at subsequent stages will lead to disqualification.

Moving forward, eligible candidates are encouraged to intensify their preparation in accordance with the syllabus and examination scheme outlined by the SSC. Regular visits to the official website are recommended to stay informed about any further announcements, including admit card release, answer keys, and result declarations. The successful conduct of the Phase 14 exam 2026 will pave the way for numerous individuals to secure stable employment in various central government roles.
