Chennai, India – The investigation into the murder of a woman whose decomposed body was discovered in a trolley bag aboard the Tamil Nadu Express at Chennai Central railway station on July 17 has intensified, with police focusing their search on Odisha. Investigators believe the crime may have originated in Odisha and that the perpetrators have fled back to the state, prompting cross-state cooperation between police forces.

The discovery was made on Monday, July 17, when railway staff conducting routine checks found a large, locked trolley bag in coach S-7 of the New Delhi-Chennai Tamil Nadu Express (Train No 12621) after its arrival at Chennai Central. Upon opening the bag, authorities uncovered the body of a woman, later identified as 45-year-old Swarnalata Sahoo from Salia Sahi in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. The Government Railway Police (GRP) in Chennai immediately launched a murder investigation.

Forensic examination and post-mortem results revealed that Ms. Sahoo died from strangulation and a head injury, with the time of death estimated to be four to five days prior to the body's discovery. This timeline suggests the murder occurred around July 12 or 13. Chennai GRP traced Ms. Sahoo's identity through personal effects found with the body and subsequently contacted her family in Bhubaneswar. Her daughter, Madhusmita Sahoo, traveled to Chennai to identify her mother's remains.

Key details emerging from the ongoing investigation include:

*   **Last Sighting:** Ms. Sahoo was reportedly last seen leaving her Bhubaneswar residence on July 12 in the company of a male friend, informing her family she was traveling to Chennai.
*   **Suspect Profile:** Chennai GRP suspects the male friend, whose identity is being withheld pending investigation, and possibly an accomplice, are involved in the murder.
*   **Crime Scene Theory:** Investigators currently hypothesize that the murder likely took place in the Bhubaneswar or Cuttack area of Odisha. The body was then allegedly concealed in the trolley bag and placed on the train at either Bhubaneswar or a nearby station along the train's route.
*   **Inter-State Search:** A dedicated team from Chennai GRP has been dispatched to Odisha to coordinate with local police departments in Bhubaneswar and Cuttack. The focus of the search is on apprehending the primary suspect(s) who are believed to have returned to Odisha following the incident.
*   **CCTV Analysis:** Police are meticulously reviewing CCTV footage from various railway stations along the train's path, particularly in Odisha, to track the movements of Ms. Sahoo and the suspected individuals.

The Chennai GRP is working closely with Odisha Police to gather intelligence, trace the last known movements of Ms. Sahoo, and locate the individuals of interest. The investigation is ongoing, with authorities focused on piecing together the sequence of events that led to Ms. Sahoo's death and her body being transported across state lines. Further details are expected as the search operations in Odisha continue.
