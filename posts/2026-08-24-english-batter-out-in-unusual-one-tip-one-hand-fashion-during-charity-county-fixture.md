An English batter was recently dismissed in a highly unusual fashion under the "one tip one hand" rule during a charity exhibition segment of a County match, generating considerable discussion across social media and among cricket enthusiasts. The incident, which occurred on August 28, 2024, at Headingley Cricket Ground during a Roses T20 Blast Charity Exhibition played alongside a County Championship fixture between Yorkshire and Lancashire, saw Alex Thompson of Yorkshire declared out after a unique catch.

The "one tip one hand" rule, commonly found in informal or street cricket, dictates that a batter is out if a fielder catches the ball with one hand after it has bounced once. This rule is not part of the official International Cricket Council (ICC) Laws of Cricket governing professional matches, making its application in a County-affiliated event particularly noteworthy. The unusual dismissal immediately captivated spectators both at the ground and online, with video footage of the incident quickly circulating.

The incident unfolded during the seventh over of the exhibition match when Thompson played a defensive shot that looped gently towards mid-on. Liam Davies, representing Lancashire in the charity fixture, moved swiftly, allowing the ball to bounce once before securely catching it with a single hand. Following an appeal, the umpire confirmed the dismissal under the specific rules agreed upon for the charity segment. Thompson, visibly surprised but understanding of the pre-match stipulations, walked off the field, marking one of the most talked-about moments of the county season.

*   **Rule Clarification:** In official cricket, a catch must be taken directly off the bat (or glove if it's part of the bat) without the ball touching the ground, to dismiss a batter. The "one tip one hand" rule, applied in this instance, deviates significantly from standard professional cricket regulations.
