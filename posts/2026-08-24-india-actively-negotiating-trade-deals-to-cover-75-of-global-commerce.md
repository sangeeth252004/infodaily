India is currently engaged in active negotiations for trade agreements with an additional 8-9 groups of countries, with the ambitious goal of expanding its global trade coverage to approximately 75%. This proactive diplomatic and economic initiative aims to integrate India further into the global economy and enhance its international trade footprint, as confirmed by government officials.

The ongoing discussions represent a significant push by India to diversify its trade partners and strengthen its position in global supply chains. These negotiations build upon existing Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) and Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements (CEPAs) that India has already established with various nations and blocs. The objective is to secure improved market access for Indian goods and services, attract foreign investment, and foster economic growth.

Key aspects of India's expanded trade negotiation strategy include:

*   **Broadening Economic Partnerships:** Engaging with diverse geographical and economic blocs to create new avenues for trade and investment.
*   **Targeted Global Coverage:** The declared goal of covering 75% of global trade signifies a strategic intent to become a central player in international commerce.
*   **Enhancing Competitiveness:** Securing favorable terms in these agreements is expected to boost the competitiveness of Indian industries and exporters.
*   **Addressing Supply Chain Resilience:** The agreements are also seen as a mechanism to build more resilient supply chains, reducing dependency on a single region or country.

India has previously finalized significant trade agreements, including those with the United Arab Emirates (UAE) in 2022, which saw a considerable increase in bilateral trade. Other notable agreements include those with Australia, which came into force in December 2022. These existing agreements serve as a framework and demonstrate India’s commitment to trade liberalization.

The current slate of negotiations reportedly involves various regions, though specific partners were not detailed in the official statements regarding the 8-9 groups. However, previous reports and ongoing discussions have indicated India's interest in deepening ties with blocs such as the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), and the European Free Trade Association (EFTA), among others. These negotiations often encompass a wide array of sectors, including goods, services, investment, intellectual property rights, and dispute resolution mechanisms.

The emphasis on new trade agreements underscores the Indian government's focus on export-led growth and enhancing the country's manufacturing capabilities. As these negotiations progress, they are expected to lay the groundwork for increased trade volumes, greater economic integration, and expanded opportunities for Indian businesses on the global stage. The timeline for the conclusion of these individual agreements varies, subject to the complexities of negotiations and the consensus among participating nations.
