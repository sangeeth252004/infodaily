Kashmiri Pandits employed under the Prime Minister's Employment Package for Kashmiri Migrants (PMEP) have recently flagged a resurgence of security threats in the Kashmir Valley, prompting renewed calls for enhanced protection and relocation. Community representatives stated that a perceived increase in hostile activities has heightened anxieties among those living and working in the region, leading to urgent appeals for government intervention.

The Prime Minister's Employment Package, initiated in 2008 and enhanced subsequently, aims to facilitate the return and rehabilitation of Kashmiri Pandits to the valley by providing government jobs. However, the employees, often referred to as 'PM Package employees,' have experienced periods of heightened vulnerability, particularly following a series of targeted killings in recent years that saw several members of the community, as well as non-local workers, targeted by militants. These incidents had previously led to widespread protests and demands for safer living and working conditions.

Concerns among the Pandit community have intensified, with representatives articulating fears for their safety given their status as government employees returning under a specific rehabilitation program. They emphasize that while employment opportunities are crucial, security remains the paramount concern. Many have reportedly curtailed movements and restricted their daily activities due to the prevailing threat perception.

Specific demands articulated by the community's representatives include:

*   **Temporary Relocation:** A primary request for temporary relocation to safer areas, often outside the Kashmir Valley, until the security situation demonstrably improves.
*   **Enhanced Security at Workplaces:** Calls for increased protection measures at government offices and other workplaces where PM Package employees are posted.
*   **Secure Residential Accommodation:** Demands for fortified and centrally secured transit camps or housing facilities that offer better protection against potential threats.
*   **Review of Security Protocols:** A comprehensive review of existing security arrangements and intelligence gathering to prevent future attacks.
*   **Timely Information Sharing:** Requests for authorities to share threat assessments promptly with the community to enable proactive safety measures.

The government has previously taken steps to address security concerns, including deploying additional forces and establishing transit accommodation. However, community leaders assert that these measures have proven insufficient in mitigating the persistent sense of insecurity. They highlight the psychological toll on individuals and families living under constant threat, impacting their ability to perform duties effectively and integrate fully.

Discussions between Kashmiri Pandit representatives and local administration officials are ongoing, with the community pressing for concrete actions beyond assurances. The future of the Prime Minister's Employment Package, and indeed the broader rehabilitation efforts, hinges significantly on the government's ability to instill confidence and provide a secure environment for its beneficiaries in the Kashmir Valley. The community awaits decisive steps to address their renewed security concerns.
