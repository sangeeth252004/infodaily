New York State Assembly Member Zohran Mamdani has publicly voiced his opposition to a planned event at Madison Square Garden featuring Mohan Bhagwat, chief of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS). The event, reportedly organized by the Hindu Swayamsevak Sangh (HSS) and scheduled for an unspecified date in the near future, has drawn scrutiny from the Astoria-based legislator.

Mamdani, representing New York's 36th Assembly District, which includes a diverse population in Queens, declared his stance, stating, "I don't support the rally." His opposition centers on the RSS, which he described as a "Hindu nationalist organization with a documented history of discrimination and violence against religious minorities." This statement highlights concerns frequently raised by critics regarding the RSS's ideological foundations and its alleged actions within India.

The Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh is a prominent right-wing Hindu nationalist volunteer organization in India. Founded in 1925, its stated aim is to protect and promote Hindu culture and values. It functions as the ideological parent to numerous organizations, including the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). The RSS has historically faced criticism from various human rights organizations and political commentators who allege its involvement in or tacit support for discriminatory practices and violence targeting religious minorities, particularly Muslims and Christians, within India. Proponents, however, often describe it as a cultural and social service organization committed to national unity.

The planned event at Madison Square Garden is expected to draw a significant audience from the Indian diaspora in the United States. Such gatherings serve as platforms for leaders like Mohan Bhagwat to address supporters, discuss current affairs, and reinforce cultural or ideological perspectives among non-resident Indians and persons of Indian origin. The Hindu Swayamsevak Sangh (HSS) is often described as the international affiliate of the RSS, operating in various countries to organize cultural, educational, and social activities based on Hindu principles.

Mamdani's public declaration places him in opposition to an event that is likely to receive support from a segment of his constituents and the broader Indian-American community. His statement underscores ongoing debates within the diaspora regarding the political landscape in India and its influence on communities abroad.

*   **Zohran Mamdani's Role:** Represents New York's 36th Assembly District, covering Astoria, Queens.
*   **RSS Ideology:** Described by critics as Hindu nationalist; proponents describe it as a cultural organization.
*   **Event Venue:** Madison Square Garden, a major public venue in New York City.
*   **Organizer:** Hindu Swayamsevak Sangh (HSS), often associated with the RSS.

The event featuring Mohan Bhagwat is expected to proceed as planned, despite the stated opposition. Mamdani's stand is likely to spark further discussion and potentially mobilize different viewpoints within the diverse South Asian communities of New York City, reflecting the complex interplay of international politics and local representation. The situation highlights the challenges elected officials face when navigating the varied opinions and concerns of their constituencies regarding global issues.
