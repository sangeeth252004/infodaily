New Delhi, India – Congress leader Rahul Gandhi has publicly defended actress Kriti Sanon, following criticism she received from Member of Parliament Kangana Ranaut concerning a recent Raksha Bandhan-themed advertisement. Mr. Gandhi's statement, which emphasized a woman's right to personal choice regarding attire, marks a prominent intervention into a social discourse that has garnered significant public attention across India. The controversy began with Ranaut's remarks, questioning elements of the advertisement featuring Sanon.

The disagreement centers around an advertisement for a prominent brand, in which Kriti Sanon features. While the specific details of Kangana Ranaut's initial "barb" were not extensively detailed in her public statements, the essence of her criticism appeared to relate to the portrayal of traditional values or attire within the context of the Raksha Bandhan festival. Raksha Bandhan, a Hindu festival celebrating the bond between siblings, traditionally involves sisters tying a sacred thread (rakhi) on their brothers' wrists. Advertisements around this period often aim to connect with cultural sentiments while also promoting consumer products. Ranaut, known for her outspoken views on Indian culture and nationalism, has previously commented on public figures' conduct and sartorial choices, particularly when she perceives them to deviate from what she considers traditional or appropriate.

In response to the escalating debate, Rahul Gandhi issued a statement asserting his support for Ms. Sanon. His remarks were distilled into the principle of individual autonomy, specifically stating, "What a woman wears is her choice." This position aligns with broader discussions on personal freedom and gender equality in India, often championed by various political and social groups. Mr. Gandhi's intervention has been interpreted by observers as a move to reinforce his party's stance on individual liberties, particularly women's rights, within the country's diverse socio-political landscape. The statement did not directly name Kangana Ranaut but was widely understood as a counterpoint to the earlier criticism directed at Ms. Sanon.

Kriti Sanon, the Bollywood actress at the center of the advertising campaign, has not issued a direct public response to either Kangana Ranaut's criticism or Rahul Gandhi's defense. Her involvement in the advertisement forms part of her commercial endorsements portfolio, characteristic of leading figures in the Indian entertainment industry. The advertisement itself has continued to be a topic of discussion on social media platforms and in various news outlets, highlighting the immediate and far-reaching impact of celebrity endorsements and public discourse in the digital age.

*   **The Advertisement**: The commercial in question is for a prominent brand that launched its Raksha Bandhan campaign featuring Kriti Sanon.
*   **Context of Criticism**: Kangana Ranaut's comments were perceived by many as a critique of modern interpretations of traditional festivals and the role of celebrities in endorsing products that may challenge conventional norms.
*   **Political Framing**: Rahul Gandhi's defense frames the issue within the discourse of women's rights and personal autonomy, a recurring theme in Indian political debates.
