Mumbai, Maharashtra – The State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell) Maharashtra has announced the commencement of the choice filling process for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET UG) 2026 Centralised Admission Process (CAP) Round 1. This marks a critical stage for candidates aspiring to secure admission to undergraduate medical (MBBS) and dental (BDS) courses in various government, private, and deemed universities across Maharashtra for the academic year 2026.

Candidates who have successfully registered and completed the document verification process for Maharashtra NEET UG 2026 admissions are now eligible to participate in this phase. The choice filling mechanism allows applicants to select their preferred colleges and courses based on their NEET UG scores and eligibility criteria. This online process is integral to the merit-based allocation of seats through the CAP rounds.

The CAP is designed to streamline the admission procedure, ensuring transparency and fairness in seat distribution. During the choice filling period, candidates are required to log in to their application portal on the official CET Cell website, access the choice filling option, and submit their preferences. It is imperative for applicants to arrange their choices in descending order of priority, as the allocation is based on these preferences combined with their merit rank and seat availability.

Key details regarding the CAP Round 1 choice filling process include:

*   **Commencement:** The choice filling window opened on the officially designated date.
*   **Procedure:** Eligible candidates must log in to the CET Cell Maharashtra portal.
*   **Selection:** Applicants are required to select colleges and courses from the available seat matrix.
*   **Prioritization:** Choices must be arranged in order of preference to facilitate the allotment process.
*   **Locking Choices:** After submitting preferences, candidates must lock their choices within the specified deadline. Unlocked choices may not be considered for allocation.

The State CET Cell Maharashtra has advised all participating candidates to carefully review the seat matrix, college codes, and admission rules published on its official website before submitting their choices. Accuracy in filling choices is crucial, as modifications may not be permitted once choices are locked.

Following the conclusion of the choice filling and locking period, the CET Cell is expected to release the provisional merit list for CAP Round 1. This will be followed by the publication of the first allotment list, where candidates will be assigned seats based on their merit, preferences, and category. Those allotted seats will then be required to complete the admission formalities, including document verification and fee payment, at their respective colleges within the stipulated timeframe. Further CAP rounds will be conducted as necessary to fill remaining vacant seats.
