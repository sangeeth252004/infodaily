Over 100 Indian nationals are reported missing following severe flash floods that struck parts of Nepal in the early hours of October 4, 2023. The devastating floods, largely attributed to a Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) in Sikkim, India, caused the Teesta River to swell dramatically, impacting downstream areas including several regions within Nepal.

The sudden surge of water has led to significant loss of life, extensive damage to infrastructure, and widespread displacement. The majority of the missing Indian citizens were reportedly engaged in various hydropower projects located along the Teesta River basin in Nepal. Nepalese authorities, in coordination with their Indian counterparts, have initiated extensive search and rescue operations across the affected zones.

The GLOF event originated from South Lhonak Lake in Sikkim, India, causing the Teesta III dam to breach and leading to a catastrophic increase in water levels. This downstream flow overwhelmed rivers and tributaries, submerging communities and facilities in its path. The rapid escalation of the floodwaters left little time for evacuation, contributing to the high number of missing persons.

Key details regarding the disaster and its aftermath include:

*   **Date of Incident:** The flash floods began in the pre-dawn hours of Wednesday, October 4, 2023.
*   **Cause:** A Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) from South Lhonak Lake in Sikkim, India, triggered by intense rainfall.
*   **Affected Areas in Nepal:** Regions along the Teesta River basin that receive direct impact from the overflowing river. Specific districts include those bordering the Indian state of Sikkim.
*   **Missing Persons:** While exact figures are fluid due to ongoing recovery efforts, initial reports indicate over 100 Indian nationals are unaccounted for. These individuals were predominantly labourers and personnel working on various infrastructure projects, particularly hydropower facilities.
*   **Infrastructure Damage:** Several bridges, roads, and crucial hydropower project components have been severely damaged or completely washed away, impeding rescue efforts and communication.
*   **Fatalities:** While the focus remains on the missing, initial reports confirm several fatalities, with numbers expected to rise as search operations continue.

The Indian Embassy in Kathmandu and the Indian government have established emergency helplines and are actively collaborating with Nepalese authorities to locate the missing citizens and provide assistance. Rescue teams, including army and disaster response personnel from both nations, are navigating challenging terrain and adverse weather conditions to reach remote areas. The primary challenge remains accessing cut-off regions and identifying victims in the vast debris field left by the floods.

As the immediate crisis response continues, attention is also turning towards long-term recovery and rehabilitation efforts. Authorities are assessing the full extent of the damage to infrastructure and livelihoods. The focus in the coming weeks will remain on recovering the missing, providing humanitarian aid to affected populations, and restoring vital services in the regions devastated by the unprecedented flooding.
