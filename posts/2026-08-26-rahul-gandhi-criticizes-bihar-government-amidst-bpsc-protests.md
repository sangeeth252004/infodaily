NEW DELHI – Senior Indian National Congress leader Rahul Gandhi has publicly criticized the Bihar government regarding its response to ongoing protests by candidates of the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC). Mr. Gandhi, a prominent opposition figure, stated that "questions don't stop with batons," implying a forceful suppression of the demonstrations and an unaddressed core issue.

The statement, delivered recently, underscores the escalating tension surrounding the BPSC recruitment process and the state government's approach to dissent. Protests by BPSC aspirants have been a recurring feature in Bihar, often driven by grievances ranging from alleged irregularities in examinations and changes in exam patterns to delays in recruitment cycles and opaque selection processes. The mention of "batons" suggests reports or allegations of police action, potentially involving lathi-charge or other measures against the student protestors.

Rahul Gandhi's intervention brings a national political spotlight to a local issue, framing it within a broader narrative of governance and the right to peaceful protest. His party, the Indian National Congress, is a key opposition force at the national level and in several states, including Bihar, where it is part of the opposition alliance. The criticism aligns with the opposition's strategy to hold the state government accountable for administrative actions and public grievances.

Key aspects surrounding the BPSC protests and Rahul Gandhi's comments include:

*   **BPSC Role**: The Bihar Public Service Commission is responsible for conducting competitive examinations for recruitment to various civil service posts in the state of Bihar. Its functions are critical for state administration.
*   **Aspirant Grievances**: Students often protest over issues such as perceived lack of transparency, frequent changes in examination schedules or syllabi, alleged paper leaks, and delayed final results, impacting thousands of young job seekers.
*   **Alleged Police Action**: The phrase "batons" points to reports of police resorting to physical force to disperse protestors, a common flashpoint in student demonstrations across the country. Such actions often draw condemnation from opposition parties and human rights activists.
*   **Political Implications**: Rahul Gandhi's statement positions the Congress party as a voice for the aggrieved students, potentially aiming to consolidate support among the youth demographic and challenge the ruling coalition's narrative on governance in Bihar.

The Bihar government has yet to issue a direct, specific response to Rahul Gandhi's latest criticism. However, state authorities typically defend their actions in maintaining law and order during protests, often citing potential disruptions to public life or unauthorized gatherings. As the political discourse intensifies, the focus remains on how the Bihar government will address both the underlying demands of the BPSC aspirants and the mounting political pressure from the opposition. The situation is expected to continue evolving as student groups maintain their advocacy for reform and political parties vie for influence ahead of future elections.
