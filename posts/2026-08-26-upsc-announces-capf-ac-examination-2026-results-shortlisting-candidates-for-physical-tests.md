The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has recently announced the results for the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026. This announcement includes the roll numbers of candidates who have been provisionally shortlisted to appear for the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET). The comprehensive list of these candidates is accessible on the official UPSC website, upsc.gov.in.

This declaration marks a significant phase in the recruitment drive for Assistant Commandants within India's Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF), which includes the Border Security Force (BSF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), and Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB). Successful candidates from this rigorous examination process will assume crucial leadership roles, contributing to national security, border protection, and internal law enforcement.

The current result identifies those who have met the cut-off criteria for the written stage and are now eligible to proceed to the physical assessment phase.

Candidates who have qualified for the PST and PET are advised to prepare for the subsequent stages of the selection process. These physical tests are mandatory and qualifying in nature, designed to assess the physical fitness and endurance capabilities required for service in the CAPF.

Key subsequent stages in the selection process include:
*   **Physical Standard Test (PST):** Evaluates candidates against prescribed physical measurements, including height, chest circumference, and weight.
*   **Physical Efficiency Test (PET):** Assesses physical endurance through a series of tasks, typically involving running events, long jump, and shot put.
*   **Medical Standard Test:** Qualified candidates from PST/PET will undergo a thorough medical examination to ensure they meet the health standards mandated for CAPF personnel.
*   **Interview/Personality Test:** Candidates who clear the medical examination will be called for an interview, which assesses their general awareness, intelligence, and suitability for a leadership role.
*   **Document Verification:** Essential documents and credentials will be verified at various stages of the recruitment.

The exact dates and venues for the PST and PET will be communicated to the shortlisted candidates by the nodal Central Armed Police Force. Candidates are advised to regularly monitor the official UPSC website, upsc.gov.in, for specific announcements, admit card releases, and further instructions regarding the upcoming stages of the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026. The final merit list will be compiled based on the combined performance in the written examination, interview, and medical fitness assessment.
