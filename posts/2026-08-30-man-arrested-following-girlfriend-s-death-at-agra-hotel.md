A 24-year-old man, Himanshu Kumar, has been arrested in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, in connection with the alleged murder of his 23-year-old girlfriend. The incident reportedly occurred on the morning of Wednesday, June 19, 2024, at Hotel The Grace on Fatehabad road, where the couple had been staying. Police initiated an investigation after hotel staff discovered the woman unresponsive in their room and alerted authorities.

According to initial reports from the Agra police, Himanshu Kumar, a resident of Aligarh, arrived at Hotel The Grace with his girlfriend on Tuesday, June 18. Both individuals are from Aligarh and had reportedly known each other for approximately two years. The alleged incident transpired on the morning of June 19, which coincided with Himanshu Kumar's birthday.

During preliminary interrogation, Himanshu Kumar allegedly provided an account of the events. He reportedly claimed to have consumed a sexual stimulant drug earlier that morning. An argument is said to have ensued between the couple when his girlfriend refused to engage in sexual activity, reportedly due to his drug use. Following this altercation, Himanshu Kumar is accused of smothering the woman with a pillow, resulting in her death.

Upon discovering the unresponsive woman, hotel staff immediately contacted local law enforcement. Police personnel from the Tajganj police station swiftly arrived at the scene, securing the area and commencing their investigation. Himanshu Kumar was subsequently apprehended by authorities in connection with the incident.

**Supporting Details:**
*   **Accused:** Himanshu Kumar, 24 years old, from Aligarh.
*   **Victim:** Unnamed, 23 years old, from Aligarh.
*   **Location of Incident:** Hotel The Grace, Fatehabad road, Agra.
*   **Date and Time:** Morning of June 19, 2024.
*   **Alleged Sequence:** Consumption of a sexual stimulant drug, an argument following the victim's refusal of sexual activity, and subsequent alleged smothering.
*   **Police Action:** Himanshu Kumar has been booked under Section 302 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) for murder. Investigators are also examining allegations of sexual assault, which could lead to additional charges.
*   **Evidence Collection:** Authorities are reportedly reviewing CCTV footage from the hotel to corroborate details and gather further evidence pertaining to the case.

The body of the deceased has been sent for a comprehensive post-mortem examination. This forensic procedure aims to determine the precise cause and manner of death, which will be crucial for the ongoing legal proceedings. Police officials have confirmed that the investigation remains active and thorough, with all aspects of the incident being meticulously examined. Himanshu Kumar is currently in police custody as the inquiry progresses and the case moves through the judicial system.
