Anubhav Gupta, a technology professional based in Bengaluru, India, has reportedly accumulated a net worth of Rs 7 crore by the age of 31. His financial journey details a strategic approach to investment, career progression, and debt management, having previously reached a net worth of Rs 1 crore at age 26 and Rs 5 crore by age 29. This trajectory highlights specific decisions made over nearly a decade in the tech industry.

Gupta's financial foundation began with an initial annual salary of Rs 3.5 lakh. At age 22, he acquired his first property, financed by a loan of approximately Rs 50 lakh. Following 2017, he began investing in the stock market, establishing an early presence in equity. This period marked the initial phase of his wealth accumulation strategy, combining property ownership with market investments.

Over subsequent years, Gupta's income saw substantial growth, primarily through career advancements and transitions between companies. His salary reportedly escalated to Rs 55 lakh per annum, significantly increasing his capital for investment and debt servicing. By 2021, he had successfully repaid the Rs 50 lakh property loan, eliminating a major financial liability. His investment portfolio diversified to include a second property, complementing his primary focus on equity. Gupta’s approach emphasized consistent investment in stocks and mutual funds, alongside strategic real estate acquisitions.

Despite a generally upward financial trajectory, Gupta encountered a setback with an entrepreneurial venture. He reportedly invested Rs 8-10 lakh into developing an Artificial Intelligence (AI) application that ultimately did not succeed. This experience, while resulting in financial loss, is noted as a learning curve within his broader financial journey. He attributes his overall success to principles such as "conscious consumption," long-term investing, and a focus on generating passive income to achieve financial independence.

*   **Initial Salary:** Rs 3.5 lakh per annum
*   **First Property Loan:** Rs 50 lakh (at age 22)
*   **Loan Repayment:** Completed by 2021
*   **Peak Reported Salary:** Rs 55 lakh per annum
*   **Failed AI App Investment:** Rs 8-10 lakh
*   **Primary Investment Vehicles:** Equity (stocks, mutual funds), real estate

Gupta’s case offers an example of wealth accumulation within the Indian tech sector, achieved through a combination of diligent saving, strategic investment choices in both equities and real estate, and proactive debt management. His journey underscores the potential impact of long-term financial planning and resilience in navigating economic opportunities and challenges. His continued focus is reportedly on maintaining and growing his diversified investment portfolio, with an emphasis on sustained financial independence.
