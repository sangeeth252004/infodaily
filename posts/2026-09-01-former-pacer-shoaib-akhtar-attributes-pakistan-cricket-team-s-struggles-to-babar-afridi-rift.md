Former Pakistan fast bowler Shoaib Akhtar has publicly attributed the recent struggles of the Pakistan national cricket team to an alleged internal rift between star players Babar Azam and Shaheen Shah Afridi. Akhtar made these comments following Pakistan's early elimination from the ICC T20 World Cup 2024 group stage.

Speaking in a recent media appearance, Akhtar asserted that the perceived discord between the prominent batsman and fast bowler negatively impacted team cohesion and performance. His statement, "Pakistan was ruined that day," referred to the point at which he believes the alleged internal issues began to manifest within the squad. This perspective from a former international player highlights concerns surrounding team dynamics amidst a period of inconsistent results for the Pakistani side.

The alleged rift between Babar Azam and Shaheen Shah Afridi reportedly emerged after the ICC ODI World Cup 2023, where Pakistan failed to qualify for the semi-finals. Following this tournament, Babar Azam stepped down from the captaincy in all formats. Shaheen Shah Afridi was subsequently appointed as the captain of the T20 International squad. However, his tenure as T20I captain was short-lived, lasting for only one series against New Zealand in January 2024, before Babar Azam was reinstated as the white-ball captain just prior to the T20 World Cup 2024.

Key details surrounding the situation and its perceived impact include:
*   **Captaincy Changes:** The rapid succession of captaincy changes, from Babar Azam stepping down, to Shaheen Afridi's brief leadership, and then Babar Azam's return, has been cited as a potential source of instability.
*   **Team Performance:** Pakistan's recent performance in major tournaments, including a group-stage exit from the T20 World Cup 2024 and failure to reach the semi-finals of the ODI World Cup 2023, has intensified scrutiny on team leadership and unity.
*   **Player Roles:** Both Babar Azam, as a key top-order batsman, and Shaheen Shah Afridi, as a frontline fast bowler, are central figures in the national team, making any perceived friction between them a significant point of discussion among cricket commentators and the public.

Akhtar's comments suggest that these internal dynamics have overshadowed on-field strategies and player performance. While the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) or the players themselves have not officially corroborated claims of a significant rift, Akhtar's remarks add to the ongoing public discourse regarding the team's internal health. The team's immediate focus will likely shift towards future international fixtures and rebuilding confidence following their recent World Cup disappointment. The implications of such public statements from former players often resonate within the cricketing fraternity, prompting further discussion on team management and player relationships.
