Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh – Air Marshal (retd) Jeetendra Mishra, a distinguished war veteran of the Indian Air Force, has been appointed as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust, the body responsible for the management and development of the Ram Temple in Ayodhya. The appointment, announced this week, marks a significant leadership transition for the prominent religious complex.

Air Marshal Mishra expressed his dedication to the new role, stating that he views it as another opportunity to serve the nation. He reportedly characterized the appointment as "God's order," signifying his commitment to the monumental project. His background as a decorated military officer brings extensive experience in administration, strategic planning, and large-scale project execution to the temple trust.

Mishra's career in the Indian Air Force spanned several decades, during which he held various critical command and staff appointments. He is recognized for his service during conflict situations, earning him the designation of a war hero. His military background is expected to contribute to the disciplined and systematic management of the expansive Ram Temple complex, which was inaugurated on January 22, 2024, and now attracts a significant number of devotees and visitors daily. The temple project continues to evolve with ongoing construction and development phases, necessitating robust organizational leadership.

The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust, established by the Government of India, is tasked with overseeing the construction, maintenance, and overall administration of the temple and its surrounding facilities. The appointment of an individual with a military command background like Air Marshal Mishra underscores an apparent emphasis on efficiency, security, and meticulous project management for the sacred site.

In his new capacity, Air Marshal Mishra will be responsible for the daily operations, visitor management, resource allocation, and continued development initiatives of the Ram Temple. His leadership is anticipated to streamline administrative processes and ensure the smooth functioning of one of India's most significant religious and cultural landmarks. The appointment signals the trust's commitment to bringing professional and disciplined oversight to the long-term management of the Ayodhya complex.
