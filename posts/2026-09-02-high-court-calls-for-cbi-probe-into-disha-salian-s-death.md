A High Court bench has reportedly deemed a Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) probe into the death of celebrity manager Disha Salian as necessary. This development signals a potential escalation in the ongoing legal discourse surrounding Salian's demise, which has been the subject of public scrutiny and various investigations since 2020. The High Court's observation underscores the perceived importance of a central agency's involvement to ensure a comprehensive inquiry into the circumstances of her death.

Disha Salian, known for managing several prominent personalities, including late actor Sushant Singh Rajput, died on June 8, 2020. Her death, initially reported as a suicide, has since been mired in speculation and calls for further investigation. The Mumbai Police had previously conducted inquiries into the matter. However, petitions and public discourse have consistently sought a deeper examination, often citing alleged inconsistencies or unanswered questions surrounding the events leading to her demise. The High Court's recent statement lends judicial weight to these persistent calls for a more thorough probe.

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) is India's premier investigative agency, known for handling complex and sensitive cases, particularly those with inter-state implications or requiring specialized expertise. A directive or strong observation from a High Court regarding the necessity of a CBI probe often leads to the transfer of the case from state police to the central agency. This could potentially entail a fresh examination of evidence, re-interviewing witnesses, and re-evaluating previous findings, aiming to provide definitive answers regarding the cause and circumstances of Salian's death. The involvement of the CBI could also address concerns about impartiality and public confidence in the investigation process.

*   **Case Background:** Disha Salian's death occurred on June 8, 2020, just days before the death of actor Sushant Singh Rajput, with whom she had worked.
*   **Previous Investigations:** Initial investigations into Salian's death were conducted by the Mumbai Police. Public and political figures have frequently called for additional scrutiny.
*   **High Court's Stance:** The High Court's observation emphasizes the perceived need for a central agency's independent and thorough investigation into the matter.
*   **CBI's Role:** The CBI's involvement typically signifies a higher level of inquiry, often brought in for cases of significant public interest or complexity that require specialized investigative resources.
