The U.S. Department of State has commenced the active issuance phase for the Diversity Immigrant Visa Program for fiscal year 2026, known as DV-2026. This announcement signals that selected participants in the annual Green Card Lottery are now required to complete all necessary application procedures to secure their visas. A critical deadline of September 30, 2024, has been set for all DV-2026 selectees to finalize their application processes, including interviews and documentation, before the close of the U.S. government's fiscal year.

The Diversity Immigrant Visa program provides up to 55,000 immigrant visas annually to individuals from countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States. It aims to diversify the immigrant population in the U.S. by offering a path to permanent residency. Selection for the program is made through a randomized computer drawing. For DV-2026, millions of applicants from around the world participated in the initial entry period, which typically occurs in the fall.

Upon notification of selection, applicants must navigate a multi-stage process to transform their selection into a permanent resident visa. The current activation of visa issuance underscores the urgency for these selected individuals to move forward promptly. Failure to complete all required steps by the September 30 deadline will result in the forfeiture of the opportunity to obtain a Diversity Visa for the DV-2026 program, as visas cannot be carried over to subsequent fiscal years.

Key steps for DV-2026 selectees to meet the September 30 deadline include:

*   **Online Application (DS-260):** Completing and submitting the Immigrant Visa and Alien Registration Application, Form DS-260, for themselves and any accompanying family members.
*   **Document Gathering:** Collecting all required supporting documents, such as birth certificates, marriage certificates, police certificates, and educational transcripts.
*   **Medical Examination:** Undergoing a mandatory medical examination by a U.S. Embassy-approved physician.
*   **Interview Scheduling:** Attending a scheduled interview at a U.S. embassy or consulate in their country of residence. Applicants must ensure their case number is high enough to be current according to the monthly Visa Bulletin before their interview can be scheduled.

The September 30 deadline is non-negotiable and corresponds to the end of the U.S. government's fiscal year. The State Department processes a maximum number of visas each fiscal year, and the total number of DV visas issued is capped at 55,000. Selected applicants are strongly advised to monitor their case status through the Entrant Status Check (ESC) online tool on the official Diversity Visa website and to respond promptly to all communications from the Kentucky Consular Center (KCC) or the U.S. embassy/consulate handling their case. This proactive approach is essential to ensure all requirements are fulfilled before the closing date.
