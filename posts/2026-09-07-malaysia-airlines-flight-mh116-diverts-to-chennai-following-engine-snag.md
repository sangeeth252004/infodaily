Malaysia Airlines flight MH116, en route from Kuala Lumpur to Hyderabad, made a precautionary diversion to Chennai International Airport (MAA) on Wednesday after encountering a technical issue with one of its engines. The Airbus A330-300 aircraft was carrying over 186 passengers and an unspecified number of crew members when the incident occurred. All individuals onboard are reported safe.

The flight, which departed Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL), was bound for Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) in Hyderabad, India. During the flight, the crew detected an anomaly with one of the aircraft's engines, prompting a decision to divert as a safety measure. Air traffic control in Chennai was notified, and the aircraft was granted permission for an unscheduled landing.

Upon arrival at approximately 08:30 IST, the aircraft landed safely at Chennai Airport. Emergency services, including fire tenders and medical personnel, were on standby as per standard protocol for such diversions. Passengers disembarked without incident and were provided with assistance by airport and airline ground staff.

Malaysia Airlines confirmed the diversion in an official statement, citing a "technical issue" as the reason. The airline emphasized that the safety of its passengers and crew remains its highest priority and that the decision to divert was made in adherence to international aviation safety standards and operational procedures.

Key details regarding the incident include:
*   **Flight Number:** MH116
*   **Aircraft Type:** Airbus A330-300
*   **Original Route:** Kuala Lumpur (KUL) to Hyderabad (HYD)
*   **Diversion Point:** Chennai International Airport (MAA)
*   **Reason for Diversion:** Engine technical issue (snag)
*   **Passengers Onboard:** Over 186
*   **Safety Status:** All passengers and crew reported safe

Following the diversion, Malaysia Airlines initiated arrangements for the affected passengers. This included providing refreshments and making provisions for their onward journey to Hyderabad. Passengers were expected to be accommodated on alternative flights or provided with other travel solutions to minimize disruption to their travel plans.

The aircraft is currently undergoing inspection by maintenance engineers to identify the precise cause of the engine snag and to perform necessary repairs. Aviation authorities may also conduct an inquiry into the incident to review operational aspects and ensure continued adherence to safety regulations. The timeline for the aircraft's return to service will depend on the findings of the technical assessment and subsequent maintenance work.
