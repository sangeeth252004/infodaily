Investigations into the security incident near Delhi's Red Fort on February 15, 2024, have revealed that the primary target was originally the Lal Mandir, a prominent temple in the Old Delhi area. According to findings from the Delhi Police's Special Cell, the arrested Pakistani national, identified as Mohd Waqar alias Umar, confessed during interrogation that he shifted his plan to the Red Fort area due to heavy traffic and an inability to find suitable parking near the temple.

The detailed confession provided by Umar outlined his initial objective: to detonate an RDX-laden vehicle at the Lal Mandir. However, on the morning of February 15, intense traffic congestion near the temple reportedly prevented him from executing the original plan as intended. Unable to secure a parking spot for the vehicle, Umar allegedly made a last-minute decision to target the vicinity of the Red Fort instead.

Police sources indicate that Umar, who has been described by investigators as an alleged terrorist, was in communication with handlers based in Pakistan. These handlers, including an individual identified as Hanzla from the Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) terror outfit, are believed to have provided instructions for the attack. The objective, as stated by investigators, was to cause significant panic and disruption within the city.

Key details from the investigation include:
*   **Suspect:** Mohd Waqar, alias Umar, identified as a Pakistani national.
*   **Incident Date:** February 15, 2024.
*   **Original Target:** Lal Mandir, Old Delhi.
*   **Reason for Change:** Heavy traffic and lack of parking near Lal Mandir.
*   **Revised Target:** Area near the Red Fort.
*   **Alleged Explosive:** RDX, recovered from the suspect.
*   **Handler:** Hanzla, allegedly from Lashkar-e-Taiba.

Umar’s arrest led to the recovery of several incriminating items, including 2.5 kg of RDX, a detonator, and a timer. These materials were reportedly found in his possession, confirming the nature of the planned attack. The Delhi Police's Special Cell utilized technical surveillance and human intelligence to track Umar's movements and eventually apprehend him.

The incident underscores the persistent threat of terror plots targeting crowded public spaces and sensitive locations in major urban centers. It also highlights how unforeseen logistical challenges, such as traffic congestion, can unexpectedly alter the execution of such plans. The ongoing investigation by the Delhi Police's Special Cell aims to uncover further details regarding the broader conspiracy, including any local support networks Umar might have had and the full extent of his handlers' involvement. Legal proceedings against Umar are underway, with authorities focused on presenting a comprehensive case based on the evidence collected.
