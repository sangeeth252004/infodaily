Russian President Vladimir Putin arrived in India on November 25, 2024, for a bilateral meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The visit precedes India's hosting of the BRICS Summit in 2026 and is expected to focus on strengthening the strategic partnership between the two nations across various sectors. The arrival underscores ongoing diplomatic engagements between Moscow and New Delhi.

President Putin's visit provides a platform for discussions on a broad spectrum of topics, including defense cooperation, energy security, trade and investment, and regional and global issues of mutual concern. India has historically maintained extensive defense ties with Russia, which remains a primary supplier of military equipment and technology to the country. Discussions are anticipated to cover potential new defense contracts, technology transfers, and upgrades to existing agreements, reinforcing the long-standing military-technical cooperation.

Economic cooperation is another key area of focus. Bilateral trade between India and Russia has experienced growth in recent years, significantly driven by increased energy imports by India and the diversification of trade baskets. The leaders are expected to review progress on targets set for bilateral trade volumes and to explore new avenues for collaboration in sectors such as pharmaceuticals, agriculture, and information technology. Both nations have also expressed interest in expanding cooperation within the framework of the International North-South Transport Corridor (INSTC) to enhance connectivity and streamline trade logistics between South Asia, Central Asia, and Europe.

The timing of President Putin's visit, ahead of India's role as the host nation for the BRICS Summit in 2026, provides an opportunity for discussions on multilateral cooperation within the bloc. BRICS, an acronym for Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa, along with its recent expansion to include additional member states, represents a significant grouping of emerging economies. India's preparations for the 2026 summit and its vision for the bloc's future trajectory, including discussions on economic policy coordination and institutional reforms, are likely to be on the agenda.

Key aspects of the India-Russia relationship include:
*   **Strategic Partnership:** Characterized by regular high-level exchanges and cooperation across political, economic, defense, and cultural spheres since the 1970s.
*   **Defense Collaboration:** Russia has been a crucial partner in India's defense modernization efforts, supplying advanced military platforms and facilitating joint production initiatives, such as the BrahMos missile system.
*   **Energy Partnership:** India is a major importer of Russian crude oil and coal, contributing to its energy security needs. Discussions frequently involve long-term supply agreements and potential investments in energy infrastructure.
*   **Multilateral Engagement:** Both nations are active participants in various international forums, including BRICS, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), and the G20, advocating for a multi-polar global order and increased cooperation on global governance issues.

The outcomes of the discussions between President Putin and Prime Minister Modi are expected to reinforce the bilateral strategic partnership and set a course for future cooperation across various domains. A joint statement or press briefing is anticipated following their meeting, detailing the agreements reached and the shared vision for different aspects of their relationship. The visit also serves to align perspectives on regional and global developments, contributing to the broader geopolitical dialogue as India prepares for its host responsibilities for the BRICS Summit in 2026.
