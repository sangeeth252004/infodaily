CUPERTINO, CA – Apple Inc. announced today that pre-orders for its newest flagship smartphones, the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, are set to commence globally on Friday, September 20, 2024. Both devices will be available for reservation through Apple's official website, the Apple Store app, and authorized retailers, following their unveiling at the company's recent keynote event. This launch marks Apple's latest entry into the premium smartphone segment, introducing a suite of hardware and software enhancements.

The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max represent the pinnacle of Apple's smartphone technology for the current generation, integrating advanced capabilities designed to appeal to professional users and technology enthusiasts. Pricing for the iPhone 18 Pro will start at $999 for the 128GB model, while the iPhone 18 Pro Max will begin at $1,199 for its 128GB configuration. General availability for both models is scheduled for September 27, 2024. Industry analysts anticipate strong initial demand, driven by a new upgrade cycle and the devices' significant technological advancements.

Key features highlighted by Apple for the iPhone 18 Pro series include:

*   **A18 Bionic Chip:** Powering both models, the new A18 Bionic chip is fabricated on a 3-nanometer process. Apple reports it delivers up to 20% faster CPU performance and 30% faster graphics processing compared to its predecessor, alongside a next-generation Neural Engine for enhanced on-device artificial intelligence capabilities.
*   **Advanced Camera System:** The iPhone 18 Pro models feature a second-generation 48-megapixel Main camera sensor, designed for improved low-light photography. The iPhone 18 Pro Max introduces a new 5x optical zoom telephoto lens, while both models include an enhanced 12-megapixel Ultra Wide camera with improved macro capabilities.
*   **ProMotion XDR Display:** The OLED Super Retina XDR displays now offer up to 2,500 nits peak outdoor brightness, an increase from previous generations. The ProMotion technology provides adaptive refresh rates from 1Hz to 120Hz, optimizing both battery efficiency and visual fluidity.
*   **Titanium Frame Design:** Both devices utilize an aerospace-grade titanium alloy for their frames, which contributes to increased durability and a reduction in overall weight by approximately 10-15 grams compared to previous stainless steel models.
*   **Enhanced Battery Life:** Apple has optimized battery performance, with the iPhone 18 Pro offering up to 26 hours of video playback, and the iPhone 18 Pro Max extending to up to 32 hours.
*   **Wi-Fi 7 and Enhanced 5G Connectivity:** The new iPhones support Wi-Fi 7, providing theoretical data speeds up to 40 Gbps. An upgraded 5G modem also offers broader band support for faster and more reliable cellular connections.
*   **USB-C 4 Port:** The Lightning port has been replaced with a USB-C 4 port, facilitating data transfer speeds of up to 40 Gbps and supporting external display output up to 8K resolution.
*   **Action Button 2.0:** An updated Action Button on the side of the device now incorporates pressure sensitivity, allowing for multiple customizable functions beyond a single press, such as launching the camera, flashlight, voice memos, or accessibility features.
*   **Satellite SOS Gen 2:** The emergency satellite communication feature has been expanded to include more regions and integrates with new roadside assistance services in select markets, providing additional emergency options.

The availability of these new flagship devices is expected to stimulate the high-end smartphone market as consumers consider upgrades. The initial sales performance and consumer feedback in the weeks following the pre-order and general release will be closely monitored by industry stakeholders to assess market trends and Apple's competitive standing.
