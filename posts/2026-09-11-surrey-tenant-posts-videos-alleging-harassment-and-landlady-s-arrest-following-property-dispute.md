A tenant in Surrey, British Columbia, identified as Jesse Blake, has recently uploaded a series of videos to social media platform TikTok, detailing an ongoing dispute with her landlady. The videos, posted over the past few weeks, allege harassment by the landlady and her son, stemming primarily from the tenant's use of a barbecue on the property. Blake further claims that the landlady was subsequently arrested by Surrey Police (RCMP) and faces charges.

The dispute reportedly began over the use of a barbecue in the property's backyard. According to Jesse Blake, who resides in a basement suite, initial permission for barbecue use was granted. However, the landlady later rescinded this, citing concerns about fire hazards and insurance policies. This reversal allegedly led to multiple confrontations documented by Blake on her TikTok account.

Key details surrounding the allegations include:
*   **Barbecue Incident:** The core of the dispute revolves around the landlady's alleged attempts to prevent Blake from using a barbecue, culminating in one video showing the landlady's son attempting to remove the barbecue from the property.
*   **Allegations of Harassment:** Blake's videos depict instances where the landlady is seen confronting her, allegedly yelling and attempting to enter the tenant's living space. One video clip, dated September 24, 2023, shows the landlady allegedly trying to enter the tenant's apartment.
*   **Claims of Trespass:** The tenant has also alleged that the landlady repeatedly "barged" into her apartment without notice or permission.

Jesse Blake claims that the situation escalated to police involvement, leading to the arrest of the landlady. Blake stated in her videos that the landlady was arrested and charged with "assault, uttering threats, and property mischief." Additionally, Blake indicated that she was issued a no-contact order as part of the legal proceedings.

Despite these claims made by the tenant on social media, Surrey RCMP has not yet released any official confirmation regarding an arrest or charges against the landlady. Official police statements typically follow standard procedures for public disclosure, often pending ongoing investigations or formal charges.
