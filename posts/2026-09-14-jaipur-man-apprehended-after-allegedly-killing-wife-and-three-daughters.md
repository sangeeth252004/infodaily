Police in Jaipur, Rajasthan, apprehended Surendra Pal Singh Rathore on Tuesday, May 21, 2024, following allegations that he killed his wife and three young daughters at their residence in Pratap Nagar. Rathore was located and taken into custody at a temple in Mount Abu after a two-day search.

The incident came to light on Sunday, May 19, when Rathore reportedly contacted his elder brother, who subsequently discovered the bodies of the four victims. Law enforcement officials were immediately alerted and commenced an investigation. The victims have been identified as Rathore's wife, Sangeeta Rathore, 30, and their three daughters: Manvi, 6, Divyanshi, 4, and an 11-month-old infant. Preliminary reports suggest the victims were allegedly smothered.

Following the discovery, Surendra Pal Singh Rathore was reported missing from the crime scene. A police team was formed to track his whereabouts. Investigators utilized technical surveillance, including tracking his mobile phone and vehicle, to pinpoint his location. He was traced to a temple in Mount Abu, a hill station in Rajasthan, where he was found seeking what he reportedly described as "divine blessings" following the alleged acts.

*   **Crime Scene:** The alleged murders occurred at the family's home in Sector 26, Pratap Nagar, Jaipur.
*   **Victims:** Sangeeta Rathore (30), Manvi (6), Divyanshi (4), and an 11-month-old girl.
*   **Method of Discovery:** Rathore's elder brother found the bodies after receiving a call from him.
*   **Apprehension:** Arrested in Mount Abu by a team from Pratap Nagar police station.

Deputy Commissioner of Police (South) Yogesh Goyal stated that initial investigations suggest financial difficulties and marital discord may have contributed to the alleged crime. Rathore reportedly accumulated significant debt, which is believed to have led to frequent arguments with his wife.

A case has been registered against Surendra Pal Singh Rathore under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including murder. He is currently in police custody, and further interrogation is ongoing to ascertain the full sequence of events and the precise motive behind the alleged killings. Police continue to gather evidence and conduct interviews as part of the formal judicial process. The case remains under active investigation by Jaipur police authorities.
