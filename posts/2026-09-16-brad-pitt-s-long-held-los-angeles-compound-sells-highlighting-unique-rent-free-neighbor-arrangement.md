Actor Brad Pitt has completed the sale of his extensive Los Angeles compound, a property collection distinguished by a long-term arrangement that allowed an elderly neighbor to reside rent-free in one of the acquired homes until his death at the age of 105. The transaction concludes Pitt's nearly three-decade ownership of the sprawling estate in the Hollywood Hills area of Los Angeles.

Mr. Pitt initially acquired the primary residence within the compound in 1994 for an approximate cost of $1.7 million. Over the following years, he systematically expanded his footprint in the neighborhood, purchasing several adjacent parcels and structures. This methodical aggregation of properties transformed the original mansion into a multi-acre, multi-structure compound, a common practice among high-profile individuals seeking privacy and extensive amenities.

Among these strategic acquisitions was a smaller house located next to Pitt's main residence, occupied by an elderly widower known to the actor as John. Following the purchase of John's property, Mr. Pitt reportedly made an agreement to allow the former owner to continue living in the home without any rental obligations. This arrangement persisted for a significant duration, spanning many years and continuing until John's passing at the remarkable age of 105. The arrangement stood out as an unconventional approach in the high-stakes Los Angeles real estate market, where property acquisition typically leads to immediate integration or redevelopment.

The compound served as Mr. Pitt's primary residence for a substantial portion of his acting career, evolving to include amenities such as a skate park, a private studio, and multiple pools, reflecting his long-term investment in the property. The recent sale, which closed for a reported figure of approximately $33 million, signifies a major shift in the actor's residential portfolio. The decision to sell was made public earlier this year when the property was quietly listed, attracting attention from high-net-worth buyers seeking expansive, private estates in the coveted Los Angeles area.

Key details surrounding the property and the unique tenancy agreement include:

*   **Initial Purchase:** Brad Pitt acquired his primary Los Angeles mansion in 1994 for an estimated $1.7 million.
*   **Compound Expansion Strategy:** Over decades, Pitt systematically purchased at least six adjacent properties to create a unified, private estate.
*   **Rent-Free Tenancy:** An elderly neighbor, identified as John, was allowed to live rent-free in a house Pitt purchased as part of his expansion.
*   **Duration of Agreement:** The rent-free living arrangement continued for many years, concluding only with John's death at 105 years old.
*   **Final Sale Price:** The entire Los Angeles compound recently sold for approximately $33 million, marking a substantial return on the initial investment and subsequent acquisitions.

With the sale of his long-time Los Angeles home, Mr. Pitt is reportedly shifting his primary residential focus. While the new owners' specific plans for the expansive compound remain private, the transaction brings to a close a notable chapter in celebrity real estate, remembered not only for its scale but also for the unique and long-standing agreement with its former neighbor. The actor's future residences are understood to include properties outside of the Los Angeles area, such as a recently acquired oceanfront home in Carmel, California.
