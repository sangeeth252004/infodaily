A luxurious riverfront mansion in Idaho, previously marketed at an asking price of $3.86 million, is scheduled for a no-reserve auction on September 17. This approach ensures the property will sell to the highest bidder, irrespective of the final price achieved. The event combines a high-value real estate transaction with a significant philanthropic commitment by the owner.

The decision to offer a property without a reserve price is a notable strategy in the luxury real estate market. It often indicates a clear intent to sell quickly and can attract a wider pool of prospective buyers, as there is no minimum threshold price that must be met. For a property of this caliber, previously listed at $3.86 million, a no-reserve auction signals a direct path to sale.

Adding a substantial charitable dimension to the sale, the owner has publicly committed to donating the entirety of the sale proceeds to two prominent medical institutions. Fifty percent of the final sale price will be allocated to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, and the remaining fifty percent will go to the Mayo Clinic. This pledge transforms the real estate transaction into a significant fundraising effort for medical research and patient care.

The property itself is situated in a prime riverfront location in Idaho, generally indicating scenic views and an exclusive setting. While specific architectural details and amenities were not immediately specified in the public announcement of the auction, the "mansion" designation suggests a sizable residence designed with high-end features and extensive living spaces typically associated with luxury properties in the multi-million dollar range. Such residences often include bespoke interiors, professional-grade kitchens, multiple bedrooms and bathrooms, and expansive outdoor living areas that capitalize on the natural surroundings.

Key details regarding the auction include:
*   **Auction Date:** September 17
*   **Auction Type:** No-reserve, meaning the property will be sold to the highest bidder without a minimum price requirement.
*   **Previous Asking Price:** $3.86 million, as it was previously listed on the market.
*   **Beneficiaries of Proceeds:** 50% to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, 50% to Mayo Clinic.
*   **Location:** Idaho riverfront.

The upcoming auction will finalize the sale of this Idaho riverfront mansion, establishing its market value through competitive bidding. Concurrently, the event will generate substantial financial contributions to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and Mayo Clinic, reflecting the owner's philanthropic intent. The sale represents a distinctive intersection of high-value real estate and direct charitable giving, with the proceeds poised to support critical healthcare initiatives.
