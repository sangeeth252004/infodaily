Hangzhou, China – The Indian contingent at the 19th Asian Games in Hangzhou has encountered significant administrative challenges, with the national karate team traveling without its official coaches and several equestrian riders facing accreditation delays upon arrival at the Games village. These issues have raised concerns regarding athlete support and preparation ahead of their respective competitions.

The Indian karate team departed for Hangzhou without its designated coaches due after their accreditations were reportedly denied by the Hangzhou Asian Games Organising Committee (HAGOC). This situation arose despite the Sports Ministry having cleared the coaches for participation. Sources indicate the denial stemmed from the Indian Olympic Association (IOA) initially submitting a "leaner contingent" list, which did not include support staff. Subsequent requests to add the coaches to the accreditation list were reportedly rejected by HAGOC, citing adherence to established deadlines and quotas.

*   **Coaches' Exclusion:** The absence of primary coaches means Indian karate athletes will navigate their crucial competitions without direct, on-site guidance from their long-term trainers. This could impact strategic decision-making and real-time adjustments during bouts.
*   **Administrative Discrepancy:** The incident highlights a disconnect between the initial planning by the IOA and the Sports Ministry's approvals, ultimately affecting athlete support at a major international event.

Adding to the administrative difficulties, several Indian equestrian riders experienced delays in receiving their accreditations upon reaching the Games village. This bureaucratic bottleneck prevented immediate access to their allocated accommodations and training facilities. Reports indicated some riders were left waiting for extended periods, disrupting their essential pre-competition routines and rest.

*   **Entry Delays:** The hold-up in accreditation meant riders could not promptly settle into the Games village, delaying access to their horses and the necessary support infrastructure required for meticulous preparation.
*   **Impact on Preparation:** For equestrian athletes, immediate access to their horses and stable facilities is critical for maintaining performance readiness, especially after international travel. Any delay can disrupt the delicate bond and training regimen between rider and horse.

These incidents underscore the complex logistical demands placed on national sporting federations and Olympic committees when preparing for multi-sport events like the Asian Games. Ensuring timely and complete accreditation for all athletes and essential support staff remains a critical component of successful participation. As the Games progress, the focus will be on resolving these outstanding issues to minimize further disruption to the Indian contingent's performance and morale. Authorities are expected to review the processes to prevent similar occurrences in future major sporting events.
