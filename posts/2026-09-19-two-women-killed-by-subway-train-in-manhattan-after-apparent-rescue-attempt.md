New York City transit authorities are investigating a tragic incident in Manhattan where two women were struck and killed by a subway train early Saturday morning. Pooja Agarwal, 35, and Akash Singh, 29, both sustained fatal injuries after being hit by a downtown Q train at the 14th Street-Union Square station.

Preliminary reports indicate that Akash Singh fell onto the tracks around 1:45 AM. Subsequently, Pooja Agarwal, an Indian-origin woman, reportedly jumped onto the tracks in an attempt to rescue her friend. Both women were then struck by the approaching train. Emergency services responded to the scene, but both individuals were pronounced dead at the location.

The incident has prompted a joint investigation by the New York City Police Department (NYPD) and the Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA). Investigators are examining surveillance footage and interviewing witnesses to determine the exact sequence of events leading to the fatalities. Officials have not yet released definitive information regarding why Singh initially fell onto the tracks.

Key details surrounding the incident include:
*   **Victims:** Pooja Agarwal, 35, and Akash Singh, 29.
*   **Location:** 14th Street-Union Square subway station, Manhattan, New York City.
*   **Time:** Approximately 1:45 AM, early Saturday morning (August 17, 2024).
*   **Train:** A downtown Q train.
*   **Circumstance:** One woman reportedly fell onto the tracks, and the other attempted a rescue.
*   **Investigation:** Ongoing by the NYPD and MTA.

The incident temporarily disrupted subway service on the N, Q, R, and W lines, with trains bypassing the Union Square station for several hours as emergency personnel and investigators processed the scene. Regular service resumed later that morning.

Authorities have urged the public to exercise caution on subway platforms and to maintain a safe distance from the edge of the tracks.
