NEW DELHI – The Delhi Police recently issued a public clarification, debunking a widely shared video circulating on social media platforms that purported to show ongoing protests at Jantar Mantar. Authorities confirmed the footage was "old," addressing claims that the video depicted current demonstrations amidst heightened public attention to various issues, including the "SIR row."

The video, which gained significant traction across social media, depicted a large gathering of individuals engaged in protest activities at the prominent Jantar Mantar site, a designated area for public demonstrations in New Delhi. Its rapid dissemination led many users to believe it represented contemporary events, fueling speculation and contributing to the online narrative surrounding recent societal discussions.

The clarification by the Delhi Police aimed to prevent the spread of misinformation and maintain clarity regarding public order. Official police channels, including their social media handles, were utilized to disseminate the fact-check, urging the public to exercise caution and verify information before sharing. This intervention underscores the growing role of law enforcement agencies in combating digital misinformation, especially concerning events that could potentially impact public perception or law and order.

*   **Official Confirmation:** Delhi Police unequivocally stated the video was "old footage," not representative of current events.
*   **Context:** The fact-check emerged amidst ongoing public discourse and viral content, specifically referenced in relation to the "SIR row."
*   **Public Safety:** The police's action highlights efforts to manage public information and prevent potential misinterpretation of events.

The incident serves as a prominent example of how unverified content can quickly spread across digital networks, influencing public opinion and potentially leading to misunderstandings. Authorities consistently advise citizens to rely on official and credible sources for news and updates, particularly during periods of active public debate or significant social discourse.

As digital platforms continue to be primary sources of information for many, the proactive role of institutions like the Delhi Police in fact-checking viral content is becoming increasingly critical. This action reinforces the importance of media literacy and responsible information sharing among the public to ensure accurate representation of events and mitigate the impact of false narratives. Further communications regarding the origins or intent behind the original spread of the old footage have not been publicly detailed.
