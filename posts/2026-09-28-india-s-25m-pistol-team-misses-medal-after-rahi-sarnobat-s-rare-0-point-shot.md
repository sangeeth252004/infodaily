The Indian women's 25m pistol team, consisting of Olympians Manu Bhaker and Rahi Sarnobat alongside rising star Esha Singh, concluded their campaign at the recent Asian Games with a fourth-place finish, narrowly missing a bronze medal. The team's final score placed them just two points shy of a podium position, an outcome significantly influenced by an unusual 0-point shot recorded by experienced shooter Rahi Sarnobat during the rapid-fire stage of the competition.

The 25m pistol team event is a demanding competition that assesses precision and speed, comprising both a precision stage and a rapid-fire stage, where athletes must fire within a tight time limit. In such high-stakes international competitions, every shot, scored typically out of 10.9 points, contributes directly to the team's cumulative total, with margins for medals often being extremely fine. The Indian contingent had shown strong performance throughout the earlier phases, building anticipation for a potential medal.

However, during the critical rapid-fire segment, Rahi Sarnobat, a decorated athlete with Commonwealth Games gold medals and previous Olympic appearances, registered a 0-point score on one of her attempts. This incident, described by observers as rare and inexplicable for a shooter of her calibre, proved to be a pivotal moment. While official reasons for a 0-point shot can vary, from a missed target to a technical malfunction such as a bullet not properly engaging the barrel (a "dry fire") and thus not registering, the direct impact was a significant loss of points for the team's total.

The team's final score of 1749 points left them in fourth position, precisely two points behind the bronze medal-winning team. This narrow gap underscores the critical nature of Sarnobat's 0-point shot, as a standard score of even 9 or 10 points would have propelled India into a medal position. Bhaker and Singh contributed robust performances, but the single anomalous score from Sarnobat ultimately altered the team's trajectory.

The incident highlights the unforgiving nature of elite sports, where a single, rare occurrence can determine outcomes despite overall strong performances. While the immediate focus remains on the missed medal, the Indian shooting federation and athletes will likely review the circumstances surrounding the unprecedented 0-point shot. The team is expected to regroup and continue their preparation for future international competitions, with the experience serving as a stark reminder of the fine margins at the pinnacle of competitive shooting.
