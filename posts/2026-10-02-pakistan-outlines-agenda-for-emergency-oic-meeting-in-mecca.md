Pakistan's Foreign Ministry has revealed that an emergency meeting of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) Foreign Ministers in Mecca will focus on "political engagement with the Houthis" and the ongoing situation in the Gaza Strip. The announcement, made by Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mumtaz Zahra Baloch, detailed the two primary agenda items for the high-level gathering convened at the request of Saudi Arabia and the State of Palestine. The meeting is scheduled for June 25, 2024, in the Saudi city of Mecca.

The decision to include "political engagement with the Houthis" on the OIC agenda signals a potential coordinated diplomatic approach from the collective Muslim world towards the group. This development occurs amid sustained Houthi attacks on commercial shipping in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden, which have disrupted global trade routes and escalated regional tensions. Pakistan's government has emphasized the importance of constructive engagement to de-escalate the situation and promote stability in the critical maritime corridor. The proposed engagement is reportedly aimed at finding diplomatic avenues to prevent further conflict and does not imply formal recognition of the Houthi administration.

The second core agenda item, the "situation in the Gaza Strip and its ramifications," reflects ongoing international concerns over the humanitarian crisis and military operations in the Palestinian territory. The OIC, as a collective voice for the Muslim world, is expected to deliberate on measures to address the suffering of the Palestinian people, call for a ceasefire, and discuss efforts for humanitarian aid delivery. The conflict, which began in October 2023, has led to widespread displacement, casualties, and a severe shortage of essential resources in Gaza, prompting calls for international action from various global bodies.

Key details regarding the OIC meeting agenda include:
*   The meeting was requested jointly by the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the State of Palestine.
*   Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mumtaz Zahra Baloch confirmed the agenda items.
*   The two main discussion points are "political engagement with the Houthis" and the "situation in the Gaza Strip and its ramifications."
*   The gathering aims to foster a unified stance among OIC member states on these critical regional issues.

The outcome of the emergency OIC meeting will likely shape the collective diplomatic efforts of its 57 member states concerning the Houthi movement and the crisis in Gaza. Decisions made during the assembly could influence future international initiatives for de-escalation in the Red Sea and concerted humanitarian and political responses to the conflict in Palestine. Observers will monitor the meeting for any resolutions or joint statements that could outline a new strategic direction for OIC countries in addressing these complex challenges.
