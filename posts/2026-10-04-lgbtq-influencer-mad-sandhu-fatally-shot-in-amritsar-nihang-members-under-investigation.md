Mad Sandhu, a prominent LGBTQ+ influencer, was fatally shot in Pandori village, Amritsar district, Punjab, with police initiating an investigation into the circumstances surrounding his death. The incident, which occurred recently, has led authorities to question members of a Nihang group reportedly involved in prior altercations with Sandhu.

According to police reports, Sandhu, known for his social media presence and advocacy, sustained gunshot wounds and was pronounced dead at the scene. The investigation commenced immediately following the discovery of his body, with law enforcement officials working to establish a clear timeline and motive for the crime. An First Information Report (FIR) has been registered, and a detailed probe is underway.

The focus of the police inquiry has broadened to include alleged prior disputes between Sandhu and certain individuals associated with a local Nihang group. Reports indicate that these disputes stemmed from objections raised by the Nihang members regarding Sandhu's clothing choices and his social media content, which they reportedly found objectionable. Old video footage has surfaced depicting arguments between Sandhu and individuals identified as Nihangs, lending context to the ongoing investigation.

Key details surrounding the incident and investigation include:
*   **Victim Identity:** Mad Sandhu, an LGBTQ+ influencer active on various social media platforms.
*   **Location of Incident:** Pandori village, situated in the Amritsar district of Punjab, India.
*   **Alleged Perpetrators:** Members of a Nihang group are under scrutiny in connection with the killing.
*   **Alleged Motive:** Previous confrontations reportedly arose from the Nihang members' disapproval of Sandhu's attire and lifestyle, as depicted in his online presence.
*   **Police Action:** An FIR has been filed, and the Amritsar Rural Police are actively investigating the case, including examining the history of altercations.

Authorities have not yet released specific details regarding arrests or identified all individuals directly involved in the shooting. The investigation is ongoing, with police officials gathering forensic evidence, interviewing witnesses, and reviewing any relevant digital information, including Sandhu's social media activities and the aforementioned videos of past confrontations.
