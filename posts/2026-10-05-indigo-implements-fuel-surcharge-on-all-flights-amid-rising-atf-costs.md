IndiGo, India's largest airline by market share, has introduced a fuel surcharge on all its domestic and international flights, effective Thursday, October 5, 2023. The carrier cited the sustained and significant increase in Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) prices as the primary reason for the implementation of this additional levy. This move is expected to impact air travel costs for passengers across all routes operated by the airline.

The decision comes as airlines face mounting operational expenses due to the escalating cost of jet fuel, which constitutes a substantial portion of an airline's expenditure. An official announcement from IndiGo stated that the surcharge aims to partially offset these rising input costs and maintain sustainable operations. This marks a reintroduction of a fuel surcharge by IndiGo, a measure previously seen in the Indian aviation sector during periods of high fuel volatility.

The new fuel surcharge will be applied per flight sector and varies based on the distance of the journey. Passengers booking new tickets or those who have already booked and whose travel falls on or after October 5, 2023, will be subject to these additional charges.

The fuel surcharge structure is as follows:
*   Flights covering distances up to 500 km: Rs 200 per passenger per sector
*   Flights covering distances between 501 km and 800 km: Rs 300 per passenger per sector
*   Flights covering distances between 801 km and 1200 km: Rs 400 per passenger per sector
*   Flights covering distances between 1201 km and 1800 km: Rs 500 per passenger per sector
*   Flights covering distances between 1801 km and 2500 km: Rs 600 per passenger per sector
*   Flights covering distances between 2501 km and 3500 km: Rs 700 per passenger per sector
*   Flights covering distances of 3501 km and above: Rs 800 per passenger per sector

This additional charge is independent of the base fare, taxes, and other applicable fees, directly increasing the total ticket price for passengers. The airline industry frequently adjusts pricing mechanisms, including fuel surcharges, in response to global crude oil prices and currency fluctuations, which directly influence ATF costs.

IndiGo's decision reflects the current economic pressures faced by carriers globally. The airline, operated by InterGlobe Aviation Ltd., serves a vast network of domestic and international destinations. The implementation of this surcharge highlights the ongoing challenge of managing operational costs within the highly competitive aviation market. Passengers booking future travel will now factor in this increased expenditure as part of their overall travel planning. The aviation sector will likely monitor ATF price trends closely for any future adjustments to such levies.
