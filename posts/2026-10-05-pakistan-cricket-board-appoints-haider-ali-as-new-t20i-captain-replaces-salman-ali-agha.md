This move comes as the PCB aims to recalibrate the team's direction following recent performance outcomes.

His tenure included T20I series defeats to Afghanistan and, more recently, to Zimbabwe. These outcomes prompted a review by the national selection committee and the PCB hierarchy, culminating in the decision to seek new leadership for the shortest format of the game. The shift indicates a focus on revitalization and strategic planning ahead of upcoming international commitments.

Haider Ali steps into the role with prior captaincy experience, notably leading the Pakistan team to a silver medal at the Asian Games. His leadership during that tournament, where Pakistan showcased a promising performance, is understood to have been a key factor in the PCB's decision.
