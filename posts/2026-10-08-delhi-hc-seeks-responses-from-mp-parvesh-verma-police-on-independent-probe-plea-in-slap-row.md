The Delhi High Court has formally sought responses from Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) Member of Parliament Parvesh Verma and the Delhi Police concerning a petition that calls for an independent inquiry into an alleged "slap row." The court's directive, issued during recent proceedings, mandates both respondents to file their detailed replies regarding the allegations and the petitioner's demand for an unbiased investigation.

The petition was filed by an individual alleging involvement in an incident, referred to as the "slap row," and has raised concerns about the impartiality and thoroughness of the ongoing investigation by the Delhi Police. The petitioner has asserted that the existing probe by local police authorities is insufficient and may not deliver justice, thereby necessitating a review by an independent agency or oversight body. The plea argues that due to the involvement of a prominent public figure, an independent probe is essential to ensure transparency and maintain public trust in the investigative process.

During the hearing, the court reviewed the arguments presented by the petitioner's counsel, who highlighted perceived shortcomings in the current investigation. While specific details of the alleged "slap row" were not extensively deliberated in the court's initial directive, the focus remained on the procedural aspects of the probe and the petitioner's right to a fair and impartial investigation.

The court, after considering the submissions, issued notices to both MP Parvesh Verma and the Commissioner of Delhi Police. They have been given a specific timeframe to furnish their respective affidavits and counter-affidavits. These responses are expected to address the allegations made in the petition, clarify the current status of any ongoing police investigation, and explain the stance of the respondents regarding the demand for an independent inquiry.

The development underscores the judicial system's role in scrutinizing investigative processes, particularly when allegations involve public officials. It signifies the court's willingness to examine claims of bias or inadequacy in investigations, ensuring that due process is followed. This legal action highlights the ongoing public and legal scrutiny faced by individuals in positions of power concerning their conduct.

Key aspects of the court's order include:
*   Issuance of notices to MP Parvesh Verma and Delhi Police.
*   Requirement for detailed responses to the independent probe plea.
*   Focus on ensuring impartiality and thoroughness in the investigation of the alleged "slap row."
*   The court will evaluate the necessity for an independent body based on the submitted replies.

The matter is now scheduled for further hearing on a future date, by which time both Parvesh Verma and the Delhi Police are expected to have submitted their detailed responses. The court will then consider these replies, along with any further submissions from the petitioner, before determining the next course of action regarding the plea for an independent investigation into the alleged incident. The outcome of these proceedings could set a precedent for similar cases involving public officials and demands for transparency in police investigations.
