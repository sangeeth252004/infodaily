The United States Treasury Department announced sanctions on two Mumbai-based companies and five Indian individuals on Wednesday, June 12, 2024, for their alleged involvement in facilitating the sale and shipment of Iranian petroleum and petrochemical products. This action is part of a broader U.S. effort to disrupt revenue streams for the Iranian regime, particularly those funding its destabilizing activities and proliferation efforts.

The sanctioned entities include Tibia Montaz Private Limited and Sibiaa Marine Private Limited, both registered in Mumbai, India. Alongside these firms, five Indian nationals—Vinit Tibrewala, Archana Tibrewala, Shivam Tibrewala, Manoj Kumar Jain, and Ashraf Ansari—were also designated under the latest round of sanctions. The U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) stated that these individuals and companies participated in an extensive network spanning Iran, the United Arab Emirates, and China, designed to circumvent existing international sanctions on Iran's energy sector.

According to the U.S. Treasury, the designated Indian entities and individuals provided critical services, including financial and logistical support, enabling the illicit trade of Iranian commodities. These activities are alleged to have played a role in generating revenue for the Iranian government and its associated entities, including the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). The U.S. government maintains that these revenues directly contribute to Iran's military and regional proxy groups.

The sanctions entail significant restrictions. All property and interests in property of the designated individuals and entities that are within U.S. jurisdiction, or in the possession or control of U.S. persons, are blocked and must be reported to OFAC. Additionally, any entities owned 50 percent or more by one or more blocked persons are also subject to these sanctions. The regulations generally prohibit all transactions by U.S. persons or within (or transiting) the United States that involve any property or interests in property of designated or otherwise blocked persons.

This action underscores the U.S. Treasury’s continued commitment to enforcing sanctions against Iran's oil trade. The department highlighted its resolve to target actors globally who facilitate the illegal sale of Iranian petroleum and petrochemicals, emphasizing that such networks enable the Iranian regime to fund activities deemed destabilizing to regional and international security.

*   **Sanctioned Indian Entities and Individuals:**
    *   Tibia Montaz Private Limited (Mumbai-based firm)
    *   Sibiaa Marine Private Limited (Mumbai-based firm)
    *   Vinit Tibrewala (Indian national)
    *   Archana Tibrewala (Indian national)
    *   Shivam Tibrewala (Indian national)
    *   Manoj Kumar Jain (Indian national)
    *   Ashraf Ansari (Indian national)

The U.S. Treasury Department indicated that it would continue to pursue and disrupt networks that provide financial, material, or technological support to Iran's petroleum and petrochemical industries. This ongoing enforcement effort aims to further limit Iran's ability to finance activities deemed contrary to U.S. national security interests.
