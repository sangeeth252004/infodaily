Former Pakistan cricket captain Shahid Afridi recently revealed that veteran Indian actor Nana Patekar maintained regular contact and offered support to his mother during her year-long illness. The disclosure was made by Afridi during an appearance on "The Shakeel Siddiqui Show" podcast, shedding light on an unexpected cross-border bond.

Afridi's mother battled cancer for approximately one year before her passing in October 2023. During this period, Patekar reportedly called Afridi's mother every week to inquire about her health and provide emotional strength. Afridi expressed deep gratitude for the actor's consistent efforts, describing Patekar as a "great human being" who stood by his family during a challenging time.

The former cricketer elaborated on the nature of the support, stating that Patekar’s calls were a source of comfort for his ailing mother. This gesture transcended the typical rivalries often associated with India-Pakistan relations, particularly in the realm of sports. Afridi indicated that the connection was facilitated by Pakistani artist Shakeel Siddiqui, who informed Patekar about his mother's health condition. Patekar had also previously visited Afridi's mother in the hospital, underscoring the depth of his concern.

Afridi's account highlighted the personal nature of Patekar's involvement, moving beyond public recognition to offer private, consistent comfort. The revelation has drawn attention to instances of humanitarian gestures that bridge national divides.

Key details from Afridi's disclosure include:
*   **Recipient of Support:** Shahid Afridi's mother, who was battling cancer.
*   **Duration of Illness:** Approximately one year.
*   **Actor's Engagement:** Nana Patekar called Afridi's mother weekly.
*   **Nature of Support:** Emotional check-ins and expressions of concern.
*   **Facilitator:** Pakistani artist Shakeel Siddiqui reportedly connected Patekar with Afridi's family regarding the illness.
*   **Previous Interaction:** Patekar had visited Afridi's mother in the hospital prior to her passing.
*   **Outcome:** Afridi's mother passed away in October 2023.

The former cricket captain’s public acknowledgement of Patekar’s actions has resonated with audiences, showcasing a personal connection between figures from the sports and entertainment industries across borders. The interaction underscores the potential for individual acts of kindness to foster goodwill, irrespective of geopolitical landscapes. No further statements have been issued by either Afridi or Patekar regarding the matter since the podcast revelation.
