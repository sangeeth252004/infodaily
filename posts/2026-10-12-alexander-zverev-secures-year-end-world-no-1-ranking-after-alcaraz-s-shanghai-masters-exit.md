Alexander Zverev has been officially confirmed as the year-end men's singles world No. 1, a significant development in the ATP rankings that followed Carlos Alcaraz's unexpected defeat at the recent Shanghai Masters. The confirmation positions Zverev at the pinnacle of men's professional tennis for the 2023 season, marking a career milestone for the German athlete. This outcome materialized after Alcaraz, a strong contender for the top spot, was eliminated in an earlier round of the tournament held in Shanghai, China.

The year-end world No. 1 ranking is awarded to the player who accumulates the most ranking points throughout the entire calendar year, reflecting consistent performance across Grand Slams, ATP Masters 1000 events, and other tour tournaments. Securing this position signifies a player's dominance over the course of a season, cementing their status as the sport's leading figure for that period. For Zverev, this achievement represents a return to the sport's upper echelons, having navigated periods of injury and competition in previous seasons.

Carlos Alcaraz, who had been vying for the year-end top spot, suffered a third-round defeat at the Rolex Shanghai Masters. The Spanish world No. 2 was upset by Grigor Dimitrov of Bulgaria in a match that concluded with a score of 5-7, 6-2, 6-4. Alcaraz's exit from the tournament prevented him from accruing the necessary points to surpass Zverev in the year-end standings. Prior to Shanghai, Alcaraz had achieved considerable success, including his Wimbledon title earlier in the season, contributing to his high ranking and contention for the year-end position.

Zverev's ascent to the year-end No. 1 ranking reflects a robust season marked by consistent results and strong performances. His 2023 campaign has included significant wins and deep runs in multiple tournaments, accumulating the points necessary to secure the top year-end position once his closest rivals could no longer mathematically overtake him. While specific tournament victories directly sealing the No. 1 position were not pinpointed as the sole factor, his overall body of work throughout the year established his leading point total.
