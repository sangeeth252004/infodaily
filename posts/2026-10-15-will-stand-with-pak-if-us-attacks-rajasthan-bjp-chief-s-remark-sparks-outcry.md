C.P. Joshi, the President of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) in Rajasthan and a Member of Parliament from Chittorgarh, recently made a controversial statement suggesting India would support Pakistan in the event of a United States attack. The remark, delivered on May 25, 2024, during a public address, has rapidly escalated into a political controversy, drawing sharp condemnation from the opposition Indian National Congress and sparking widespread debate.

Mr. Joshi's statement, reportedly made during an election rally in Rajasthan, was quoted as, "If America attacks Pakistan, we will stand with Pakistan." This comment has been viewed critically by various political commentators and opposition parties, who argue it deviates from India's long-standing diplomatic principles and could potentially complicate international relations. The timing of the remark, amidst the ongoing national election cycle, has further intensified the political discourse surrounding it.

The Indian National Congress swiftly denounced Mr. Joshi’s remarks, characterizing them as irresponsible and potentially detrimental to India’s foreign policy standing.

*   Congress leader Pawan Khera stated, "This is the true face of BJP," criticizing the statement as unpatriotic and contradictory to India's interests.
*   Another prominent Congress figure, Gaurav Gogoi, labeled the statement as "anti-national," questioning the BJP's stance on sensitive geopolitical issues.

Critics from the opposition emphasized that such a statement from a senior leader of the ruling party could send confusing signals regarding India's diplomatic position, particularly concerning its strategic partnerships with the United States and its complex historical relationship with Pakistan. They argued that the remark could be misinterpreted on the international stage, potentially affecting India's carefully cultivated diplomatic balance.

In response to the growing controversy, the BJP has reportedly distanced itself from Mr. Joshi's comments. While no comprehensive national party statement was immediately issued, reports indicate that the BJP has clarified that the statement represents Mr. Joshi's personal views and does not reflect the official policy or stand of the party. This move is aimed at mitigating the political fallout and reaffirming the party's adherence to established foreign policy norms.

The incident underscores the heightened scrutiny on public statements made by prominent political figures during election periods. As the national elections progress, the controversy is expected to remain a talking point in political rallies and media discussions. Opposition parties are likely to continue pressing the BJP for further clarification or action regarding Mr. Joshi's remarks, maintaining pressure on the ruling party to formally address the implications of such statements. The episode highlights the delicate balance between political rhetoric and diplomatic responsibility, particularly for leaders holding significant public office.
