Canadian entrepreneur David Ingram has listed "Sylvan Rock," an Aston Martin-designed luxury residence in New York's Hudson Valley, for $19.5 million. The 5.5-acre compound, located approximately two hours north of Manhattan, represents the first private residential project undertaken by the British luxury automotive manufacturer. Ingram acquired the property as a rendering in 2020, and the completed estate is now available on the market through Corcoran Country Living.

The listing signifies a growing trend among ultra-luxury brands to extend their design philosophies beyond their core products into high-end real estate. Aston Martin's foray into residential architecture with "Sylvan Rock" highlights its diversification strategy and aim to offer a complete luxury lifestyle experience to ultra-high-net-worth individuals (UHNWIs). The Hudson Valley region itself has experienced increased demand for exclusive properties, making it a strategic location for such a distinctive offering.

Developed in collaboration with renowned architectural firm Adjaye Associates, the residence features a distinctive design characterized by sharp angles and geometric forms, integrated into the natural landscape. The property spans 5,983 square feet of living space in its main residence, offering three bedrooms, three full bathrooms, and two half bathrooms.

Key features and amenities of the "Sylvan Rock" estate include:
*   **Architecture and Design:** Floor-to-ceiling windows, custom furniture and finishes by Aston Martin, black walnut millwork, leather detailing, and bronze accents throughout the interior.
*   **Comprehensive Compound:** In addition to the main residence, the property includes three standalone guest pods, a treehouse, a poolhouse, and a dedicated spa and integrated wellness facility.
*   **Automotive Integration:** A custom garage designed to house a car collection, with specific provisions for an Aston Martin DBX SUV, underscoring the brand's identity.
*   **Infrastructure:** A helipad is incorporated into the estate, facilitating private access for residents.

The sale of "Sylvan Rock" is positioned to appeal to buyers seeking a blend of architectural innovation, bespoke luxury, and brand heritage. While the initial purchase price by Ingram in 2020 was not disclosed, its current listing price of $19.5 million reflects the unique value proposition of a branded residence designed by a globally recognized luxury marque. This venture illustrates the evolving landscape of luxury real estate, where brand association and unique design increasingly contribute to property value and market appeal. The successful sale of "Sylvan Rock" could further encourage luxury brands to explore similar residential design and development opportunities globally.
