Mumbai, India – Kaizad Bharucha, Deputy Managing Director of HDFC Bank, has announced his decision to resign from his position, with his tenure slated to conclude on March 31, 2025. This development marks a significant leadership transition at India's largest private sector lender and precedes the anticipated succession of Anup Bagchi as the bank's next Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director.

Bharucha's departure comes as HDFC Bank navigates the integration phase following its merger with HDFC Ltd. He has been a pivotal figure in the bank's growth and operational stability for nearly three decades. His exit is part of a broader leadership reshuffle as the institution plans for its future strategic direction. Anup Bagchi, currently an Executive Director at HDFC Bank, is slated to take on the top role, pending necessary regulatory approvals from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

*   **Kaizad Bharucha's Tenure:** Bharucha joined HDFC Bank in 1995 and steadily rose through the ranks. He has held diverse responsibilities across key areas, including wholesale credit, credit and market risk, retail credit, business banking, operations, and finance. His contributions were particularly noted in establishing robust credit underwriting and risk management frameworks.
*   **Anup Bagchi's Background:** Bagchi brings extensive experience to his anticipated role. Prior to his current position as Executive Director at HDFC Bank, he served as the Managing Director and CEO of ICICI Securities and held various leadership positions at ICICI Bank, focusing on retail liability and wealth management. His appointment is expected to steer the bank's next phase of growth and digital transformation.
*   **Current CEO and MD Status:** The bank's current Managing Director and CEO, Sashidhar Jagdishan, had his tenure recently extended by the Reserve Bank of India until October 2026. This creates a dynamic leadership context, with Bagchi's expected succession forming part of the bank's long-term strategic leadership planning.

HDFC Bank, following its mega-merger with parent HDFC Ltd. in July 2023, has become one of the world's largest banks by market capitalization. The merger consolidated a substantial loan book and diversified portfolio, making leadership continuity and strategic vision critical for its future performance.

The bank's board of directors will proceed with formal procedures regarding Bharucha's resignation and seek regulatory clearance for Anup Bagchi's appointment as CEO and MD. This transition is expected to be managed to ensure seamless operations and continued focus on the bank's strategic objectives, including further integrating the merged entities and expanding its market footprint both domestically and internationally. Further announcements are anticipated as the regulatory processes unfold.
