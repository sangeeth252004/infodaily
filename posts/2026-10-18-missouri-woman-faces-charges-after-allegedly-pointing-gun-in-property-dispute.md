A 60-year-old Missouri woman has been arrested and charged following an incident where she allegedly pointed a gun at a neighbor during an ongoing property line dispute. The confrontation occurred as the neighbor was reportedly fixing a fence, according to police statements.

Authorities responded to reports of the incident, which is the latest development in a months-long disagreement between the neighbors over the shared property boundary. Police reports indicate that the dispute escalated when the neighbor, a Missouri man, was performing maintenance on a fence. Video evidence reportedly shows the woman pointing a firearm in the direction of the fence line during the altercation.

The woman was subsequently taken into custody and faces multiple charges related to the incident. While specific charges may vary by jurisdiction, reports indicate charges such as felony assault, unlawful use of a weapon, and armed criminal action are being considered or have been filed. These charges reflect the gravity of the alleged threat involving a firearm.

Following her arrest, the woman appeared in court where a bond was set at $500,000. This substantial amount reflects the serious nature of the allegations and is a standard measure to ensure a defendant's appearance in future court proceedings. The ongoing property dispute, which has reportedly been active for several months, provides the background context for the alleged confrontation.

The legal process is now underway, with the woman expected to face further court hearings to address the charges. The resolution of the criminal case will depend on the evidence presented by prosecutors and the defense. Property line disputes, while often resolved through civil means, can escalate to criminal charges when threats or violence are alleged. This incident underscores the potential for neighborly disagreements to lead to serious legal consequences.
