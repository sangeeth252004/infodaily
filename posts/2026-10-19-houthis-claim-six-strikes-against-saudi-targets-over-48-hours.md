Yemen's Houthi rebels, through their military spokesperson Yahya Saree, announced on Monday that their forces conducted six military operations targeting strategic sites in Saudi Arabia over a 48-hour period. These alleged strikes reportedly included Saudi airports, an oil field belonging to Saudi Aramco, and various military installations. There has been no immediate confirmation or comment from Saudi authorities regarding these claims.

According to Saree's statement, the claimed operations involved the use of both Qasef-2K drones and ballistic missiles. The alleged targets were identified as Jizan International Airport and Abha International Airport, both located in Saudi Arabia's southwestern region. Additionally, the Houthis claimed to have targeted an unspecified Saudi Aramco oil field and several military sites within the same geographical area.

The Houthi movement has frequently launched drone and missile attacks into Saudi Arabia, citing retaliation for the Saudi-led coalition's military intervention in Yemen, which began in March 2015. The coalition supports the internationally recognized Yemeni government against the Houthis, who control the capital Sanaa and much of the country's north. These ongoing hostilities have significantly impacted regional stability and international shipping lanes.

Key details of the Houthis' claims include:
*   **Targeted Airports:** Jizan International Airport and Abha International Airport, both critical civilian and military aviation hubs in southwestern Saudi Arabia.
*   **Energy Infrastructure:** An unnamed Saudi Aramco oil field, which, if confirmed, could represent a significant disruption attempt to global energy supplies. Saudi Aramco is the world's largest oil producer.
*   **Military Sites:** Multiple military installations located in the southwestern region of Saudi Arabia.
*   **Weaponry Used:** Qasef-2K drones and ballistic missiles.
*   **Timeframe:** Six separate operations conducted over a 48-hour period, as stated by the Houthi spokesperson.

The reported operations occur amidst persistent diplomatic efforts to find a lasting resolution to the Yemeni conflict. The targeting of airports and oil facilities carries potential implications for both regional security and the global energy market. While the Houthis routinely issue claims of such attacks, Saudi Arabia does not always confirm every incident, particularly those resulting in no significant damage or casualties. The absence of immediate confirmation from Riyadh aligns with past patterns following similar Houthi announcements.

As the conflict in Yemen continues into its ninth year, international observers will monitor the situation for any verified damage reports, Saudi responses, or further developments regarding these claimed strikes. The broader context of Red Sea maritime security and ongoing regional tensions remains a focal point for international stakeholders.
