Significant details have emerged during court proceedings regarding an alleged attack on Tasmanian MP Cassy O'Connor in Hobart in October 2023. Gavin Glenn O'Connor, 49, appeared in the Hobart Magistrates Court in December 2023, facing charges including attempted murder and driving a motor vehicle in a manner dangerous to the public causing bodily harm. The court heard allegations detailing the incident that led to Ms. O'Connor sustaining a fractured wrist and other abrasions.

The prosecution outlined a sequence of events alleging a deliberate act. It was heard that Ms. O'Connor was walking near her Hobart office on October 25, 2023, when a vehicle, allegedly driven by Mr. O'Connor, swerved towards her, striking her leg. Following this initial impact, the vehicle reportedly performed a U-turn and accelerated, allegedly hitting Ms. O'Connor a second time. She was subsequently transported to Royal Hobart Hospital for treatment of her injuries.

During the bail application hearing, Prosecutor Sarah Mollard opposed bail, citing the gravity of the charges and the alleged premeditated nature of the actions. Mollard reportedly told the court that the accused allegedly "did a U-turn to hit her again," emphasizing the intent inferred from the alleged second impact. The prosecution argued that the accused posed an unacceptable flight risk and a potential danger to the community, including Ms. O'Connor.

Specific charges laid against Gavin Glenn O'Connor include:
*   Attempted Murder
*   Driving a Motor Vehicle in a Manner Dangerous to the Public Causing Bodily Harm
*   Dangerous Driving

Magistrate Michael Daly presided over the hearing. After considering the submissions from both the prosecution and the defense, Magistrate Daly denied bail for Mr. O'Connor. The accused was remanded into custody.
