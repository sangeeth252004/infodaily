Brisbane, Australia – Australian cricketer Steve Smith reached a significant career milestone on Friday, January 26, 2024, by surpassing 11,000 Test runs during the second Test against the West Indies at The Gabba. This achievement places him in an exclusive group as only the third Australian player to reach this figure, joining former captains Ricky Ponting and Allan Border.

Smith, 34, achieved the milestone in his 109th Test match and 205th innings, making him the fifth-fastest batsman in the history of Test cricket to accumulate 11,000 runs in terms of innings played. His consistent scoring rate has been a hallmark of his career since his debut in 2010. The right-handed batsman reached the figure with a single off West Indies bowler Alzarri Joseph.

The Sydney-born batsman now stands alongside two of Australia's most prolific run-scorers. Ricky Ponting holds the record for the most Test runs by an Australian with 13,378 runs, followed by Allan Border with 11,174 runs. Smith’s entry into this cohort underscores his sustained impact on the Australian Test team's batting lineup over more than a decade.

Globally, Smith's achievement places him among an elite set of batsmen who have reached the 11,000-run mark. The list of players who achieved this feat in fewer innings includes:
*   Kumar Sangakkara (Sri Lanka): 195 innings
*   Brian Lara (West Indies): 195 innings
*   Sachin Tendulkar (India): 200 innings
*   Mahela Jayawardene (Sri Lanka): 201 innings

Smith's Test average, which has historically been among the highest for batsmen with over 5,000 runs, reflects his run-scoring consistency. This milestone was achieved during a day-night Test match, adding to the list of memorable moments in his career. His ongoing presence in the Australian top order continues to be a key component of their Test strategy.

The second Test against the West Indies is part of a two-match series. Smith’s innings contribution will be a factor in Australia's efforts to secure a series victory. His performance continues to be a focus for the team as they navigate future international fixtures.
