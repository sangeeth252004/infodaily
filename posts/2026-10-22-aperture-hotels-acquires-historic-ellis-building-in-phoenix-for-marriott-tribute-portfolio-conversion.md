Aperture Hotels has acquired the historic Ellis Building in downtown Phoenix for $10.3 million, with plans to convert the nearly century-old structure into a Marriott Tribute Portfolio hotel. The transaction involves the six-story office tower, originally constructed in 1922, and represents a notable adaptive reuse project aimed at expanding hospitality options within the city’s evolving urban core. The future hotel is projected to feature approximately 100 rooms.

The acquisition by Aperture Hotels underscores a continued trend in urban development towards repurposing historic buildings for modern commercial uses, particularly within the hospitality sector. The Ellis Building, recognized for its architectural heritage, will undergo a significant transformation to accommodate contemporary hotel amenities while preserving its historical facade and character. This strategy aligns with Marriott's Tribute Portfolio brand, which emphasizes independent hotels offering unique designs and vibrant social scenes, often within culturally rich or historic settings.

Downtown Phoenix has experienced substantial redevelopment and investment in recent years, drawing new residents, businesses, and visitors. The introduction of a Marriott Tribute Portfolio property is expected to contribute to this revitalization by providing a distinctive lodging option that caters to both business and leisure travelers seeking an authentic local experience. Such projects typically enhance local tourism infrastructure and can stimulate economic activity through construction phases and subsequent hotel operations.

Key details regarding the Ellis Building conversion include:

*   **Acquisition Price:** $10.3 million
*   **Property:** Ellis Building, a six-story office tower
*   **Original Construction Year:** 1922
*   **Location:** Downtown Phoenix, Arizona
*   **Developer/Owner:** Aperture Hotels
*   **Planned Hotel Brand:** Marriott Tribute Portfolio
*   **Projected Room Count:** Approximately 100 rooms

The adaptive reuse of the Ellis Building into a hotel also addresses current market demands for unique accommodations and sustainable development practices. By repurposing an existing structure, the project contributes to urban density and minimizes new construction impacts, aligning with broader environmental and urban planning objectives. The decision by Aperture Hotels to partner with Marriott's Tribute Portfolio suggests a strategic focus on delivering a high-quality, distinctive guest experience that leverages the building's historical appeal.

Following the acquisition, the project will proceed through various stages including detailed architectural design, permitting, and construction. While specific timelines for groundbreaking and completion have not been publicly disclosed, the conversion of a building of this age and stature typically involves extensive planning and renovation work. Once operational, the new Marriott Tribute Portfolio hotel is anticipated to become a fixture in downtown Phoenix's growing array of boutique hotel offerings, further cementing the area's status as a dynamic urban destination.
