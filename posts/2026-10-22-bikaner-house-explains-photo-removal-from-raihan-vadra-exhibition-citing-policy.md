New Delhi, India – Bikaner House management recently confirmed the removal of several photographs from "The Dark Divide," a debut solo exhibition by photographer Raihan Vadra. The cultural venue stated the decision was made because the images were "inconsistent with policy" that prohibits political content within its art spaces.

The exhibition, which opened on May 9 and is scheduled to conclude on May 15, showcases black and white photographs of the Himalayas by Raihan Vadra. However, photographs depicting prominent political figures, specifically Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi, and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, were removed following the management's directive.

Bikaner House issued a statement clarifying its position, emphasizing that it operates as a facility dedicated "for the promotion of art and culture and not for political purposes." This policy aims to maintain the venue's neutral stance and focus purely on artistic expression, free from political association or endorsement. The management asserted that all exhibitors are made aware of these guidelines to ensure compliance.

Sources close to Raihan Vadra indicated that the removed photographs were part of a broader collection depicting moments of everyday life, rather than being intended as political statements or endorsements. The exhibition primarily features landscapes and other non-political subjects. The incident has drawn attention to the interpretation and enforcement of content policies within cultural institutions that host public art displays.

Key details regarding the incident:
*   **Artist:** Raihan Vadra.
*   **Exhibition Title:** "The Dark Divide."
*   **Venue:** Bikaner House, New Delhi.
*   **Exhibition Dates:** May 9 to May 15, 2024.
*   **Removed Content:** Photographs featuring political figures Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi, and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra.
*   **Reason for Removal:** Bikaner House stated the content was "inconsistent with policy," specifying the venue's purpose is for art and culture, not politics.

The exhibition continues at Bikaner House with the remaining collection of Raihan Vadra's photographic works, focusing on the landscapes and themes originally intended for "The Dark Divide." The management has not indicated any further changes to the exhibition schedule or content beyond the initial removals. The incident highlights the operational considerations for cultural institutions balancing artistic expression with established venue policies.
