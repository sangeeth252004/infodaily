The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the city intimation slips for the Uttar Pradesh Special Teacher Eligibility Test (UP Special TET) 2026. Registered candidates can now access and download their respective intimation slips from the official UPESSC website, upessc.up.gov.in. This release precedes the actual examination, which is scheduled to take place on November 3, 2026.

The primary function of the city intimation slip is to inform candidates about the specific city where their examination center will be located. This advance notification is designed to assist applicants in making necessary travel and accommodation arrangements well before the examination date. It is crucial for candidates to understand that this slip is not the admit card; it will not grant entry into the examination hall. The final admit cards, which contain precise details including the full address of the examination center, reporting times, and comprehensive instructions, are expected to be issued by UPESSC closer to the exam date.

The UP Special TET 2026 is a significant qualifying examination for individuals aspiring to teach in various educational institutions across Uttar Pradesh. Success in this test is a mandatory criterion for eligibility in teacher recruitment processes within the state. The issuance of these city intimation slips represents a pivotal phase in the examination cycle, confirming the progression towards the scheduled test date.

To download their city intimation slips, candidates are generally required to log in to the official portal using their registration number and date of birth or a designated password. The slip typically displays essential information such as the candidate's name, roll number, application number, and the allotted examination district. Candidates are advised to meticulously review all particulars printed on their slip for accuracy and immediately report any discrepancies to the concerned authorities.

Key details regarding the UP Special TET 2026 and the intimation slip release include:

*   **Issuing Authority:** Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC)
*   **Official Website for Download:** upessc.up.gov.in
*   **Purpose:** To inform candidates of their designated examination city in advance
*   **Examination Name:** Uttar Pradesh Special Teacher Eligibility Test (UP Special TET) 2026
*   **Examination Date:** November 3, 2026
*   **Login Credentials:** Typically requires registration number and password/date of birth

Candidates are strongly advised to download and secure a printout of their city intimation slip for their records. While this document confirms the examination city, the official admit cards, which are essential for entry into the examination hall, are anticipated to be released approximately one to two weeks prior to November 3, 2026. Applicants should maintain regular checks on the official UPESSC website for further announcements regarding the admit card release and any other updates pertaining to the UP Special TET 2026.
