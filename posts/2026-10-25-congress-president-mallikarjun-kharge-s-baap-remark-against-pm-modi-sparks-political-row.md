Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge recently became the subject of a significant political controversy following a remark directed at Prime Minister Narendra Modi. During a public address, Mr. Kharge reportedly used the term "baap" in reference to the Prime Minister, a statement that quickly drew sharp criticism and condemnation from the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), leading to an escalation of political tensions between the two major parties.

The remark, made during a political rally in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, prompted immediate and widespread condemnation from BJP leaders, who characterized it as disrespectful and indicative of a decline in political discourse standards. While the full context of Mr. Kharge's statement has been debated, the use of the colloquial Hindi term "baap" (father), which can imply superiority, authority, or even disdain depending on the inflection and surrounding dialogue, was deemed inappropriate by the BJP. This incident highlights ongoing friction and intensified rhetoric between political parties, particularly in the lead-up to upcoming state and general elections.

BJP representatives swiftly responded to the statement, demanding an apology from the Congress President. Several prominent BJP figures criticized the remark, asserting that it demonstrated a lack of decorum expected from a leader of a national party. They argued that such language demeans the office of the Prime Minister and sets a poor precedent for political debate. The ruling party utilized the incident to portray Congress as engaging in personal attacks rather than focusing on substantive policy discussions.

Specific reactions from various BJP leaders included:
*   **Union Minister Pralhad Joshi** stated that Kharge's comments reflected frustration within the Congress ranks and amounted to an insult to the nation's leader, indicating a disregard for democratic traditions.
*   **BJP Spokesperson Shehzad Poonawalla** termed the remark "shameful" and publicly questioned whether the Congress high command endorsed such language from its president. He further suggested a pattern of disrespectful comments from Congress leaders directed towards the Prime Minister.
*   **Other BJP leaders** across various platforms echoed these sentiments, condemning the remark and reiterating their demand for an unequivocal apology from Mr. Kharge.

Conversely, the Congress party and Mr. Kharge's office have largely defended the statement, suggesting it was either taken out of context or intended as a colloquial expression without malicious intent. Some Congress spokespersons indicated that the BJP was overreacting to divert public attention from more pressing national issues, such as economic challenges or government policies. They maintained that Mr. Kharge's extensive track record as a seasoned politician does not suggest an intent to personally disparage the Prime Minister or his office.

The exchange underscores the currently charged political atmosphere in India, where statements made by top leaders are often scrutinized intensely and amplified through both traditional and social media channels. Political analysts observe that such remarks, regardless of intent, frequently become significant talking points that can influence public perception and shape voter sentiment, particularly during electorally sensitive periods.

The controversy is expected to continue resonating in political circles for the foreseeable future, with both parties likely to utilize the incident in their respective campaigns. The BJP is anticipated to maintain pressure on Congress for a public apology, while Congress leadership may continue to clarify the intent behind the remark or deflect criticism. This incident adds another layer to the already contentious political landscape, potentially influencing campaign narratives and public discourse ahead of key electoral battles, emphasizing the ongoing debate about decorum and civility in public statements made by political leaders.
