New Delhi has issued a formal advisory to Indian nationals residing in Saudi Arabia, urging them to exercise heightened vigilance and caution amid ongoing missile and drone attacks by Houthi rebels. The advisory specifically instructs citizens to refrain from filming or distributing footage of missile interceptions or strikes, emphasizing adherence to local safety protocols.

The directive, disseminated through India's diplomatic missions in Saudi Arabia, highlights the prevailing security situation in the region. It underscores the potential risks associated with the ongoing conflict originating from Yemen, where the Houthi movement has frequently targeted assets and infrastructure in neighboring countries, including Saudi Arabia, as well as commercial shipping in the Red Sea. The advisory serves as a proactive measure by the Indian government to ensure the safety and well-being of its expatriate community.

The explicit instruction to avoid filming missile incidents is significant. It aims to prevent citizens from inadvertently placing themselves in harm's way by approaching active conflict zones or contributing to the spread of potentially sensitive or unverified information. Such actions could also contravene local security regulations in Saudi Arabia, which maintains strict controls over reporting on security incidents.

Key aspects of the advisory include:
*   **Vigilance:** Nationals are advised to remain alert to their surroundings and any potential threats.
*   **Avoidance of Affected Areas:** A recommendation to steer clear of locations that may be targeted or are close to ongoing security incidents.
*   **Registration with Embassy:** Encouragement for all Indian citizens to register their presence with the Indian Embassy in Riyadh or the Consulate General in Jeddah to facilitate communication and assistance during emergencies.
*   **Adherence to Local Laws:** Strict compliance with all local laws and regulations concerning public behavior and media sharing, especially regarding security matters.
*   **No Filming or Sharing:** A direct instruction not to film, photograph, or disseminate any content related to missile or drone interceptions, impacts, or associated damage.

The advisory comes against a backdrop of increased regional tensions, particularly in the Red Sea area. Houthi forces, who control large parts of Yemen, have intensified their attacks on commercial shipping lanes and have continued to launch missiles and drones towards Saudi Arabian territory. These actions have drawn international condemnation and have prompted several nations to issue updated travel and security advisories for their citizens in the Gulf region.

Indian diplomatic missions continue to monitor the security landscape closely. Nationals in Saudi Arabia are encouraged to stay informed through official channels and remain prepared for any potential developments. The guidance emphasizes that citizen safety remains the paramount concern for the Indian government in the evolving regional security environment.
