At least four individuals were found fatally shot at a hotel in Guwahati, Assam, leading local law enforcement to initiate an immediate and extensive manhunt for the suspects involved. The incident, which occurred recently in the northeastern Indian city, has prompted a significant police response, with authorities working to secure the scene and gather critical evidence.

Upon receiving reports of the shooting, police units were dispatched to the hotel, located in an undisclosed part of Guwahati. The area was swiftly cordoned off to ensure the integrity of the crime scene and to facilitate the commencement of forensic procedures. Initial assessments confirmed the deaths of at least four victims, all of whom sustained gunshot wounds. Details regarding the identities of the deceased, potential motives, or the number of assailants have not yet been publicly released by investigators as the active probe continues.

The severity of a multiple homicide has elevated the incident to a high priority for the Assam Police. Specialized units, including criminal investigation teams, have been deployed to assist in the operation. The manhunt involves coordination across various police departments, utilizing resources to track down individuals believed to be connected to the killings. Efforts are focused on examining surveillance footage from the hotel and surrounding areas, interviewing potential witnesses, and pursuing any leads that may emerge from the preliminary investigation.

*   **Police Response:** Immediate deployment of multiple units, establishment of a secure perimeter.
*   **Investigation Focus:** Forensic analysis of the crime scene, collection of physical evidence, review of security camera footage, pursuit of leads.
*   **Public Safety:** Authorities have assured the public that all necessary measures are being taken to apprehend those responsible and maintain law and order.

The incident has raised concerns within the local community regarding public safety. Authorities have urged residents to remain vigilant and to report any suspicious activity directly to the police. While the investigation is in its early stages, the intensity of the manhunt underscores the commitment of law enforcement to swiftly resolve the case and bring the perpetrators to justice.

As of the latest reports, the manhunt for the suspects remains active and ongoing. Police officials have stated that updates will be provided as significant developments occur, urging discretion to avoid hindering the investigation. The focus remains on apprehending those responsible and understanding the full circumstances that led to the tragic deaths at the Guwahati hotel.
