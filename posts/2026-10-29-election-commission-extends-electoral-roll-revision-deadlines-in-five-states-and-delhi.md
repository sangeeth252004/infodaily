The Election Commission of India (ECI) has extended the deadlines for the ongoing Special Summary Revision (SSR) of electoral rolls, often referred to as "SIR" in official communications, in five Indian states and the National Capital Territory of Delhi. This decision involves new dates for the final publication of updated voter lists, a critical step in ensuring the accuracy and comprehensiveness of democratic records.

The extension provides an additional window for citizens to participate in the crucial process of updating voter information. Special Summary Revision is a routine exercise conducted by the ECI to allow eligible individuals to register as voters, verify their existing details, and submit corrections or requests for deletion of incorrect entries. This cycle is particularly significant for young citizens turning 18 years of age on the designated qualifying dates, enabling their inclusion in the electoral framework.

While specific earlier deadlines were in place for various stages of the revision, the ECI's recent notification outlines revised timelines for activities such as the filing of claims and objections, their subsequent disposal by electoral authorities, and the final updates to the electoral database. The Election Commission has now set definitive new dates for the final publication of the electoral rolls in the affected regions.

*   **Affected Regions:** Five unnamed Indian states and the National Capital Territory of Delhi.
*   **Purpose of Extension:** To facilitate greater participation in voter registration and ensure more accurate and inclusive electoral rolls.
*   **Key Process:** Special Summary Revision (SSR) involves adding new voters, correcting existing entries, and removing duplicate or deceased entries.
*   **Outcome:** New dates have been stipulated for the ultimate publication of the updated voter lists.

The Special Summary Revision is fundamental to upholding the integrity and fairness of the electoral process. By maintaining updated and accurate voter lists, the ECI aims to prevent discrepancies and ensure that every eligible citizen has the opportunity to exercise their franchise. The extension reflects the Commission's commitment to facilitating voter inclusion and streamlining the administrative procedures involved in electoral roll management.

The updated electoral rolls, once finally published following these revised timelines, will serve as the foundational documents for upcoming elections in the concerned states and Delhi. The Election Commission continues to oversee this meticulous process to support the conduct of free, fair, and inclusive elections across the country.
