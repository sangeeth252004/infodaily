Thiruvananthapuram, India – New Zealand cricketer Glenn Phillips stands on the brink of setting a new T20 International world record for the most sixes hit in a single calendar year. The powerful middle-order batsman requires two more maximums in the upcoming final T20i match against India, scheduled for October 27, 2023, at the Greenfield International Stadium, to surpass the current benchmark.

Phillips, known for his explosive hitting, has accumulated 49 sixes in T20 International cricket throughout 2023, placing him just one short of equalling the standing record of 50, currently held by West Indies' Evin Lewis, set in 2021. Should Phillips achieve two sixes in the decisive encounter, he would establish a new all-time record of 51 sixes within a calendar year in the shortest format of international cricket.

The 26-year-old’s consistent performance has been a key factor in New Zealand’s T20i campaigns this year. His ability to accelerate scoring in the middle and death overs has frequently provided impetus for the Black Caps. Phillips's form has been notable, contributing vital runs and maintaining a high strike rate across various series. The potential record-breaking innings adds an additional layer of anticipation to the series' conclusion.

The final match of the T20 International series against India holds significance for both teams, with the series balance potentially at stake. For Phillips, it represents a unique opportunity to etch his name into the sport's record books, underscoring his evolution as a premier power-hitter in T20 cricket. The focus will be on his batting performance as he aims to reach this personal milestone while contributing to his team's objectives.

Cricket enthusiasts and analysts alike will be closely observing Phillips's innings. Breaking such a record would not only be a testament to his individual prowess but also highlight the increasing impact of aggressive batting in modern T20 cricket. The final T20i is set to commence at 7:30 PM local time, with attention firmly on whether Glenn Phillips can deliver the two sixes required to claim the world record.
