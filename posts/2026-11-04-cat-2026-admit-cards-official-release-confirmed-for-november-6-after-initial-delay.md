The Common Admission Test (CAT) 2026 admit cards, which aspirants had anticipated for release today, will now be issued on November 6. This official update was confirmed by the CAT conducting body, providing clarity for thousands of candidates preparing for the national-level management entrance examination. The delay necessitates a slight adjustment in candidate planning for accessing these crucial documents.

The admit card serves as a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall, containing critical details essential for a candidate's participation. These details include the aspirant's name, roll number, photograph, signature, the specific examination date, assigned time slot, and the designated test center venue. Its timely availability is paramount for candidates to finalize travel arrangements and other logistical requirements well in advance of the examination day.

Once officially released on November 6, candidates will be able to download their admit cards directly from the official CAT website, iimcat.ac.in. The standard download procedure typically requires aspirants to log in to the portal using their registered user ID and password. It is advisable for all candidates to promptly download and print multiple copies of their hall ticket. This ensures they have sufficient copies for personal record-keeping and for presentation at the examination center.

Key details regarding the CAT 2026 admit card release are as follows:
*   **New Admit Card Release Date:** November 6
*   **Official Website for Download:** iimcat.ac.in
*   **Information Required for Download:** Registered User ID and Password
*   **Mandatory Details on Admit Card:** Candidate's name, roll number, photograph, signature, examination date, time slot, and test center.
*   **Action for Candidates:** Verify all printed details immediately upon download. Any discrepancies should be reported to the CAT helpdesk without delay for rectification.

The Common Admission Test (CAT) is a prominent computer-based standardized test conducted annually by one of the Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs). It serves as a primary gateway for candidates seeking admission to various postgraduate management programs, including MBA and PGDM courses, offered by the IIMs and numerous other participating business schools across India. The CAT 2026 examination, for which these admit cards are being issued, is designated for admission to the academic year 2026-2027. The exam is typically held in late November or early December each year.

Aspirants are strongly advised to regularly monitor the official CAT website for any further announcements or updates related to the examination process. With the admit card release date now officially confirmed, candidates can continue to focus their efforts on final preparation strategies for the upcoming examination.
