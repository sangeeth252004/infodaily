The Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan has officially released the admit cards for the Kerala Teacher Eligibility Test (KTET) 2026. Candidates who have applied for the upcoming examinations, scheduled to take place on November 14 and 15, can now download their hall tickets directly from the official website, keralapareekshabhavan.in. This release is a crucial step for aspiring teachers in Kerala, enabling them to prepare for the mandatory certification test.

The KTET is a state-level examination conducted by the Pareeksha Bhavan on behalf of the Government of Kerala. It serves as an essential qualification for individuals aspiring to be appointed as teachers in Lower Primary, Upper Primary, High School, and Specialist Teacher categories in government, aided, and un-aided schools across the state. The release of admit cards signifies the final phase before the examinations commence, providing candidates with vital details regarding their test centers, reporting times, and examination guidelines.

Candidates are required to use their application number and date of birth to access and download their admit cards from the designated portal on the official website. The hall ticket is a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall; candidates without a valid admit card will not be permitted to take the test. It is strongly advised that all candidates download their admit cards well in advance to avoid last-minute technical issues and to verify all details printed on them.

Key details regarding the KTET 2026 examinations and admit card download process include:

*   **Exam Dates:** November 14 and 15, 2026
*   **Issuing Authority:** Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan
*   **Official Website for Download:** keralapareekshabhavan.in
*   **Required Credentials:** Application Number and Date of Birth
*   **Examination Categories:** KTET is conducted for four categories: Category I (Lower Primary Classes), Category II (Upper Primary Classes), Category III (High School Classes), and Category IV (Language Teachers up to UP Classes/Specialist Teachers).

Upon downloading, candidates should meticulously check all information printed on their admit cards, including their name, photograph, signature, date of birth, category of examination, exam center details, and examination timing. Any discrepancy noted should be immediately reported to the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan authorities for rectification before the examination dates. Alongside the admit card, candidates are typically required to carry a valid government-issued photo identification proof to the examination center.

The release of these admit cards marks an important period for thousands of teacher hopefuls. Candidates are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the exam guidelines and strictly adhere to the instructions provided to ensure a smooth examination experience. Further updates or advisories regarding the KTET 2026 examinations will be communicated through the official Pareeksha Bhavan website.
