Ten personnel from the State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) are feared missing in Telangana after two boats they were operating reportedly capsized during a rescue mission. The incident occurred recently as teams were deployed to assist citizens affected by severe weather and widespread flooding across the region. Search operations have been immediately launched by authorities.

The SDRF teams were engaged in evacuating residents from low-lying areas when the incident took place. While specific details regarding the exact cause of the capsize remain under investigation, preliminary reports suggest the boats encountered turbulent waters and strong currents, exacerbated by continuous heavy rainfall. The loss of contact with the ten personnel has prompted a comprehensive search and rescue effort involving multiple agencies.

Authorities have confirmed the deployment of additional SDR SDRF units, alongside teams from the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), local police, and district administration, to locate the missing individuals. Specialized divers and equipment have been mobilized, focusing on the riverine stretches where the boats were last seen. The prevailing adverse weather conditions, including incessant rain and elevated water levels, are presenting significant challenges to these ongoing operations.

Key details surrounding the incident include:
*   **Personnel Involved:** Ten members of the State Disaster Response Force (SDRF).
*   **Incident Type:** Two boats capsized during active rescue duties.
*   **Location:** Telangana state, specifically in areas experiencing heavy flooding.
*   **Context:** Operation to evacuate civilians stranded by rising floodwaters.
*   **Current Status:** Extensive search and rescue operations are underway.

The incident underscores the inherent risks faced by disaster response personnel operating in challenging environments. Officials have appealed to local communities residing near the affected areas to assist search efforts by reporting any relevant information. Family members of the missing personnel have been notified, and government support measures are being prepared.

As the search continues, authorities are also reviewing safety protocols for rescue operations in flood-affected zones. The state government has assured full support for the rescue teams and has reiterated its commitment to locate the missing personnel. Further updates are expected as the search progresses and more information becomes available from the incident site.
