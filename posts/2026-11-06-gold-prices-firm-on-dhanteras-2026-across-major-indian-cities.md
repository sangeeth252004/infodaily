As India celebrated Dhanteras on Thursday, November 6, 2026, gold prices displayed firm trends across major cities, with consumer demand anticipated to remain robust. The auspicious occasion, marking the beginning of the five-day Diwali festival, traditionally sees a surge in purchases of gold and silver as investments and symbols of prosperity. Market analysts noted stable rates for both 22-carat and 24-carat gold, prompting interest from buyers planning their festive acquisitions.

Dhanteras holds significant cultural and economic importance in India, with millions of households participating in the age-old tradition of buying precious metals. This annual surge in demand makes the period crucial for the Indian bullion market and jewellery retailers. The day's gold prices are closely monitored by consumers across urban and rural areas, influencing investment decisions and festive expenditures. The stability in rates on Dhanteras 2026 was observed as a positive indicator for market sentiment, despite prevailing global economic conditions.

Several factors typically influence domestic gold prices, including international spot gold rates, the value of the Indian Rupee against the US Dollar, and local demand-supply dynamics. On November 6, 2026, global gold benchmarks showed moderate stability, which translated into largely consistent domestic prices. Jewellers reported steady footfall throughout the day, indicating sustained consumer confidence in gold as a safe-haven asset and a culturally significant purchase. The market observed minor variations in rates driven by local taxes and premiums across different metropolitan areas.
