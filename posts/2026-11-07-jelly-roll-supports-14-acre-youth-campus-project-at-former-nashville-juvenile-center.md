Nashville, TN – Musician Jason DeFord, widely known as Jelly Roll, has announced his support for a significant new initiative to develop a 14-acre youth campus in Nashville. The proposed facility is planned for the site of the former juvenile detention center where DeFord spent part of his teenage years. This project aims to provide comprehensive resources and support for at-risk youth in the community.

The multi-acre campus represents a substantial investment in local youth services, focusing on prevention, rehabilitation, and skill development. While specific operational details for the campus are under development, the overall vision is to create an environment offering educational programs, mentorship opportunities, and recreational facilities designed to steer young individuals toward positive life trajectories. DeFord's involvement underscores a personal commitment to addressing the challenges faced by vulnerable youth, leveraging his own experiences to advocate for improved community infrastructure.

The decision to site the campus at the former juvenile center location is notable. This strategic placement seeks to transform a site historically associated with confinement into a center for growth and positive change. Developers and community leaders involved in the project emphasize that the campus will serve as a beacon of hope, providing alternatives and support systems that may not be readily available to many young people. The scale of the 14-acre plot allows for the integration of various facilities, potentially including academic spaces, vocational training workshops, counseling services, and outdoor recreational areas.

The initiative aligns with broader efforts in Nashville to expand community-based programs that address root causes of youth delinquency and promote long-term well-being. By focusing on holistic development, the campus intends to equip young individuals with the tools necessary to succeed academically, professionally, and personally. This proactive approach aims to reduce recidivism rates and foster a supportive environment where youth can access critical intervention and development programs.

*   **Location:** Site of a former juvenile detention center in Nashville, Tennessee.
*   **Scale:** 14-acre development.
*   **Objective:** To provide resources, support, and positive development opportunities for at-risk youth.
*   **Backed by:** Musician Jason DeFord (Jelly Roll).

Moving forward, project organizers are expected to release further details regarding fundraising milestones, architectural plans, and a projected timeline for the campus's construction and eventual opening. The initiative represents a significant step towards reinforcing community support structures for Nashville's youth, with an emphasis on creating a restorative and empowering future.
