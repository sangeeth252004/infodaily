Prime Minister Narendra Modi, speaking at a public meeting in Gadchiroli, Maharashtra, on May 11, 2024, asserted that while the influence of armed Naxalite groups has significantly diminished, a new challenge from "dimagi Naxals," or intellectual Naxals, persists in instigating individuals. The Prime Minister's remarks were delivered in a region historically impacted by left-wing extremism, highlighting the government's approach to internal security challenges.

The Prime Minister differentiated between traditional armed Naxal groups, often referred to as "Reds," and the "dimagi Naxals," a term he has previously used to describe individuals or groups allegedly supporting Naxal ideology through intellectual or urban channels. He claimed that despite the reduced presence of physical Naxalism, these "intellectual Naxals" continue to operate, influencing and misleading segments of the population.

This statement comes amidst ongoing efforts by the Indian government to counter Naxalism through both security operations and development initiatives. Mr. Modi reiterated his administration's commitment to eradicating the remaining pockets of Naxal influence while simultaneously focusing on socio-economic development in affected areas. He outlined the government's achievements in curtailing Naxal-related violence, citing a significant reduction in incidents over the past decade.

Key points from the Prime Minister's address included:

*   **Reduction in Naxal Incidents:** Prime Minister Modi stated that Naxal-related incidents, which reportedly stood at approximately 9,000 during the preceding ten years, have decreased to around 2,200 in the last ten years under his administration.
*   **Focus on Development:** He emphasized that infrastructure development, including roads and mobile towers, has been critical in weakening Naxal networks by improving connectivity and governance in remote areas. The government has prioritized bringing essential services and opportunities to tribal communities in regions previously dominated by extremists.
*   **Distinction between Naxalite Forms:** The Prime Minister's remarks underlined a perceived shift in the nature of the Naxal threat, from overt armed rebellion to more subtle forms of ideological instigation attributed to "urban" or "intellectual" Naxals.

The address in Gadchiroli forms part of a broader political discourse during a general election period, where national security and internal challenges frequently feature in public speeches. The government maintains that its multi-pronged strategy, combining robust security measures with comprehensive development programs, has been effective in addressing the Naxal problem across the country.

Looking ahead, the government is expected to continue its vigilance against all forms of perceived Naxal activity. Efforts are anticipated to intensify both security operations in affected areas and the implementation of development projects aimed at integrating marginalized communities into the national mainstream, thereby addressing root causes often exploited by extremist ideologies.
