Shah Mahmood Qureshi, former Foreign Minister of Pakistan and Vice Chairman of the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) party, was hospitalized on Monday after reportedly collapsing within Adiala Jail in Rawalpindi. Mr. Qureshi, who is currently incarcerated, was transferred to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) in Islamabad for medical observation and treatment.

Reports from hospital sources indicated that Mr. Qureshi presented with symptoms including low blood pressure and elevated blood sugar levels. Medical teams at PIMS are actively monitoring his condition following the incident. No official statement regarding his specific diagnosis or long-term prognosis had been released by hospital authorities or government officials as of Monday evening.

Mr. Qureshi's incarceration stems from the "cipher case," an investigation initiated under the Official Secrets Act. This case revolves around allegations concerning a confidential diplomatic cable, or cipher, which allegedly went missing or was misused. He is a co-accused alongside former Prime Minister Imran Khan in this high-profile legal matter. The judicial proceedings for this case are ongoing.

His arrest in the cipher case occurred on August 19, 2023, following a period of intermittent detentions related to various charges since May 2023. These earlier arrests were often linked to countrywide protests that erupted after Imran Khan's initial detention.

*   **Detention Location:** Adiala Jail, Rawalpindi.
*   **Hospitalization Venue:** Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), Islamabad.
*   **Reported Symptoms:** Low blood pressure and elevated blood sugar levels.
*   **Primary Case:** Cipher case, under the Official Secrets Act.
*   **Co-Accused:** Former Prime Minister Imran Khan.

Concerns regarding Mr. Qureshi's health have been previously voiced by his family and members of the PTI, citing his age and various pre-existing medical conditions. His hospitalization introduces a new dimension to his ongoing legal challenges and occurs amidst a politically charged environment in Pakistan, particularly for the PTI, which faces multiple legal battles involving its key leadership ahead of the upcoming general elections.

Mr. Qureshi remains under medical care at PIMS. The timing and specifics of his return to judicial custody will depend on his medical assessment and recovery. Legal proceedings related to the cipher case are expected to continue following his stabilization and discharge from the hospital.
