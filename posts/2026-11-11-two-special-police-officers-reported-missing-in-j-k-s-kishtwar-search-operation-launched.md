Authorities in Jammu and Kashmir have launched a comprehensive search operation in Kishtwar district following reports that two Special Police Officers (SPOs) are missing. The disappearance of the personnel was confirmed recently, prompting an immediate and extensive response from local law enforcement and security agencies across the region.

The two SPOs, whose identities have not been officially released, were reported missing from an unspecified location within the geographically challenging Kishtwar district. Special Police Officers are locally recruited personnel who assist the regular police force, often playing a crucial role in maintaining law and order, intelligence gathering, and anti-insurgency operations in sensitive areas like Jammu and Kashmir. Their disappearance constitutes a significant security concern for the region.

The search operation encompasses various parts of Kishtwar, a district known for its rugged mountainous terrain and dense forests, which can complicate search and rescue efforts. Multiple teams comprising personnel from the Jammu and Kashmir Police, along with contingents from the Indian Army and paramilitary forces, have been deployed. These teams are systematically scouring potential routes, checking local villages, and examining forested areas where the officers might have last been seen or traversed.

*   **Agencies Involved:** Jammu and Kashmir Police, Indian Army, Paramilitary Forces.
*   **Search Scope:** Rugged mountainous terrain, dense forests, local habitations.
*   **Objective:** Locate the missing SPOs and ascertain the circumstances of their disappearance.

Kishtwar, situated in the Chenab Valley, has historically been a challenging region from a security perspective, though incidents of militancy have significantly decreased over the past few years due to sustained efforts by security forces. The district's strategic location and difficult topography mean that any untoward incident involving security personnel is treated with the utmost seriousness. The ongoing search operation underscores the commitment of the authorities to account for their personnel and ensure their safety.

Officials have appealed to the public for any information that could assist in locating the missing SPOs. Checkpoints have been established at strategic locations, and security patrols have been intensified across the district. While the exact circumstances leading to their disappearance remain under investigation, the immediate focus of all deployed units is on tracing and safely recovering the two officers. The operation is expected to continue round-the-clock until the missing personnel are found. Authorities have not yet released further details regarding the incident, emphasizing that the primary objective is the safe return of the officers.
