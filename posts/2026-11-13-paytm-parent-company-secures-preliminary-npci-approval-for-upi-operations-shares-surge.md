One97 Communications Limited, the parent company of the digital payments platform Paytm, announced on March 15, 2024, that it has received preliminary approval from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) to operate as a Third-Party Application Provider (TPAP) for the Unified Payments Interface (UPI). This development allows the Paytm application to continue offering UPI services by migrating users to new Payment System Provider (PSP) banks, following previous regulatory actions against its associate, Paytm Payments Bank Limited (PPBL). The news led to a significant surge in One97 Communications shares, which rose to a 52-week high on stock exchanges.

The preliminary approval from NPCI is a critical step for One97 Communications (OCL) to ensure the uninterrupted availability of UPI services for its large user base. The directive comes after the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) imposed comprehensive restrictions on Paytm Payments Bank Limited (PPBL) on January 31, 2024, prohibiting it from accepting new deposits, credit transactions, or wallet top-ups after March 15, 2024. The TPAP approval is designed to mitigate the operational impact of these restrictions on Paytm's UPI functionality.

Under the TPAP model, OCL will integrate with multiple PSP banks to facilitate UPI transactions. This means that while PPBL will cease to operate as a bank offering UPI services, the Paytm app itself can continue to function as an interface for UPI payments, routing transactions through partner banks.

Key details surrounding the development include:
*   **Approval Authority:** The preliminary TPAP approval was granted by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), the entity overseeing UPI.
*   **Purpose:** To enable One97 Communications Limited (OCL) to continue providing UPI services to its customers by migrating them from Paytm Payments Bank Limited (PPBL) to other PSP banks.
*   **Partner Banks:** NPCI has advised OCL to complete the migration of all existing UPI handles and mandates to four specific PSP banks: Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, State Bank of India (SBI), and Yes Bank. Yes Bank will also serve as the merchant acquiring bank for existing and new Paytm QR codes.
*   **Share Market Impact:** Following the announcement, shares of One97 Communications Limited surged by 5% to reach a 52-week high of Rs 427.95 on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), reflecting investor confidence in the continuity of Paytm's core payment services.
*   **Regulatory Context:** The move directly addresses the operational challenges arising from the RBI's directive, which effectively limited PPBL's banking and wallet operations.

One97 Communications has stated its commitment to completing the user migration process expeditiously in collaboration with NPCI and its new PSP bank partners. This transition aims to provide a seamless experience for Paytm's users and merchants, ensuring that their UPI payment infrastructure remains functional. The company's efforts are focused on integrating with the identified banks to maintain operational stability for its digital payment offerings going forward.
