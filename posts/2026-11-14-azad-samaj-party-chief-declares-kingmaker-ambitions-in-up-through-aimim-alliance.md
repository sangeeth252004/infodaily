**Azad Samaj Party Chief Declares "Kingmaker" Ambitions in UP Through AIMIM Alliance**

NEW DELHI – Chandrashekhar Azad, chief of the Aazad Samaj Party (Kashi Ram), has publicly stated his party's ambition to become "kingmakers" in Uttar Pradesh's electoral landscape through a proposed alliance with Asaduddin Owaisi's All India Majlis-e-Ittehadul Muslimeen (AIMIM). The declaration, made by Azad, positions the emerging political front as a significant contender aiming to challenge established parties in the upcoming elections, including the 2024 Lok Sabha polls and the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections.

Azad articulated a vision for a "Bahujan Raj," or rule of the majority population, asserting that the combined strength of his party and AIMIM would mobilize Dalit and Muslim voters who he claims have been underserved by traditional political entities. He criticized established political blocs, including the opposition INDIA alliance, stating they held little relevance for his movement. Azad specifically accused parties like the Samajwadi Party (SP) and Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) of utilizing Dalit and Muslim votes without providing adequate leadership or representation to these communities.

The proposed alliance seeks to leverage the electoral bases of both parties, particularly among historically marginalized communities. Azad, who also leads the Bhim Army, expressed confidence in the "unbreakable" nature of his party's ties with Owaisi, signaling a long-term strategic partnership. This projection of influence comes despite previous electoral challenges for both political figures in Uttar Pradesh.

*   In the 2022 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections, Chandrashekhar Azad contested from Gorakhpur Urban and was defeated by current Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath.
*   The Aazad Samaj Party (Kashi Ram) secured one seat in the 2022 UP Assembly elections, with one candidate winning from the Dhampur constituency.
*   Asaduddin Owaisi's AIMIM did not secure any seats in the 2022 Uttar Pradesh polls, achieving approximately 0.49% of the total vote share across the state.

Looking ahead, Azad detailed an ambitious electoral strategy. He announced plans for his party to contest all 80 Lok Sabha seats in Uttar Pradesh in the 2024 general elections. Furthermore, he stated that the Aazad Samaj Party would field candidates across all 403 Vidhan Sabha seats in the state during the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections. These declarations underscore the alliance's intent to establish a comprehensive electoral presence rather than focusing on a few specific strongholds.

The implications of this potential alliance between the Aazad Samaj Party and AIMIM for Uttar Pradesh's complex political dynamics remain to be observed. The formation and practical implementation of their joint electoral strategy, as well as their ability to consolidate support from their target demographics, will be crucial factors determining their impact in the forthcoming 2024 Lok Sabha and 2027 UP Assembly elections.
