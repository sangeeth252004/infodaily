London, UK – The sons of former Pakistani Prime Minister Imran Khan, Suleiman Khan and Kasim Khan, have recently accused Pakistani authorities of subjecting their father to torture and harsh conditions during his imprisonment, expressing fears for his life. In statements made from London, the brothers alleged that Mr. Khan is being held in solitary confinement and denied adequate medical care, leading to a significant decline in his health.

These accusations emerge amidst Mr. Khan's continued detention in Pakistan, following a series of legal actions that have seen him convicted in multiple cases since his arrest in August 2023. His sons claim that the former premier is facing inhumane treatment, stating, "It's inevitable he will die in jail." They detailed concerns about his isolation and lack of contact, asserting that these conditions amount to psychological and physical torment. The allegations highlight a contentious period in Pakistani politics, marked by the detention of a prominent political figure.

Mr. Khan, the founder of the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) party, has been incarcerated primarily at Adiala Jail in Rawalpindi. His detention stems from convictions in cases including the Toshakhana reference, which involved the alleged illegal sale of state gifts, and the cipher case, concerning the alleged disclosure of classified state secrets. While Mr. Khan has consistently denied all charges, maintaining they are politically motivated, his legal team has pursued appeals against these convictions.

Key points of the allegations include:
*   **Solitary Confinement:** Claims that Mr. Khan is being held in isolation, severely limiting his contact with the outside world, including family and legal representatives.
*   **Medical Neglect:** Allegations of inadequate access to necessary medical examinations and treatment, despite reported health issues.
*   **Deteriorating Health:** Assertions that Mr. Khan's physical and mental health have significantly worsened due to the prison environment.
*   **Limited Access:** Concerns over restrictions placed on visits from family members and legal counsel, hindering his defense preparations and well-being checks.

Pakistani authorities have consistently denied any claims of torture or inhumane treatment of Imran Khan. Officials maintain that Mr. Khan is being treated in accordance with prison rules and national laws, receiving all due facilities and medical attention as required. They have dismissed the allegations as baseless and politically motivated attempts to discredit the legal process. The government has affirmed that all legal proceedings against Mr. Khan are transparent and adhere to due process.

The accusations by Mr. Khan's sons have prompted calls from human rights organizations and international observers for thorough and impartial investigations into the conditions of his detention. As legal challenges continue and the political landscape remains tense, the focus remains on the judicial process and the welfare of the former Prime Minister. The situation is expected to remain a significant point of discussion both domestically and internationally, with ongoing appeals and political developments shaping future outcomes.
