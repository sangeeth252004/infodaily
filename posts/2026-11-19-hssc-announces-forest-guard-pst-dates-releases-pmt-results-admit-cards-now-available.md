The Haryana Staff Selection Commission (HSSC) has announced the dates for the Physical Standard Test (PST) for its Forest Guard recruitment drive, reportedly scheduled for 2026. Concurrently, the commission has released the results for the Physical Measurement Test (PMT) that was conducted previously. Candidates who qualified in the PMT can now download their admit cards for the upcoming PST from the official HSSC website, hssc.gov.in.

This development marks a significant progression in the Forest Guard recruitment process, impacting numerous aspirants across Haryana. The PMT results pertain to tests conducted between October 11, 2023, and October 22, 2023, for various posts, with specific outcomes now released for the Forest Guard positions. Successful candidates from this initial measurement phase are now eligible to proceed to the PST, a crucial physical endurance component of the selection procedure.

The PST, which evaluates candidates' physical fitness and standards, is a mandatory stage for final selection into the Forest Guard cadre. The announcement of its dates for 2026 provides a clear timeline for candidates to prepare. The availability of admit cards signifies that all logistical arrangements are being finalized for this future testing phase.

Key details for candidates include:

*   **PMT Results:** Released for candidates who participated in the Physical Measurement Test held from October 11 to October 22, 2023. Results are accessible on the HSSC portal.
*   **PST Schedule:** The Physical Standard Test for Forest Guard posts is scheduled to take place in the year 2026. Specific dates and venues will be detailed on the admit cards.
*   **Admit Card Download:** Admit cards for the 2026 PST are currently available for download on the official HSSC website, hssc.gov.in. Candidates are required to use their registration number and date of birth to access their individual admit cards.
*   **Essential Documents:** Aspirants must ensure they download and print their admit cards. It is mandatory to carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport) to the PST venue.

Candidates are strongly advised to meticulously review all instructions provided on their admit cards and the HSSC website. Failure to present the required documents or adhere to the specified guidelines may result in disqualification from the selection process. This phase is critical as it assesses the physical capabilities necessary for the demanding role of a Forest Guard. Further updates regarding the recruitment drive, including subsequent stages such as document verification and the final merit list, will be published exclusively on the official HSSC website. Candidates are encouraged to regularly monitor the portal for the latest information.
