NEW DELHI – The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has introduced a significant policy reform, allowing its subscribers to withdraw up to 90% of their accumulated provident fund (PF) corpus for the purpose of purchasing a house or land, or for house construction. This announcement, detailed in recent notifications, marks a substantial increase from previous withdrawal limits and is aimed at bolstering home ownership among salaried individuals across the nation.

The new regulation provides a considerable boost to members seeking to finance residential property. This change is particularly relevant for those facing challenges in accumulating substantial down payments or securing external housing loans. By providing access to a larger portion of their PF savings, the EPFO aims to make housing more accessible and reduce the financial burden on its contributors. The reform is expected to have a broad impact, potentially stimulating the real estate sector while offering greater financial flexibility to millions of employees.

Under the revised guidelines, eligible EPFO members can now access a significant portion of their retirement savings for specific housing needs. This non-refundable advance is subject to certain conditions to ensure its appropriate utilization.

Key details and conditions of the new withdrawal facility include:
*   **Purpose:** The withdrawal is specifically sanctioned for the purchase of a plot, purchase of a ready-built house, or construction of a house.
*   **Eligibility Criteria:** Subscribers must generally have completed a minimum of five years of service and active contribution to their PF account to avail themselves of this facility.
*   **Withdrawal Limit:** The permissible withdrawal amount is capped at the lesser of 90% of the accumulated EPF corpus (employee's share, employer's share, and interest) or the actual cost of the property/plot.
*   **Joint Application:** An EPF member can apply for withdrawal jointly with their spouse if the spouse is also an EPFO subscriber, combining their eligible balances.
*   **Documentation:** Applicants are required to provide necessary documentation, including property registration papers, allotment letters, or construction estimates, to substantiate the housing purpose.
*   **One-Time Facility:** This specific housing withdrawal under the 90% limit is generally a one-time provision for members.

The reform is a response to long-standing demands from various employee groups and industry stakeholders for increased flexibility in accessing PF funds for critical needs like housing. The EPFO's move reflects a broader strategic objective to balance long-term retirement security with the immediate socio-economic aspirations of its members.

Going forward, the EPFO is expected to issue further detailed clarifications and frequently asked questions (FAQs) to ensure smooth implementation and understanding among its vast subscriber base and employers. Members are encouraged to consult official EPFO channels and notifications for the most accurate and up-to-date information regarding the application process and specific requirements for this enhanced withdrawal facility. The long-term implications for individual retirement planning and the housing market will be closely observed as the reform takes full effect.
