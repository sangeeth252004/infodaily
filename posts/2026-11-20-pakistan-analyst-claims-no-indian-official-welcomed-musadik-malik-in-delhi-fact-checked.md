Claims made by a Pakistani analyst regarding the reception of Pakistan's Minister of State for Petroleum, Musadik Malik, during his recent visit to Delhi for a Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) event have been fact-checked and found to be incorrect. The analyst, Arshad Sharif, had asserted that no Indian official was present to welcome Malik upon his arrival in the Indian capital.

The assertion circulated online, prompting a review of the circumstances surrounding Minister Malik's visit. Musadik Malik was in Delhi to participate in the SCO meeting of heads of energy ministries, an official diplomatic engagement. Such visits typically involve established diplomatic protocols, particularly concerning the reception of ministerial-level delegates.

Contrary to the claim, visual evidence and protocol reports indicate that Indian officials were present to receive the Pakistani minister. Footage and photographic records from the event show Malik being greeted by senior protocol officers from India's Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) upon his arrival. These officials are tasked with managing the logistical and ceremonial aspects of foreign dignitaries' visits, ensuring adherence to diplomatic norms. The presence of these officers aligns with standard diplomatic practice for such multilateral gatherings hosted by India.

Key details surrounding the event and the fact-check include:
*   **Visitor:** Musadik Malik, Pakistan's Minister of State for Petroleum.
*   **Purpose of Visit:** Attending the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) meeting of heads of energy ministries.
*   **Location:** New Delhi, India.
*   **Analyst's Claim:** Arshad Sharif, a Pakistani analyst, claimed no Indian official welcomed Malik.
*   **Fact-check Finding:** The claim was disproven by visual evidence, which showed Indian Ministry of External Affairs protocol officers present to receive Malik.
*   **Standard Protocol:** Diplomatic protocols dictate that host nation officials, typically from the foreign ministry's protocol division, are responsible for welcoming visiting dignitaries.

The dissemination of unsubstantiated claims regarding high-level diplomatic interactions can contribute to misinformation, particularly in contexts involving sensitive bilateral relations. The documented presence of Indian protocol officials aligns with established international norms for receiving foreign dignitaries at multilateral events hosted on Indian soil. This incident underscores the importance of factual verification when reporting on diplomatic exchanges and international relations.
