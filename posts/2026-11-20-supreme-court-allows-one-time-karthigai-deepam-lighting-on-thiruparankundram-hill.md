New Delhi – The Supreme Court of India on Wednesday allowed the one-time lighting of a traditional Karthigai Deepam lamp on Thiruparankundram hill in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, scheduled for November 24, 2024. The interim order modifies a previous restriction imposed by the Madras High Court regarding religious observances on the culturally significant hill, which houses both Hindu and Muslim places of worship.

A bench comprising Justices Sanjiv Khanna and S.V.N. Bhatti issued the directive, permitting a single traditional lamp to be lit near the Kasi Viswanathar temple, situated close to the Sikandar Badushah Dargah atop the hill. The court emphasized that the lighting must be carried out under strict supervision by local police, fire services, and temple authorities, ensuring public safety and order. The decision comes in response to a petition filed by religious leaders, including Kanchi Sankaracharya Sri Vijayendra Saraswathi, challenging the earlier High Court ruling that restricted access and activities, including the Karthigai Deepam lighting.

Thiruparankundram hill holds spiritual importance for both Hindu and Muslim communities. For Hindus, it is home to the ancient Subramaniya Swamy Temple and the Kasi Viswanathar temple. Muslims revere the hill for the dargah of Sikandar Badushah, a revered Sufi saint. This dual significance has led to historical discussions and legal proceedings regarding access and the conduct of religious ceremonies, particularly those involving fire. The Madras High Court had previously issued orders aimed at preventing potential damage to the ecologically fragile hill and maintaining communal harmony, which included restrictions on large-scale lamp lighting.

The Supreme Court's ruling aims to strike a temporary balance between religious freedom and environmental concerns while recognizing the long-standing traditions. The Karthigai Deepam festival, celebrated during the Tamil month of Karthigai, involves lighting lamps to symbolize the dispelling of darkness. The petitioners argued for the continuation of this ritual, citing historical practice and religious sentiments.

Key details of the Supreme Court's interim order include:
*   **Date of lighting:** November 24, 2024.
*   **Number of lamps:** A single traditional Karthigai Deepam lamp.
*   **Location:** Near the Kasi Viswanathar temple atop Thiruparankundram hill.
*   **Supervision:** Mandated presence and oversight by police, fire services, and temple management.
*   **Scope:** The permission is for a one-time event for the current year’s festival.

The court has scheduled further hearings to address the broader issues surrounding religious practices and access to Thiruparankundram hill. This interim decision indicates a judicial effort to accommodate religious traditions while concurrently considering safety and environmental preservation in areas of shared cultural heritage. The final resolution of the long-standing dispute is pending further judicial deliberation.
