Green Gas Limited (GGL) has announced an increase in the prices of Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) and Piped Natural Gas (PNG) across five cities in Uttar Pradesh. Effective May 14, 2024, CNG prices have risen by Rs 2 per kilogram (kg), and PNG prices by Re 1 per standard cubic meter (SCM) in Lucknow, Agra, Ayodhya, Unnao, and Sultanpur.

This adjustment directly impacts approximately 1.5 lakh CNG vehicle owners and over 1 lakh PNG domestic connections in the operational areas of GGL. The company, a joint venture of GAIL (India) Limited and Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOC), is a significant distributor of natural gas in these regions.

Following the revision, the new retail price for CNG in Lucknow and Agra now stands at Rs 95 per kg. Previously, the price was Rs 93 per kg. For domestic consumers, PNG is now priced at Rs 59 per SCM in these cities, up from the earlier rate of Rs 58 per SCM. Similar price increments have been implemented in Ayodhya, Unnao, and Sultanpur.

The price change is expected to influence operational costs for various sectors. Commercial vehicles, including auto-rickshaws, taxis, and public transport buses that run on CNG, will experience an increase in fuel expenses. Private vehicle owners utilizing CNG as fuel will also face higher costs for their daily commutes. For households, the revised PNG rates will lead to a marginal increase in monthly utility bills.

GGL indicated that the price revision is a response to the sustained increase in the input cost of natural gas. Factors such as changes in global gas benchmarks and fluctuations in foreign exchange rates can influence the procurement cost of natural gas, leading to such adjustments. The company manages the distribution infrastructure for natural gas in these five cities, providing fuel for transportation and household energy needs.

Key details of the price revision include:
*   **Cities Affected:** Lucknow, Agra, Ayodhya, Unnao, Sultanpur
*   **CNG Price Increase:** Rs 2 per kg
*   **PNG Price Increase:** Re 1 per SCM
*   **New CNG Price (Lucknow, Agra):** Rs 95 per kg
*   **New PNG Price (Lucknow, Agra):** Rs 59 per SCM
*   **Effective Date:** May 14, 2024

The increase reflects an ongoing evaluation of supply chain costs and market dynamics, aiming to maintain the viability of natural gas distribution while addressing rising input expenditures. Further price adjustments will depend on future shifts in global energy markets and operational costs.
