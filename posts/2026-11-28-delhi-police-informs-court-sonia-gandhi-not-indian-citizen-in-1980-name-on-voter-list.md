Delhi Police has informed a city court that former Congress president Sonia Gandhi was not an Indian citizen in 1980, a year in which her name appeared on the voter list for the Lok Sabha elections. This detail was presented in an Action Taken Report (ATR) filed with Additional Chief Metropolitan Magistrate (ACMM) Tanya Baswani in a Delhi court on Friday, January 19, 2024.

The police report addresses a complaint filed by Dr. Subramanian Swamy, who has sought to cancel Sonia Gandhi's Lok Sabha membership over alleged discrepancies regarding her citizenship and voting rights. The court had directed the police to file the ATR in September 2023, following Swamy's persistent legal challenge which has been pending since 2018.

Key findings presented in the Delhi Police's ATR include:
*   A preliminary enquiry was conducted based on the court's order.
*   Police found no evidence to suggest Sonia Gandhi made any attempt to become an Indian citizen before 1983.
*   She acquired Indian citizenship in April 1983 through registration under Section 5(1)(c) of the Citizenship Act.
*   Despite not being an Indian citizen, her name was present on the voter list for the 1980 Lok Sabha elections from the New Delhi constituency.

The police report further elaborated on the circumstances surrounding her voter registration in 1980. It stated that there was no clear provision at the time to cancel a voter's registration specifically on the grounds of not being a citizen. According to the report, her name "appears to have been included on the basis of her declaration, as was the practice at the time," and "no official had any reason to doubt the veracity of her claim." Consequently, the police found no criminal offense to have been committed by election officials under the Representation of the People Act, 1950, or the Indian Penal Code (IPC). The report noted that "there was no clarity regarding her status of citizenship at that point of time as per official records."

Dr. Swamy has maintained that Sonia Gandhi, being an Italian citizen in 1980, voting in India constituted a violation of law. He has sought a directive to the Election Commission of India to annul her parliamentary membership.

The court has acknowledged the Delhi Police's Action Taken Report. The matter is scheduled for further hearing on April 26, 2024, when the court is expected to review the submissions and determine the next course of action in the long-running legal proceedings.
